# BGBl I 1998 p. 2501 — NS-AufhG (25 August 1998)

Anchors the chapter-1 claim that the 1998 NS-AufhG provides the statutory mechanism by which the 2007 Generalbundesanwalt determination annulled van der Lubbe's 1933 conviction. The "statutory record durability across regime change" mechanism named in the chapter's "escape" beat rests on this statute.
