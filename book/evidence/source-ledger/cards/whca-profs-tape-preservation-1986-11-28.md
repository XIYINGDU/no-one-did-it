# WHCA PROFS-tape preservation decision (Nov. 28, 1986)

The card anchors the chapter-12 lines 91 and 170 citations of the Nov. 28, 1986 WHCA
preservation decision as the operative rule-3 escape in the Iran-Contra worked example.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 beat 5 ("record hardens") and beat 9 (the four-panel "escape" walk) both treat
the WHCA preservation order as the operative interception that produced the PROFS-tape
recovery on which both the Tower Commission and the Walsh Final Report relied. The chapter's
argument is that the recovery was the *result* of a preservation decision at a layer the
destroyers did not control; the preservation decision is the structurally important fact,
not the later forensic work.
