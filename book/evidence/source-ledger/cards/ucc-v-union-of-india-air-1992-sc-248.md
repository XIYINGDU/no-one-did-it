# UCC v. Union of India — AIR 1992 SC 248 (review judgment, Oct 3, 1991)

Anchors chapter 5 beat-7 alibi-weakens citation of the Indian Supreme Court 1991 review judgment
upholding the 1989 settlement quantum and reinstating criminal proceedings against UCC and Anderson.

## Holding context

The 1991 judgment of the Constitution Bench is the load-bearing alibi-weakening event on the
Indian side. It separated the civil-side closure (settlement quantum upheld) from the criminal-side
re-opening (criminal proceedings reinstated against UCC and Anderson), producing the procedural
posture under which the 2010 CJM Bhopal convictions of seven former UCIL officials were entered.
The judgment is one of three principal alibi-weakening events the chapter cites (paired with the
2010 CJM convictions and the 1999-2014 Sahu SDNY litigation).
