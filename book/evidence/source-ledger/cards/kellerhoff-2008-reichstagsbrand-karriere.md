# Kellerhoff 2008, Der Reichstagsbrand: Die Karriere eines Kriminalfalls (2013 2nd ed.)

Anchors the chapter-1 claim that Kellerhoff's 2008 synthesis, drawing on post-reunification Berlin court archive material, concluded for the alone-perpetrator thesis. Closing position in the chapter's historiographical sequence (Tobias / Mommsen / Hofer / Bahar-Kugel / Kellerhoff). The chapter notes that "the Hofer committee and the Bahar-Kugel volume remain the standing counter-positions" — Kellerhoff is one pole; the chapter does not resolve the question.
