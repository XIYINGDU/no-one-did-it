# Tobias 1962, Der Reichstagsbrand: Legende und Wirklichkeit

Anchors the chapter-1 claim that Tobias's 1962 monograph was the first major postwar challenge to the prevailing assumption of Nazi authorship of the fire, arguing for the lone-perpetrator reading. Part of the chapter's "the question is open" beat that names the Tobias / Mommsen / Hofer / Bahar-Kugel / Kellerhoff sequence.
