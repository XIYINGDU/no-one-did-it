# OLC Levin withdrawal memorandum (30 December 2004)

The card anchors the chapter-11 accusation-scene claim that the Bybee 1 August 2002 OLC opinion was
withdrawn by Acting OLC head Daniel Levin on 30 December 2004.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 11 uses the OLC opinion-shop as one of three design-absence accusation-scene exhibits.
The withdrawal date and signatory anchor the chapter's structural point: the OLC process produces
documents that can be withdrawn but contains no statutory attestation mechanism comparable to
SOX § 302 / § 906. The Bybee memo is anchored by the existing `bybee-yoo-olc-torture-memos-2002-opr-2009`
card; this card adds the withdrawal-memorandum primary source as a discrete (source, claim) pair.
