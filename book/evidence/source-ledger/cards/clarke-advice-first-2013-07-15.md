# The first Clarke Advice (Simon Clarke, Cartwright King, 15 July 2013)

The card anchors the chapter-7 claim that the Post Office's own outside counsel, in 2013, advised the institution in writing that prosecutions which had relied on Gareth Jenkins's expert evidence had serious problems given Jenkins's failure to disclose Horizon issues — and that the institution did not act on the advice to systematically review or stop affected prosecutions.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 7 line 63 carries the anchor `[CITE: Clarke advice, 2013, disclosed to Inquiry]` to substantiate the chapter's claim that knowledge of the prosecutorial problem reached the Post Office's counsel function in 2013, and the institution did not act on it. The advice document is the load-bearing piece of evidence for the "should have known" prong of the eight-question diagnostic walk.
