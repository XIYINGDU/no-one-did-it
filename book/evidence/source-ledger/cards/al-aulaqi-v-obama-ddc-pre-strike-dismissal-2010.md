# Al-Aulaqi v. Obama, 727 F. Supp. 2d 1 (D.D.C. 2010) — pre-strike suit dismissed without reaching merits

The card anchors the accountability-avoidance fact at the heart of the cross-valence al-Aulaqi case:
the only judicial test available before the strike was dismissed on threshold grounds, so the
government's factual case against a U.S. citizen was never adjudicated before he was killed. This is
the "no external review" leg of the system/object-alibi classification (the opinion-as-shield was the
only "review," and it was internal to the branch that benefited).
