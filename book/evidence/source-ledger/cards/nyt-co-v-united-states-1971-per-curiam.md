# NYT Co. v. United States (1971) — per curiam ruling against prior restraint

The card anchors the chapter-12 claim that the Supreme Court's June 30, 1971 per curiam ruling
in the Pentagon Papers case held that the government had not met the heavy burden required for
prior restraint, allowing publication of the McNamara study excerpts in *The New York Times* and
*The Washington Post* to resume.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 line 19 cites the case as the constitutional anchor for the chapter's beat-1
accusation scene (Pentagon Papers / publication-permitted ruling) and beat-9 escape panel
(preservation outside originating institution forces suppression attempt into adversarial
venue).
