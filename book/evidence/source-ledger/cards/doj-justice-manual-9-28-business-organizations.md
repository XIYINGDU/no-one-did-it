# DOJ Justice Manual § 9-28.000 — Principles of Federal Prosecution of Business Organizations

The card anchors the chapter-11 beat-2 ("official story") citation of the Justice Manual as the
authority for the institutional defence of the corporate-plea structure used in the Boeing DPA.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 11 takes the institutional defence of the Boeing DPA structure at full strength before
turning the diagnostic on it. The defence runs through Justice Manual § 9-28.000 and its
cooperation/individuals subsections. The card anchors the defence's operative policy text.
