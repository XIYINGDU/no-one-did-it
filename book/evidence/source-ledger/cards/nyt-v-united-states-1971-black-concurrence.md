# Justice Black concurrence — "bare the secrets of government"

The card anchors the chapter-12 line-19 quotation of Justice Black's concurrence at 403 U.S. 717
on the function of a free press: to expose government secrets and inform the public.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 beat 1 uses the Black concurrence as the most-cited single sentence on the
Pentagon Papers' First Amendment function — the press's constitutional mandate to expose
government secrets is the structural ancestor of the chapter's rule three (make destruction
visible).

the people" inside double quotes. The fragment is byte-exact within Black's full sentence

people."). Black is named in his judicial capacity; no defamation exposure. Hugo Black died
