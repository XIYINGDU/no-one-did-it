# DOJ "Capitol Breach Cases" Dashboard Removal (January 2025)

The card anchors chapter 8 line 17 — the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District
of Columbia's public "Capitol Breach Cases" page, a running ledger of the
January 6 prosecution, that was live as of its last Wayback capture on
2025-01-24 and removed between 2025-01-24 and 2025-01-28 (after the 2025-01-20
mass pardons), thereafter returning a page-not-found notice.

## Figure-lock status (RESOLVED 2026-05-29 — LOCKED)

The chapter sentence carries "a count of more than 1,500 people charged,
according to the page's last archived capture on January 24, 2025." The
documented facts (page existed, tracked the prosecution, capture date, removal)
are grade B. The figure "more than 1,500 charged" is now grounded in a **DOJ
primary statement** for the same window: the DOJ USAO-DC update **"48 Months
Since the Jan. 6 Attack on the U.S. Capitol"**
(justice.gov/usao-dc/48-months-jan-6-attack-us-capitol), dated **January 6,
2025** — the version of record current at the January 24, 2025 capture —
states "**approximately 1,583 defendants have been charged criminally in
federal court.**" Confirmed via two independent renderings of the DOJ update
text on 2026-05-29.

"More than 1,500" is a true, conservative floor under the DOJ's own 1,583
charged-count, and the *charged* framing matches CBS / Newsweek / CREW and
Proclamation 10887 coverage. Daily Dot's "more than 1,200 *cases*" counts a
different denominator (cases, not charged individuals) and does not contradict
it.

The chapter phrasing is correct under rule 02 as **documented-with-attribution**:
it attributes the count to the archived capture rather than asserting one
precise masthead numeral. The claim no longer depends on a single archive
render — it is corroborated by the DOJ's own monthly update.
