# Hipponax frr. 5-11 West — earliest extant description of the pharmakos

Anchors the chapter-1 claim that Hipponax of Ephesus (sixth century BCE) is the earliest surviving description of the Greek pharmakos ritual. The West numeration (frr. 5-11) is the canonical scholarly reference; the chapter pairs this card with `bremmer-1983-scapegoat-rituals-greece` (the modern scholarly synthesis of the rite).
