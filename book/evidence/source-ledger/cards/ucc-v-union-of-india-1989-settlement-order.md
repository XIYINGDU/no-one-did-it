# UCC v. Union of India — Indian Supreme Court 1989 settlement order

Anchors chapter 5 beat-5 record-hardens citation of the 14-15 February 1989 Indian Supreme Court
settlement order locking the USD 470 million civil-claims quantum.

## Holding context

The settlement order is one of the two load-bearing record-controller documents on the
Indian-side closure of the Bhopal civil case (paired with the 2d Cir. 1987 forum non conveniens
decision on the US side). The 1991 review judgment (AIR 1992 SC 248) upheld the quantum but
reinstated the criminal proceedings, separating the alibi-hardening civil-side closure from the
subsequent criminal-side weakening that would produce the 2010 CJM Bhopal convictions.
