# RGBl I 1933 p. 83 — Reichstagsbrandverordnung (28 February 1933)

Anchors the chapter-1 claim that the Reichstag Fire Decree was published in RGBl I 1933 p. 83 on 28 February 1933 and that it suspended seven specific Weimar constitutional articles. The decree is the load-bearing legal instrument the chapter's "altar moves" argument rests on.
