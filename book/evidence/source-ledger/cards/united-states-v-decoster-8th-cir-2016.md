# United States v. DeCoster, 828 F.3d 626 (8th Cir. 2016)

The card anchors the chapter-11 older-echo (beat 4) and beat-9 ("the escape") treatment of
DeCoster as the modern senior-officer test of Park-doctrine reach, including the divided-panel
constitutional objections that did not carry on appeal or cert.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 11 cites DeCoster at lines 98 and 172 in support of the claim that the Park doctrine has
been judicially extended to actual prison time against named senior corporate officers in the
half-century since the original decision, against the strongest contemporary Eighth-Amendment and
due-process objections.
