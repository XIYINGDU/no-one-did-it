# FMVSS 301 (49 C.F.R. § 571.301) — fuel system integrity standard

The card anchors the chapter-3 goat-resists (Schwartz steelman) reference to the federal
fuel-system-integrity standard whose 1976 finalisation and phased compliance through model year
1977 are the procedural facts behind Ford's 'we complied with the standards in effect' framing.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 3 cites FMVSS 301 at line 131 as the regulatory anchor for the steelman argument that
the Pinto satisfied the federal motor vehicle safety standards as enacted at each model year.
The chapter engages this argument by acknowledging that Ford's own internal crash testing
documented a vulnerability that the regulator had not yet codified — a fact pattern the
diagnostic surfaces as Q4 (knowledge) and Q5 (preventability) regardless of the regulatory
chronology.

## Diagnostic significance

For the chapter's slot-6 'goat resists' beat, FMVSS 301 is the primary-source anchor that lets
the chapter engage Schwartz's steelman precisely: Ford's compliance-with-standards-in-effect
defense is true on its face, and the chapter's argument depends on the diagnostic's
control-knowledge-preventability axes operating independently of regulatory chronology.
