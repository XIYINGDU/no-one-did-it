# Cullen, Maakestad & Cavender, Corporate Crime Under Attack (1987) — Pinto academic account

The card anchors the chapter-3 older-echo (Q4 knowledge) reference to the standard academic
monograph on the Indiana criminal prosecution of Ford for the Pinto.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 3 cites this monograph at line 99 alongside Dowie (1977) and Schwartz (1991) as the
three load-bearing academic and journalistic sources on the Ford Pinto matter. Cullen et al.
supply the procedural account of the criminal-path interception failure (Indiana acquittal).

## Diagnostic significance

For the chapter's older-echo treatment of asymmetric interception (civil succeeded, criminal
failed), Cullen et al. is the canonical academic reference for the criminal-path failure under
Indiana law. The chapter uses this to establish that interception is not single-channel and that
different procedural venues have different evidentiary requirements.
