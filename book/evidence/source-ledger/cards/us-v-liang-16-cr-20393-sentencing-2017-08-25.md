The card anchors the chapter-3 hidden-architecture (Q8 chain) and goat-resists references to

## Why this card exists

Chapter 3 cites the Liang sentencing at two anchor occurrences (lines 79 and 137). The
sentencing is the procedural anchor for the chapter's "deliberate-fraud counterargument" beat 6
(goat resists) — the named engineer received a substantial criminal sentence while the
upstream chain remained insulated under US procedure.

## Diagnostic significance

For the chapter's Q8 (what would responsibility look like) question, the Liang sentencing
illustrates the asymmetry that the diagnostic must surface: a named engineer served 40 months
while the executives who directed the engineering response did not face equivalent personal
adjudication in the US.
