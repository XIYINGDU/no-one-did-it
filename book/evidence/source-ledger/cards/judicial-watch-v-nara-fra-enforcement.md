# Judicial Watch v. NARA — FRA enforcement-architecture deficit (D.C. Cir.)

The card anchors the chapter-12 line-162 citation of the D.C. Circuit's FRA enforcement
jurisprudence (Armstrong v. Bush; Armstrong v. EOP; subsequent Judicial Watch v. NARA) for
the proposition that operative enforcement under 44 U.S.C. § 3106 depends on discretionary
Archivist referral to the Attorney General — the chapter's load-bearing structural argument
at beat 8 (anti-laundering rule).

## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 advocates an enforcement architecture rather than new statutory text; the
chapter's argument requires the empirical documentation that the existing FRA enforcement
scheme leaves the operative layer dependent on intra-executive-branch referral. The
Armstrong / Judicial Watch line is the case-law anchor for that documentation.
