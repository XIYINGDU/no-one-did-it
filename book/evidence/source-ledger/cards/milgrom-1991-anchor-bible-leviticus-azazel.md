# Milgrom 1991 Anchor Bible Leviticus 1-16, on Azazel

Anchors the chapter-1 claim that modern Hebrew Bible scholarship treats Tyndale's compound etymology (ez ozel = scape-goat) as philologically strained, and that Milgrom's demon-name reading is widely held but not universal. Load-bearing for the chapter's "the word is, by current scholarly judgement, a misreading" sentence (line 39).
