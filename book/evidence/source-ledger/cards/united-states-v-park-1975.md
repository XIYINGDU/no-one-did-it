# United States v. Park, 421 U.S. 658 (1975) — responsible-corporate-officer doctrine

The card anchors the chapter-11 hidden-architecture and older-echo treatment of the Park doctrine
as the codified named-attribution design move from which SOX §§ 302/906 and the UK SMCR descend.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 11's design-rule argument rests on Park as the foundational U.S. statutory-doctrine
worked example of responsibility-following-control at the corporate-officer layer. The card
collects the chapter's three pin-cites (671-674 for the holding; 660-665 for the trial record;
the case caption itself) into a single (source, claim) pair.

## Doctrinal-reading caveat

The chapter relies on a structural reading of Park as opportunity-to-prevent conditioned on the
officer's position and notice; the casebook line characterises Park as FDCA strict liability with
an objective-impossibility defense. The chapter discloses this; specialist confirmation from Alan
is pending.
