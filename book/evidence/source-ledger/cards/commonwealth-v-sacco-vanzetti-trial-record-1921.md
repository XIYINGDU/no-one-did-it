  text: On 14 July 1921, a Norfolk County, Massachusetts jury convicted Nicola Sacco and Bartolomeo Vanzetti of first-degree murder for the 15 April 1920 robbery-killings of paymaster Frederick Parmenter and guard Alessandro Berardelli at the Slater & Morrill shoe factory in South Braintree, Massachusetts. The trial ran from 31 May to 14 July 1921 at Dedham before Judge Webster Thayer. Seven motions for a new trial, all denied by Thayer, ran from 1921 through 1924; a final motion citing the Madeiros confession was denied in October 1926.
