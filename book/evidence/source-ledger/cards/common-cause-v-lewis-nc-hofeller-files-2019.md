# Common Cause v. Lewis — Hofeller-files admission and ruling (2019)

The card anchors the chapter-12 lines 132 and 174 citations of the three documented dates
in the Hofeller-files survival case: July 12, 2019 (35-file admission), July 15–26, 2019
(bench trial), and September 3, 2019 (three-judge-panel ruling for plaintiffs).

## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 beat 7 (asymmetry table) lists the Hofeller files in the surviving column; beat
9 walks them as the fourth and honest-closer panel demonstrating that the most consequential
disclosures sometimes turn on events institutional design cannot reach (here, a posthumous
custody change by the mapmaker's estranged daughter). The chapter distinguishes the three
specific dates because they were collapsed in an earlier draft; this card fixes the
distinction.
