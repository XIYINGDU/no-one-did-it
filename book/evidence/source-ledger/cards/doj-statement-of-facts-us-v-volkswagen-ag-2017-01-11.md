# DOJ Statement of Facts, US v. Volkswagen AG (E.D. Mich.) — 11 January 2017

The card anchors the chapter-3 hidden-architecture references to the DOJ Statement of Facts, the
document that records the chain of internal decisions Volkswagen AG admitted to as part of its
guilty plea.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 3 cites the Statement of Facts at three anchor occurrences (lines 67, 71, 75) as the
primary-source anchor for Q2 (control), Q4 (knowledge), and Q6 (record-controller) under the
eight-question diagnostic. The Statement is the modern procedural equivalent of the Pinto's
Grimshaw trial exhibits — a primary document that consolidates the chain at uncommon clarity.

## Diagnostic significance

The Statement's account of the 2006 defeat-device design decision, the 2007-2008 Bosch warning,
and the May 2014 - September 2015 obstruction window is the chapter's primary evidence base for
the alibi-shift sequence (technical-issues -> rogue-engineers -> defeat-device-software).
