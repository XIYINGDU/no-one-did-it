# Hofeller 2015 CVAP redistricting study

The card anchors the chapter-6 claim about the documented alternative purpose Hofeller's study identified for the census citizenship question — partisan and demographic advantage rather than VRA enforcement.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 6 line 103 originally carried an `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` marker requesting the verbatim phrasing of the operative sentence. Resolved 2026-05-26 with the two-clause version, which is stronger than the chapter's prior single-clause excerpt.

## Filing context

The study was filed publicly on 30 May 2019 by plaintiffs in *Kravitz v. U.S. Department of Commerce*, D. Md. Case No. 18-cv-1041, in a letter to Judge Jesse Furman; it was reproduced in plaintiffs' supplemental filings in *State of New York v. U.S. Department of Commerce*, S.D.N.Y. Stephanie Hofeller Lizon, the late Thomas Hofeller's estranged daughter, located the four external hard drives and 18 thumb drives after her father's death in August 2018 and delivered them to plaintiffs' counsel.
