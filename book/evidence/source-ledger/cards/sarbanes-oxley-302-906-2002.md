# Sarbanes-Oxley Act §§ 302 and 906 (2002) — paired certification regime

The card anchors the chapter-11 record-hardens (beat 5) and asymmetry-table (beat 7) treatment of
SOX §§ 302 / 906 as the U.S. responsibility-following-control statutory move at the
financial-reporting layer.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 11 cites §§ 302 / 906 at four anchor occurrences (lines 61 [x2], 106, 108, 174). Single
statutory card collects all four anchor occurrences into one (source, claim) pair on the paired
operation of § 302 (personal certification) and § 906 (criminal exposure for knowing or wilful
falsity).

## Doctrinal-reading caveat

The chapter notes that the criminal-enforcement record under § 906 is small in volume and rests
its argument on the structural effect of the named-signature requirement, not on prosecutorial
output. Alan's specialist verification covers whether this framing is supported.
