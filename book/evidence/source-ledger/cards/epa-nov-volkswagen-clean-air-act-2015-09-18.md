# EPA Notice of Violation to Volkswagen (18 September 2015) — regulator opening

The card anchors the chapter-3 accusation-scene reference to the EPA NOV that publicly opened the
Dieselgate enforcement record.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 3 cites the NOV at two anchor occurrences (lines 24 and 117). The NOV is the canonical
regulator-finding document for the case and the trigger for the public-record sequence that
followed (CARB parallel notice; Winterkorn resignation 23 Sept 2015; DOJ plea 11 Jan 2017).

## Diagnostic significance

For the chapter's eight-question walk, the NOV is the document that converted the CAFEE/WVU
technical record into a regulator-actionable finding under the Clean Air Act, making the
record-hardens beat legible in primary-source form.
