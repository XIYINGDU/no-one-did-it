# State of MP v. Keshub Mahindra et al. — CJM Bhopal June 7, 2010

Anchors chapter 5 beat-3 hidden-architecture citation of the 2010 CJM Bhopal conviction of seven
former UCIL officials, the layer at which the responsibility chain stopped.

## Holding context

Seven former UCIL officials, including former UCIL chairman Keshub Mahindra, were convicted on
June 7, 2010 of culpable homicide not amounting to murder (IPC s.304-A) and sentenced to two
years each. The case file (bhopal-union-carbide.md) treats this judgment as evidence that the
disaster was not a one-employee sabotage event and as the upper boundary of where the chain
stopped: UCC engineering at South Charleston, where the MIC unit had been designed and the May
1982 hazards documented, was not personally adjudicated; UCC corporate management at Danbury,
where the cost-reduction directives originated, was not personally adjudicated. Mahindra is
deceased (April 2023); the chapter draft carries a [Stephen] flag at this anchor pending appeal
status of the remaining six convicts before any individual naming beyond the public record.
