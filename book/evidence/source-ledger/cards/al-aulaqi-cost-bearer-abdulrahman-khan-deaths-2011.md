# Cost-bearer cluster: Abdulrahman al-Aulaqi and Samir Khan (2011)

The card carries the SECONDARY taxonomy classification of the cross-valence al-Aulaqi case:
cost-bearing goat. Abdulrahman al-Aulaqi (16, U.S. citizen, never alleged a combatant) and Samir
Khan absorbed lethal cost without being the named responsibility-holders. The primary classification
remains system/object alibi (legal-opinion-as-shield); this is the declared hybrid's secondary leg.

## Why this card exists and why it is graded carefully

Loki packet Candidate 1 correctly flagged this as the most evidence-fragile part of the case: the
cause of Abdulrahman's death is C-grade (no adversarially-tested accounting). The book's own rule 02
forbids anchoring a chapter claim on C-grade evidence. This card therefore SPLITS the grade:

- **A-grade, usable:** a 16-year-old U.S. citizen was killed by a U.S. drone strike on Oct. 14, 2011;
  Khan was killed in the Sept. 30 strike; the court characterized both as not-targeted / unanticipated.
- **C-grade, must be hedged or omitted:** WHY Abdulrahman died; the adequacy of the government's
  accounting; the "wrong place at the wrong time" attribution (NOT an admission; do not quote as one).

## Guidance for Wayne

The cleanest chapter use is a single hedged sentence: that a 16-year-old U.S. citizen was killed in a
separate strike two weeks later, was not (per the court) a target, and that no adversarially-tested
public accounting of his death exists. Do NOT build a sympathy beat that outruns the C-grade record
(rule 12 V1: earned not manufactured; rule 12 V3: sympathy follows the chain). Loki's open question 3
(confine to the memo + first strike, or mention the second strike at all) is a Bonnie/xaiolai call;
if mentioned, this is the grade envelope.
