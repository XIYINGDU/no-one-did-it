# Barron OLC memorandum on lethal operations against Anwar al-Aulaqi (July 16, 2010)

The card anchors the cross-valence (Democratic-administration) case proposed for chapter 8
to rebalance the cold-reader finding that every administration case in the book is Republican.
It is the structural twin of [[bybee-yoo-olc-torture-memos-2002-opr-2009]]: an OLC opinion
operating as the authorizing instrument for a contested executive action, which then becomes
the named cause ("it was lawful; OLC said so") while the named decision-makers recede behind
the legal instrument.

## Why this card exists

Loki's cross-valence packet (`process/review-memos/cross-valence-case-candidates-2026-05-28.md`,
Candidate 1) identified the al-Aulaqi OLC memo as an A-grade Democratic-administration case
that fits the book's system/object-alibi pattern (legal-opinion-as-shield) with near-perfect
symmetry to a case the book already uses against a Republican administration. This card locks
the load-bearing primary document.

## What is A-grade and what must be hedged

- **A-grade (primary record):** the memo's existence, date (July 16, 2010), exact caption,
  authorship (Barron, Acting AAG, OLC), its three principal conclusions, its years-long
  withholding, and its court-ordered release in June 2014.
- **Must be hedged (rule 05 / rule 07):** any characterization of intent, bad faith, or
  knowledge of wrongfulness on the part of Barron or any named official. The honest framing
  is the opinion-as-authorization structure, NOT "Obama murdered a citizen" — the act may well
  have been lawful; the laundering claim turns on control/benefit/knowledge/preventability and
  on the opinion detaching a discretionary decision from external review, which holds regardless
  of the strike's ultimate legality. See Loki packet section 6 (steelman) — the steelman that
  this is categorically different from Bybee/Yoo (lawful AUMF instrument of war vs. evasion of
  an absolute torture prohibition) is strong and the chapter must carry it at full strength.
