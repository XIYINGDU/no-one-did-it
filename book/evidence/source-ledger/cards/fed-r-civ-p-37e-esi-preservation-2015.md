# Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 37(e) — ESI preservation (2015 amendment)

The card anchors the chapter-12 line-65 citation of FRCP 37(e) as the litigation-hold
structural model the chapter advocates extending into administrative-record retention beyond
active-litigation triggers.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 beat 3 (rule three — make destruction visible) borrows the discipline from FRCP
37(e). The card carries the operative text and confirms the 2015 amendment scope. Post-2015
spoliation jurisprudence remains open for deferred work — the chapter's argument relies on the
rule's structural logic, not on a specific case interpretation.

## Deferred work

A future ch-12 polish pass may add a representative post-2015 spoliation case (e.g., a
sanctions ruling finding intent to deprive under 37(e)(2)) as a secondary card if the chapter
needs jurisprudence as well as the rule text.
