# Charan Lal Sahu (1989) + Bhopal Act 1985 — within-India interception apparatus

Anchors chapter 5 beat-9 the-escape citation of the within-India interception apparatus that
produced the 1989 settlement, the 1991 review judgment reinstating criminal proceedings, and the
2010 CJM convictions.

## Why this card exists

The chapter cites the Bhopal Act 1985 and the constitutional sustainment in *Charan Lal Sahu* (1989)
together as the institutional apparatus for the within-India interception. They are grouped into a
single card because the chapter treats them as one load-bearing piece of the responsibility-chain
infrastructure: the Act concentrated representation in the Government of India; the constitutional
sustainment locked that concentration into the procedural posture under which the 1989 settlement
order, the 1991 review judgment, and the 2010 CJM convictions were entered. The interception
climbed within India; it did not climb across the sovereign border to UCC engineering at South
Charleston or UCC corporate management at Danbury.
