# Walsh Final Report, Chapter 3 (Poindexter) — November 1986 PROFS deletions and backup recovery

The card anchors the chapter-12 beat-5 PROFS-deletion contrast: North deleted 736 PROFS messages
and Poindexter deleted 5,012 between November 22-29, 1986, but the WHCA-retained November 15 backup
tapes preserved the record, which investigators recovered before most deletions occurred.

## Why this card exists

The ch-12 render of the Iran-Contra record-destruction scene was promoted with a provisional inline
marker (`[CITE: walsh-final-report-iran-contra — pending-stephen-card]`) on the PROFS-deletion
sentence because no card existed for the deletion counts. The verification memo
`process/review-memos/ch12-shredding-record-verify-2026-05-29.md` established that the deletion counts
and backup-recovery live in the Walsh Final Report's Chapter 3 (Poindexter), with the North count
cross-recorded in Chapter 2 (North) — not in the Tower Commission Report. This card supplies the
primary anchor.

## Render discipline (carry-forward)

- The chapter's earlier draft described Poindexter as destroying "contemporaneous notes." The
  documented Poindexter acts are the PROFS deletion (5,012 messages) and the destruction of a signed
  presidential covert-action Finding (recorded separately); "contemporaneous notes" is imprecise and
  was corrected to the documented acts.
- The numeric contrast (736 + 5,012 deleted vs. the November 15 backup that survived) is a documented
  fact, not an abstraction.
