# Walsh Final Report, Chapter 5 (Fawn Hall) — November 1986 document destruction

The card anchors the chapter-12 beat-5 rendered shredding scene: the November 21, 1986
destruction of paper inside North's office, the alteration of originals on North's instruction,
and the November 25, 1986 removal of about sixteen pages concealed in Hall's clothing.

## Why this card exists

The ch-12 render of the Iran-Contra record-destruction scene was promoted with a provisional
inline marker (`[CITE: walsh-final-report-iran-contra — pending-stephen-card]`) because no card
existed for the shredding-scene specifics. The verification memo
`process/review-memos/ch12-shredding-record-verify-2026-05-29.md` established that the renderable
detail lives in the Walsh Final Report's Chapter 5 (Fawn Hall), not in the Tower Commission Report.
This card supplies the primary anchor.

- The shredding is concentrated on the evening of November 21, 1986; the broader
  destruction/alteration/removal conduct spans November 21-25, 1986 ("across November 21 through 25,
  1986"), not "spent days."
