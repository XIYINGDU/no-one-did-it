# Generalbundesanwalt January 2008 annulment of van der Lubbe's 1933 conviction

The card anchors the chapter-1 claim that van der Lubbe's 1933 Reichsgericht conviction was annulled by operation of the 1998 NS-AufhG via the *Generalbundesanwalt*'s 2007 determination, publicly announced January 2008.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 1 line 129 originally carried two `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` markers: the official German press-release text and a standard English translation. Both lifted 2026-05-26.

## English translation (author's translation per rule 06)

"The Federal Public Prosecutor's Office determined on 6 December 2007 that the verdict against the man convicted in the Reichstag fire trial was annulled."

The annulment rested on two specifically National Socialist injustice provisions: the *Verordnung des Reichspräsidenten zum Schutz von Volk und Staat* of 28 February 1933 (which introduced the death penalty retroactively) and the *Gesetz über Verhängung und Vollzug der Todesstrafe* of 29 March 1933 (the *Lex van der Lubbe*, applying the increased penalty retroactively to acts committed before 28 February 1933).
