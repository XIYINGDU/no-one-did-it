# Boeing OMB TBC-19 (Nov 6, 2018) — initial AOA-sensor framing

Anchors chapter 5 beat-4 older-echo citation of Boeing's first post-Lion-Air communication
framing the AOA sensor (not MCAS architecture) as the issue.

## Why this card exists

The bulletin is the principal documentary anchor for the chapter's claim that Boeing's initial
framing concentrated attribution on the AOA sensor input rather than on the MCAS authority that
acted on the input. The bulletin's contents are corroborated through the KNKT Final Report and
the House T&I Final Committee Report (existing A-grade cards), which together establish what the
bulletin did and did not disclose.
