# ACSAA — Pub. L. 116-260 (December 27, 2020)

Anchors chapter 5 beat-7 alibi-weakens citation of the Aircraft Certification, Safety, and
Accountability Act of 2020 as the statutory reform of the ODA framework that produced the 737 MAX
certification gap.

## Why this card exists

The chapter anchors the ACSAA as the third of three principal alibi-weakening events on the Boeing
side (paired with the KNKT Final Report and the House T&I Final Committee Report). The Act is the
statutory expression of the FAA's procedural reform; the chapter treats it as the end of the
alibi-weakening arc, not the resolution of the chain question (the certification-process layer
where the criminal chain stopped is read separately at beat 9 via the DPA / breach / NPA sequence).
