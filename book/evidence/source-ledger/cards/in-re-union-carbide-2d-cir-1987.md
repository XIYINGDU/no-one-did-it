# In re Union Carbide (2d Cir. 1987) — forum non conveniens dismissal upheld

Anchors chapter 5 beat-2 official-story citation of the Second Circuit decision locking US-court
dismissal of the consolidated Bhopal tort actions.

## Holding context

The Second Circuit affirmed Judge Keenan's SDNY dismissal on forum non conveniens grounds, with
modifications to two of the three conditions Keenan had imposed (Union Carbide's consent to
Indian jurisdiction and the discovery condition). The chapter uses the decision as a
record-controller artifact: the holding administratively confined the parent-corporation chain
to Indian courts and was one of two load-bearing record-locking events in the alibi-hardening
sequence (paired with the 1989 Indian Supreme Court settlement).
