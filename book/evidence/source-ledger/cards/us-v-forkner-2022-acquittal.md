  text: Mark A. Forkner, former Boeing 737 Chief Technical Pilot, was indicted on four counts of wire fraud in October 2021 for allegedly deceiving the FAA's Aircraft Evaluation Group about the operational scope of MCAS. He was tried in the Northern District of Texas and acquitted by a jury in March 2022. He was the only individual to face trial arising from the 737 MAX certification conduct documented in the 7 January 2021 DPA Statement of Facts. The second Technical Pilot named in the DPA, Patrik Gustavsson, was not personally charged.
