# Hamilton & Ors v. Post Office Limited [2021] EWCA Crim 577 — "affront to the conscience of the Court"

The card anchors the chapter-7 claim that the Court of Appeal held the Post Office's failure to disclose what it knew about Horizon's unreliability was "an affront to the conscience of the Court." Chapter 7 lines 65 and 143 both rely on this verbatim finding as load-bearing for the disclosure-failure prong of the diagnostic walk.

This card carries the distinct "affront to the conscience" abuse-of-process finding.
