# Ethics in Government Act of 1978, Title VI — Independent Counsel (as in force Dec. 1986)

The card anchors the chapter-12 line-85 citation of the statutory authority under which
Lawrence E. Walsh was appointed Independent Counsel for Iran/Contra Matters on Dec. 19, 1986.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 beat 5 ("record hardens") opens with the statutory authority for Walsh's
appointment. Alan (administrative-law frame) flagged at the brief stage that the chapter
must specify the operative version of Title VI at the appointment date (post-1983
reauthorization, pre-1987 reauthorization); the chapter's prose now uses the precise
qualifier and this card carries the verification.
