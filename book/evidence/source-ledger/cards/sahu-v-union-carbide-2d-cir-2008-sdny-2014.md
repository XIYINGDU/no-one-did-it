# Sahu v. Union Carbide (2d Cir. 2008; SDNY 2014)

Anchors chapter 5 beat-7 alibi-weakens citation of the 1999-2014 Sahu SDNY litigation that
introduced internal UCC engineering documents into the US court record.

## Why this card exists

The Sahu litigation arc is treated as a single record-controller artifact in the chapter because
the chapter uses it for one load-bearing claim: internal UCC engineering documents bearing on
parent-design-authority were introduced into the US court record, even though the final 2014
dismissal turned on statute-of-limitations grounds and did not reach the merits of the
parent-liability question. The specific UCC May 1982 Operational Safety Survey introduced through
this docket is carried in a separate card pending Delon's PACER pull.
