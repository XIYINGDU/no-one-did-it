Chapter 3 cites the plea agreement at line 24 as the procedural anchor for the corporate guilty
plea. The plea entry before Judge Sean F. Cox on 10 March 2017 is covered by a separate card
(us-v-volkswagen-ag-16-cr-20394-plea-entered-2017-03-10). The accompanying DOJ Statement of Facts
is covered by doj-statement-of-facts-us-v-volkswagen-ag-2017-01-11.
