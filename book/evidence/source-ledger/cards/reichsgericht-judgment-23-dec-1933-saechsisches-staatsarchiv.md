# Reichsgericht judgment of 23 December 1933 (Sächsisches Staatsarchiv Leipzig)

Anchors the chapter-1 claim that the Reichsgericht's 23 December 1933 verdict convicted van der Lubbe alone (of Hochverrat and arson against a public building) and acquitted Torgler, Dimitrov, Popov, and Tanev. The chapter's "Dimitrov reduced the prosecution's conspiracy case to public embarrassment" beat rests on the trial record itself; the acquittals are the recorded outcome.

Also anchors the van der Lubbe execution date (10 January 1934, three days before his 25th birthday, guillotined in the prison courtyard at Leipzig). The execution date is uncontested across every standard source.
