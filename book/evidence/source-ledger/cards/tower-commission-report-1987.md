## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 beat 5 ("record hardens") relies on the Tower Commission as the documentary
demonstration that the PROFS backups preserved a record the destroyers thought had been
eliminated. The chapter's two narrative beats — the destruction by North and Poindexter, and
the recovery via WHCA preservation and Tower-Commission reprint — both anchor against this
report.
