# Federal Records Act — 44 U.S.C. chapter 31

The card anchors the chapter-12 line-65 citation of the Federal Records Act as the operative
statute the chapter's rule three advocates extending into routine non-litigation enforcement.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 12 beat 3 (rule three — make destruction visible) and beat 8 (anti-laundering rule)
both cite the FRA as the statutory floor whose enforcement architecture is the chapter's
target. The card carries the chapter/section citation and cross-references
[[judicial-watch-v-nara-2017-dc-cir]] which limits private enforcement.

## Companion statute

Chapter 12 also cites the Presidential Records Act, 44 U.S.C. §§ 2201 et seq., on the same line.
The PRA is a structurally parallel statute (records-management duties on the President and
the Executive Office) and the chapter cites it in the same enforcement-deficit context. A
ch-12 backfill pass may create a separate `presidential-records-act-44-usc-2201` card if
needed; the present card carries the principal FRA citation.
