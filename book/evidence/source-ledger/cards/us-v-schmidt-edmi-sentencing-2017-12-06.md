docket.

## Why this card exists

counterargument cluster, alongside Liang's sentencing and the Stadler conviction in Germany.

## Diagnostic significance

For the chapter's diagnostic walk, Schmidt's seven-year sentence is the procedural anchor for
the deepest individual criminal consequence imposed in the US Dieselgate case. The chapter uses
this against the steelman counterargument that "this is pure deliberate fraud, not laundering"
— Schmidt's sentence is real accountability, and the chapter's argument concerns whether the
chain stopped at the right level.
