# CAFEE/WVU PEMS in-use diesel emissions report (15 May 2014) — the piercing artifact

The card anchors the chapter-3 accusation-scene and hidden-architecture references to the
CAFEE/WVU on-road emissions testing that surfaced VW's in-use NOx discrepancy and triggered the
16-month CARB/EPA enforcement window.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 3 cites this report at three anchor occurrences (lines 20, 71, 117). The report is the
load-bearing piercing artifact for the diagnostic walk: it converted a research question ("are US
diesel cars clean?") into an enforcement-actionable empirical record that no laboratory-only
compliance protocol could surface.

## Diagnostic significance

independent technical verification breaking a procedural-shell alibi. The report's small budget
(approximately $50,000 per CAFEE accounts in subsequent journalism) against the enforcement
