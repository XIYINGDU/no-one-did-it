# Boeing 737 MAX Deferred Prosecution Agreement (7 January 2021)

The card anchors the chapter-11 opening accusation-scene claim that the Boeing DPA closed the
prosecution at the corporate layer without indicting any officer above the Technical Pilot operating
layer, despite the FAA having received misleading information about MCAS.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 11's design-rule argument depends on a worked example where the corporate-plea structure
operated without a Park-shaped named-officer attachment. The Boeing DPA is the chapter's clearest
recent U.S. example. The card is the first card created for the chapter's accusation scene; ch-5
treated Boeing at full case depth and may add this card to its own sidecar on later backfill.
