# RGBl I 1933 Nr. 28 p. 151 — Lex van der Lubbe (29 March 1933)

Anchors the chapter-1 claim that the retroactive death-penalty law that authorized van der Lubbe's execution was published in RGBl I 1933 Nr. 28 at page 151. The retroactivity is the load-bearing fact that, sixty-five years later, becomes the legal ground for the 1998 NS-AufhG annulment (see card `generalbundesanwalt-van-der-lubbe-annulment-2008`).
