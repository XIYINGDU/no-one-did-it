  text: Article 78 of Additional Protocol I to the Geneva Conventions of 1949 (1977) prohibits the evacuation by a party to the conflict of children, other than its own nationals, to a foreign country, except temporarily, except where compelling reasons of health or safety require it, and except with the written consent of the parents or legal guardians where they can be found. The article requires that the child's identification be preserved, that education be in the child's own language and culture, and that the child be returned when conditions allow.
