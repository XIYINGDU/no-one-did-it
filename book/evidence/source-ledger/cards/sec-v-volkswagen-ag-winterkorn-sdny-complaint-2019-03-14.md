## Why this card exists

Chapter 3 cites the SEC complaint at line 75 as a procedural anchor for the breadth of the
record-controller layer of the diagnostic: the case crossed regulatory jurisdictions (EPA, CARB,
DOJ Criminal Division, SEC) and produced a primary-source record at each layer.

## Diagnostic significance

For the chapter's Q6 (record controller) treatment, the SEC complaint is the document that makes
the connection between the underlying engineering misconduct and the US capital-market misconduct
legible in adjudicatory form. The complaint also names Winterkorn personally as the executive
whose alleged knowledge of the defeat device coincided with the bond issuance, sustaining the
chapter's "named-individual reach upward" analysis at allegation-stage strength.
