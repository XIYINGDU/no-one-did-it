# Executive Order 13328 (6 February 2004) — Robb-Silberman charter

The card anchors the chapter-11 accusation-scene and beat-9 ("the escape") contrast between the
Robb-Silberman charter scope and the UK Inquiries Act 2005 §21 compulsion design.

## Why this card exists

Chapter 11 uses EO 13328 as the third accusation-scene exhibit (alongside the Boeing DPA and the
Bybee/Levin OLC pair) of a design that contains nothing unlawful on its face yet leaves the
named-attribution seam unfilled. Chapter 8 established the political and intelligence context;
this card anchors the executive instrument itself for ch-11's design-rule reading and may be added
to ch-8's sidecar on a later backfill pass.
