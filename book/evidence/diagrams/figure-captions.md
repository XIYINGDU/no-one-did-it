## Figure 1 — ch-2 · Four-category taxonomy table (`four-goats.html`)

**Caption.** The four ways blame is moved off the actor who held control: pure scapegoat, partial scapegoat, system/object alibi, cost-bearing goat — installed once, reused by every later chapter.

**Audio-rendering paragraph.**

> *Audio-form rendering of the table that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> Four categories, one discrimination. The first is the pure scapegoat: a largely innocent actor is blamed so the case can be closed, and the blame protects whoever the substitution served. Sacco and Vanzetti, convicted in 1921 on contested evidence, sit near this category — though, as we will see, the individual case is harder to classify than the category. The second is the partial scapegoat: a genuinely involved actor is blamed, but the blame stops too low, leaving the wider chain above the stopping point untouched. The junior military police court-martialled over Abu Ghraib are the type case — the chain above them was largely never reached. The third is the system or object alibi: a machine, a model, a market, or a legal entity becomes the named cause, while responsibility sits with whoever designed, deployed, or profited from it. The Therac-25 radiation deaths, blamed on "the machine," are the early instance. The fourth is the cost-bearing goat: a victim absorbs the harm without ever being accused, while the controllers who avoided the cost are the ones responsibility would name. The Bhopal gas-disaster victims bore the harm. Keep the four apart. The discrimination is the instrument; the rest of the book runs it.

## Figure 2 — ch-3 · VW Dieselgate responsibility-chain map (`vw-chain.html`) — Nancy-flagged

**Caption.** Volkswagen "Dieselgate": what the story blamed — individual engineers and "the software" — against where control, benefit, and the avoided cost actually sat.

**Audio-rendering paragraph.**

> *Audio-form rendering of the chain map that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> The story blamed individual engineers and "the software." Volkswagen first described "a few" engineers; James Liang and Oliver Schmidt were convicted in the United States. But the eight questions point upward. On control: VW engineering and management approved the defeat-device approach to meet the US emissions limits without giving up performance or cost. On benefit: Volkswagen AG took the diesel sales and the "Clean Diesel" market position, and the cost of genuinely compliant engineering was avoided. On knowledge: the EPA and the Department of Justice found the device was built deliberately to detect the test cycle — but how far up that knowledge reached is contested, and CEO Martin Winterkorn, who was charged in the United States and in Germany, has denied knowing. On preventability: a compliant, costlier engineering path existed and was not taken. On record control: the deception lived inside the car's own software, which sensed the test and changed the emissions, so the records lied. The cost fell on roughly eleven million vehicle owners, on the public breathing the excess nitrogen oxides, and on the individual engineers who were prosecuted. Blame landed on the people and the machine at the bottom of the chain. Control and benefit sat at the top.

## Figure 3 — ch-7 · Five-role conflation schematic, Horizon (`ch07-horizon-five-roles.html`) — Nancy-flagged

**Caption.** One institution wearing five record-control roles — deployer, audit-trail holder, complainant, prosecutor, disclosure controller — against a lone sub-postmaster in the dock.

**Audio-rendering paragraph.**

> *Audio-form rendering of the schematic that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> Run the eight questions against the Post Office and watch them return the same answer. The Post Office was the deployer: it bought Horizon from Fujitsu Services and ran it on every branch terminal. It was the audit-trail holder: it held the Known Error Log, the source code, and the remote-access logs — the very record that could have falsified the accounts. It was the complainant: when a Horizon shortfall appeared, it identified the sub-postmaster as having stolen or falsely accounted. It was the prosecutor: it brought criminal prosecutions of sub-postmasters directly under English law, without a Crown Prosecution Service charging review — the Inquiry's Volume 1 Final Report, at paragraph 3.24, records that approximately one thousand sub-postmasters were prosecuted and convicted across the United Kingdom based on Horizon evidence. And it was the disclosure controller: it decided what to disclose under the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act, and it withheld the engineering record. Fujitsu held the engineering record and the source code. At the bottom stood the lone sub-postmaster in the dock — dispersed, self-employed, one per branch. The Inquiry's Volume 1 records approximately one thousand prosecuted and convicted across the United Kingdom based on Horizon evidence; contemporaneous reporting puts the imprisonment count at two hundred and thirty-six, with around four thousand more pursued in civil recovery and, on Sir Wyn Williams's finding in Volume 1 at paragraph 3.12, thirteen suicides — a causal link that Williams said he could not rule out. Seema Misra was sentenced at eight weeks pregnant in 2010; her conviction was not quashed until 2021. Six of the eight questions return the same institution; two return its contractor. There was no one left to check it.

## Figure 4 — ch-13 · Role-by-domain crosswalk (`ch13-crosswalk.html`)

**Caption.** Eight reader roles against six anti-laundering moves: the eight questions do not change at each reader's surface — the action does — and the blank cells are deliberate.

**Audio-rendering paragraph.**

> *Audio-form rendering of the crosswalk that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> The eight questions do not change as they reach each of us. The action does. Take the six moves and walk them across the roles we actually occupy. Name the controller: a citizen names the displaced human chain behind public-cause language; an employee asks in writing which manager made the decision; a journalist refuses the verb whose subject is a thing. Demand the record: file a public-record request, keep your own contemporaneous notes, insist consequential records have more than one custodian. Refuse the signature: decline to sign where the procedure names no decider, or to certify on a product you cannot inspect. Make destruction visible: read for destruction announcements that carry no inventory, request preservation in writing before any deletion, report the inventory-less destruction as the story itself. Apply the design test: ask, in a hearing or a draft statute, whether the design names a controller or only a procedure. And vote for the architecture: support the inquiry-statute and oversight-charter questions that decide every other cell. Some cells are blank on purpose — a juror cannot vote on a charter from the jury box, and the guide refuses to invent an action to fill a box. The one starred cell is the voter's: vote and advocate for the compelling architecture, because that is the cell that determines all the others.

## Figure 6 — ch-9 · Three-layer AI-stack table (`ch09-ai-stack.html`) — Nancy-flagged · LIVE content (Bartz)

**Caption.** "The model did it" at three layers — input, deployment, evaluation — each with a different non-human subject taking the verb and a different party bearing the cost.

**Audio-rendering paragraph.**

> *Audio-form rendering of the table that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> The machine-as-cause alibi runs at three layers, so the diagnostic runs three times, not once. At the input layer, the named non-human subjects are "data," "the corpus," "the shadow library," "the market"; the displaced human decision is which corpora were acquired, on what dates, from what sources, under what licensing. The cost-bearers are the authors, photographers, journalists, code contributors, and artists — erased from the grammar, not accused. At the deployment layer, the subjects are "the model," "the reward signal," "the release process"; the displaced decision is the version designation, the reward-shaping change, the release approval. The cost-bearers are hundreds of millions of users acted on by the deployed model; the interception is user-community surfacing on platforms like X and Reddit, a fast rollback, and a self-authored post-mortem, over about seventy-two hours, reaching fewer people. The anchor case is OpenAI's GPT-4o sycophancy post-mortem of the twenty-ninth of April 2025. At the evaluation layer, the subjects are "the variant," "the policy," "the benchmark"; the displaced decision is which variant was submitted, under what disclosure policy, on what date. The cost-bearers are the credentialing commons — the open-source community, downstream developers, rival providers; the interception is benchmark-organisation self-reform, over about ten days, leaving the architecture intact. The anchor case is the Llama-4-Maverick experimental variant submitted to LMArena in April 2025. A different named agent, a different displaced decision, a different cost-bearer at each layer.

## Figure 7 — ch-10 · Five-layer war-stack table (`ch10-war-stack.html`) — Nancy-flagged

**Caption.** War stacks five laundering layers that fire at once; the diagnostic resolves each by climbing into it, not by granting war an exemption.

**Audio-rendering paragraph.**

> *Audio-form rendering of the table that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> War does not escape the diagnostic; it stacks five of its layers at once. The first is chain-of-command secrecy: low-rank personnel are blamed while doctrine memos and theatre authorisations stay classified through the operative window. At Abu Ghraib, the night shift of the 372nd Military Police Company was court-martialled; the layer above staff-sergeant rank — the Office of Legal Counsel memos by Bybee and Yoo of the first of August 2002, Rumsfeld's approvals, and Sanchez's authorisation of the fourteenth of September 2003 — was never criminally adjudicated. The second is proxy deniability: operations run through entities of deniable legal status while sovereign command of supply and training is denied. Over MH17, the Buk launcher of the Russian 53rd Brigade was attributed to "the DPR"; the Joint Investigation Team reached "strong indications" of senior-command approval — a prosecutorial finding, not a verdict — and the convicted individuals were not surrendered. The third is the contractor legal-status shell: private military and logistics operate under contracts that exclude criminal jurisdiction. At Nisour Square, seventeen civilians were killed; the chain of command above the contractors was never criminally examined, CPA Order 17 granted immunity, and the 2014 convictions were reversed by the December 2020 pardons. The fourth is the civilian-invisibility euphemism: cost-bearers are reframed in care-language while the underlying conduct continues. The Russian transfer of Ukrainian children ran under Decrees 330 and 585; the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Putin and Lvova-Belova on the seventeenth of March 2023, on its "reasonable grounds" standard. The fifth is strategic-justification reification: a national institutional product is presented with its hedges stripped. The 2002 Iraq WMD National Intelligence Estimate was presented as certainty at the United Nations on the fifth of February 2003; the diagnostic climbs to the rooms that set that certainty — and notes that the Robb-Silberman commission was fenced by charter from examining policy use. Five layers fire simultaneously. Naming the layer is naming the operation; the diagnostic resolves each by climbing, not by exception.

## Figure 9 — ch-12 · Ten record-fights table (`ch12-record-fights.html`) — Nancy by reference

**Caption.** Ten record fights in two columns: every record that survived satisfied at least two of the three rules — write it down, keep custody outside, make destruction visible — and every suppressed record lacked at least two.

> *Audio-form rendering of the table that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> Survival was not random. Read ten record fights in two columns and the pattern is the argument. The Pentagon Papers survived: written by McNamara's order, with copies held outside the originating institution — rules one and two. Iran-Contra survived in part: the PROFS backup chain outlived the awareness of those deleting — rule two. The Iraq Inquiry's Cabinet papers survived: statutory compulsion under the Inquiries Act made suppression visible — rule three. The DOGE Signal and records destruction is contested still: rule three is being litigated in *CREW v. DOGE* and *American Oversight v. DOGE*. And the Russian decrees on the transfer of Ukrainian children were suppressed within their own jurisdiction: the counter-record had to be assembled from outside it, by the UN human-rights office, the Joint Investigation Team, the European Court, and the International Criminal Court. In every surviving row, at least two of the three rules were in operation; in every suppressed row, at least two were absent. The asymmetry is the argument.

## Figure 11 — ch-5 · Bhopal responsibility ladder (`ch05-bhopal-ladder.html`)

> **PLACEMENT NOTE (inventory).** Run only if the signature chain map landed in ch-3 (no two adjacent chain maps). If ch-3's VW map is the sole chain-map in the early run, this ladder may be cut to a prose-only treatment; the audio paragraph below stands on its own without the figure.

**Caption.** The Bhopal responsibility ladder: blame settled at the top floor and the bottom — the two floors in between, where design authority and cost-cutting sat, were never reached by a court.

**Audio-rendering paragraph.**

> *Audio-form rendering of the ladder that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> Climb the Bhopal ladder one floor at a time and watch where the courts stopped. On the bottom floor stand the UCIL plant operators — the night-shift crew who ran the methyl-isocyanate unit, the proximate and most-visible actors; this layer was adjudicated, with seven UCIL officials convicted in 2010 on two-year sentences. One floor up sits UCIL local management, holding day-to-day operational control, including keeping the Tank E610 refrigeration off through 1984; adjudicated at this layer. Above them, UCIL's Indian engineering management worked under a budget envelope set higher still; partly adjudicated. Then the two floors that no court reached. Union Carbide Corporation's engineering arm in South Charleston, West Virginia, held the design authority for the methyl-isocyanate process and documented sixty-one hazards, thirty of them major, in its May 1982 Operational Safety Survey; not adjudicated. Above it, Union Carbide corporate management in Danbury, Connecticut, held parent control through majority equity and technical-service agreements, and was the origin of the 1983–84 cost-cutting that reduced safety staffing; not adjudicated. At the apex stands Warren Anderson, the corporation's chairman and the single human personification of blame in 1984: charged that year, photographed under guard, released on bail, never extradited, never tried, dead in 2014. The two floors where control and knowledge concentrated — design at South Charleston, cost-cutting at Danbury — are the two no court reached.

## Figure 12 — ch-6 · Three-case pretext-grammar table (`ch06-pretext-grammar.html`) — Nancy-flagged

**Caption.** Three pretexts, one grammar: each cites a real authority that does not require what was done, each has a documented alternative purpose, and each was pierced by a venue that reached the record.

**Audio-rendering paragraph.**

> *Audio-form rendering of the table that follows. Print and screen readers may skip to the figure.*
>
> Three cases share one grammar of pretext, and we can read it across four dimensions. Each cites a real authority. Family separation cited the improper-entry misdemeanor at 8 U.S.C. § 1325, on the books since 1929; the census citizenship question cited Voting Rights Act enforcement, via a Justice Department data request; the Ukraine aid hold cited an apportionment review under the Impoundment Control Act. Each cited authority did not require what was done. The misdemeanor permits prosecution but does not require the blanket referral of parents with children; Voting Rights enforcement can run on existing survey data and does not require a decennial citizenship question; the Impoundment Control Act permits a deferral only with formal notice to Congress, not a policy hold without it. Each has a documented alternative purpose — and the source of that finding matters. For family separation, the Justice Department Inspector General and a Senate subcommittee found the purpose was deterrence of Central-American migration. For the census, the Hofeller files, surfaced posthumously, showed the purpose was redistricting advantage for, in the files' own words, "Republicans and Non-Hispanic Whites." For the Ukraine hold, the released call memorandum and Sondland's sworn testimony recorded the request for a "favor" — a public announcement of investigations of a domestic political rival. And each was pierced by a venue that reached the record: the *Ms. L.* litigation before Judge Sabraw and the inspector-general reports; the Supreme Court's review in *Department of Commerce v. New York* alongside the posthumous Hofeller disclosure; the Government Accountability Office's statutory opinion and the House impeachment. The cost fell on separated children and parents, several hundred still unreunited as of January 2026; on a census question removed only because of one private disclosure; and on Ukraine during active hostilities, along with the career officials who testified — Vindman, Yovanovitch, and the inspector general Atkinson, who was removed.
