“The model did it” — at three different layers

The machine-as-cause alibi operates at three layers of an AI system. At each, a different non-human subject takes the verb, a different human decision is displaced, a different party bears the cost — so the diagnostic must run three times, not once.

Deployment “the model,” “the reward signal,” “the release process” The version designation, the reward-shaping change, the release / default-swap approval Hundreds of millions of users acted on by the deployed model User-community surfacing (X / Reddit) + fast rollback + self-authored post-mortem (~72 hrs; reaches less) GPT-4o sycophancy post-mortem — OpenAI, 29 Apr 2025
Evaluation “the variant,” “the policy,” “the benchmark” The submission decision: which variant, under what disclosure policy, on what date The credentialing commons — open-source community, downstream developers, rival providers Benchmark-organisation self-reform (policy revision; ~10 days; leaves the architecture intact) Llama-4-Maverick-03-26-Experimental → LMArena (Apr 2025)
The diagnostic runs three times per AI system, not once: a different named agent, a different displaced decision, a different cost-bearer at each layer.
