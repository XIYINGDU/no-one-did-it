Eight channels of accountability, one scandal

The Post Office Horizon scandal across eight accountability channels — which succeeded, which is in progress, which has not been delivered.
Channel Horizon outcome as of May 2026
Civil-society aggregation Justice for Subpostmasters Alliance founded May 2009; survived institutional resistance for a decade; produced the documentary base for litigation. Succeeded.
Litigation funding Therium funded the Bates Group Litigation Order from 2017. Succeeded — though substantial share of the December 2019 £57.75 million settlement absorbed by funder cost, reducing per-claimant distribution.
Independent technical examination Jason Coyne's reverse-engineering of Horizon error logs and Known Error Log records. Succeeded as the technical foundation for the Horizon Issues findings.
Court with disclosure power High Court (Fraser J) granted the Group Litigation Order, compelled disclosure, and handed down Common Issues and Horizon Issues judgments in 2019. Succeeded.
Criminal-conviction reversal Court of Appeal (Criminal Division) Hamilton & Others [2021] EWCA Crim 577 quashed 39 convictions; subsequent appellate judgments brought the judicially overturned total to approximately 100. Succeeded for those reached.
Statutory political response Post Office (Horizon System) Offences Act 2024 (Royal Assent 24 May 2024) — statutory exoneration without case-by-case appeal. Succeeded.
Statutory inquiry Post Office Horizon IT Inquiry under Sir Wyn Williams, statutory under the Inquiries Act 2005 since June 2021; final report pending. In progress.
Criminal accountability for institutional actors Operation Olympos investigating possible offences since 2020; no charges as of May 2026. Not delivered to date.
