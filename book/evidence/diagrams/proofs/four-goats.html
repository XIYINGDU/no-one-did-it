The four goats

Four ways blame is moved off the actor who actually held control.
Category The move Blame lands on Responsibility sits with Worked example
Pure scapegoat A largely innocent actor is blamed so the case can be closed. the substitute whoever the substitution protects Sacco & Vanzetti — convicted 1921 on contested evidence.
Partial scapegoat A genuinely involved actor is blamed, but the blame stops too low. a low-level participant the wider chain above the stopping point Abu Ghraib — junior MPs court-martialed; the chain above largely untouched.
System / object alibi A machine, model, market, or legal entity becomes the named cause. the thing (“it malfunctioned”) whoever designed, deployed, or profited from it Therac-25 — “the machine” blamed for radiation deaths.
Cost-bearing goat A victim absorbs the harm without ever being the responsibility-holder. — not accused; simply bears it the controllers who avoided the cost Bhopal — gas-disaster victims bore the harm.
