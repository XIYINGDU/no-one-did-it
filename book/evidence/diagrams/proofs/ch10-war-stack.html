War stacks five laundering layers at once

War is not an exception to the diagnostic; it is where the most layers fire together. Naming the layer is naming the operation — and the diagnostic resolves each by climbing into it, not by granting war an exemption.
Layer Mechanism Anchor case What the diagnostic climbs to
Chain-of-command secrecy Low-rank personnel are blamed while doctrine memos and theatre authorisations stay classified through the operative window. Abu Ghraib — 372nd MP Company night shift court-martialled OLC memos (Bybee / Yoo, 1 Aug 2002); Rumsfeld approvals; Sanchez’s 14 Sep 2003 authorisation — the layer above staff-sergeant rank, never criminally adjudicated
Proxy deniability Operations run through entities of deniable legal status while sovereign command of supply and training is denied. MH17 — Buk-TELAR of the Russian 53rd Brigade, attributed to “DPR” / Ukraine State supply-chain responsibility — JIT’s “strong indications” of senior-command approval (a prosecutorial finding, not a verdict); convicted individuals not surrendered
Contractor legal-status shell Private military / logistics operate under contracts that exclude criminal jurisdiction, framed as administrative convenience. Blackwater / Nisour Square — 17 civilians killed, 16 Sep 2007 The chain of command above the contractor — never criminally examined; CPA Order 17 immunity; the 2014 convictions reversed by the Dec 2020 pardons
Civilian-invisibility euphemism Cost-bearers are reframed in care-language while the underlying conduct continues. Russian transfer of Ukrainian children — Decrees No. 330 & No. 585, 2022 The conduct, not the language: ICC arrest warrants for Putin and Lvova-Belova (17 Mar 2023, “reasonable grounds”)
Strategic-justification reification A national institutional product is presented with its hedges stripped; the audit body is barred by charter from examining policy use. Iraq WMD NIE (Oct 2002) presented as certainty at the UN (5 Feb 2003) The rooms that set the public certainty — WHIG, OVP / NSC speech drafting, the OSP channel, the No. 10 dossier; Robb-Silberman was fenced from examining policy use
Five layers fire simultaneously. Naming the layer is naming the operation; the diagnostic resolves each by climbing, not by exception.
