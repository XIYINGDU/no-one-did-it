# Acronyms and Institutions

Quick reference for the legal, administrative, and technical shorthand used in the chapters. Sources are named in full on first appearance in each chapter; this list collects them for lookup.

---

**ACS** — American Community Survey. The U.S. Census Bureau's sample-based instrument that has supplied citizenship data since 2005, replacing the long-form decennial. *(Ch. 6.)*

**AEA** — Alien Enemies Act of 1798 (50 U.S.C. § 21). U.S. statute permitting the President to detain or remove non-citizens of a hostile nation in time of declared war or "invasion." Invoked by Proclamation 10903 of March 14–15, 2025. *(Ch. 8.)*

**CAFEE** — Center for Alternative Fuels, Engines, and Emissions at West Virginia University. The 2014 testing that surfaced VW Dieselgate. *(Ch. 3.)*

**CARB** — California Air Resources Board. *(Ch. 3.)*

**CCRC** — Criminal Cases Review Commission (UK). The body that referred the principal block of Horizon convictions to the Court of Appeal. *(Ch. 7.)*

**CPA Order 17** — Coalition Provisional Authority Order Number 17 (Iraq, 2004 revision). Granted contractors immunity from Iraqi criminal jurisdiction. Repealed prospectively by the 2008 U.S.–Iraq Status of Forces Agreement. *(Ch. 4.)*

**CPIA** — Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996 (UK). Governs prosecution disclosure to the defence; the initial duty sits at section 3 and the continuing duty at section 7A, added by the Criminal Justice Act 2003. *(Ch. 7.)*

**CREW** — Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington. Plaintiff in *CREW v. U.S. DOGE Service*. *(Chs. 8, 12.)*

**DHS OIG** — Department of Homeland Security Office of Inspector General. *(Ch. 6.)*

**DOGE** — Department of Government Efficiency. U.S. body chartered by Executive Order 14158 of January 20, 2025. *(Chs. 8, 12.)*

**DOJ OIG** — Department of Justice Office of Inspector General. *(Ch. 6.)*

**DPA / NPA** — Deferred Prosecution Agreement / Non-Prosecution Agreement. *(Chs. 5, 9.)*

**ECAA** — Ethiopian Civil Aviation Authority. Investigated Ethiopian Airlines ET302. *(Ch. 5.)*

**ECtHR** — European Court of Human Rights. *(Chs. 4, 7.)*

**EO** — Executive Order (U.S.). The numbered orders cited include 13328 (Robb-Silberman, 2004), 13841 (family-separation reversal, 2018), and 14158 (DOGE, 2025). *(Chs. 6, 8, 10, 11.)*

**EPA** — Environmental Protection Agency (U.S.). *(Ch. 3.)*

**FCA** — Financial Conduct Authority (UK). Operates the SMCR regime. *(Ch. 11.)*

**FDCA** — Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (U.S.). The statute the Park doctrine enforces. *(Ch. 11.)*

**FOIA** — Freedom of Information Act (U.S., 5 U.S.C. § 552). *(Ch. 6.)*

**FRA** — Federal Records Act (U.S.). Governs preservation of federal records; live in *CREW v. DOGE*. *(Ch. 12.)*

**FSMA 2000 / FSBRA 2013** — Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 / Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013 (UK). Together they install the Senior Managers and Certification Regime. *(Ch. 11.)*

**GAO** — Government Accountability Office (U.S., congressional). Issued Decision B-331564 (2020) on the Ukraine aid hold. *(Ch. 6.)*

**ICAO Annex 13** — International Civil Aviation Organization, Convention on International Civil Aviation, Annex 13. Governs civil aircraft accident investigation. The Indonesian and Ethiopian boards investigated the 737 MAX crashes under Annex 13 procedure. *(Ch. 5.)*

**ICA** — Impoundment Control Act of 1974 (U.S., 2 U.S.C. § 681). Permits an executive deferral of congressionally appropriated funds only with formal notice to Congress. *(Ch. 6.)*

**ICC PTC II** — International Criminal Court, Pre-Trial Chamber II. Issued arrest warrants for President Putin and Commissioner Lvova-Belova on March 17, 2023. *(Chs. 2, 10.)*

**ICIG** — Intelligence Community Inspector General (U.S.). Michael Atkinson, holder of the office during the 2019 whistleblower process on the Ukraine aid hold, was removed in April 2020. *(Ch. 6.)*

**JFSA** — Justice for Subpostmasters Alliance. Founded by Alan Bates in May 2009; the aggregating institution that made the Horizon Group Litigation possible. *(Ch. 7.)*

**JIC** — Joint Intelligence Committee (UK). *(Chs. 10, 12.)*

**JIT** — Joint Investigation Team. Dutch-led criminal investigation of MH17, established 2014; closing statement February 8, 2023. *(Ch. 4.)*

**KEL** — Known Error Log. The Fujitsu-held log of Horizon system errors that the Post Office controlled and withheld from CPIA disclosure across the prosecutorial window. *(Ch. 7.)*

**KNKT** — *Komite Nasional Keselamatan Transportasi*. Indonesian transportation safety committee; investigated Lion Air JT610. *(Ch. 5.)*

**LMArena** — Chatbot Arena, an LLM-ranking benchmark organisation. Revised its submission-disclosure policy after the Llama-4-Maverick incident of April 2025. *(Ch. 9.)*

**MCAS** — Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System. Boeing 737 MAX flight-control software. *(Chs. 3, 5.)*

**MPSB** — Merit Systems Protection Board (U.S.). Reviewable forum for prohibited personnel actions. *(Ch. 8.)*

**NIE** — National Intelligence Estimate. The October 2002 NIE on Iraq WMD is the canonical case discussed. *(Chs. 10, 11.)*

**NSC** — National Security Council (U.S. White House). The PROFS email system, deleted in the Iran-Contra cover-up, supplied the recovered Iran-Contra record from backup. *(Chs. 8, 12.)*

**NS-AufhG** — *Gesetz zur Aufhebung nationalsozialistischer Unrechtsurteile in der Strafrechtspflege* (1998, *BGBl* I 1998, S. 2501). The German Bundestag statute under which the *Generalbundesanwalt* annulled van der Lubbe's conviction. *(Ch. 1.)*

**NTSB** — National Transportation Safety Board (U.S.). Procedural participant under ICAO Annex 13 as state-of-design representative for the 737 MAX investigations. *(Ch. 5.)*

**ODA** — Organization Designation Authorization (U.S. FAA). The delegation framework under FAA Order 8110.4C that lets manufacturer employees act as FAA Authorized Representatives. *(Chs. 2, 5.)*

**OHCHR HRMMU** — Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights, Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine. Assembled record on the Ukrainian-children transfers from outside the Russian-controlled jurisdiction. *(Chs. 10, 12.)*

**OLC** — Office of Legal Counsel (U.S. DOJ). The executive branch's legal opinion shop; opinions bind executive-branch action unless withdrawn. Memoranda discussed include Bybee/Yoo (August 1, 2002), Goldsmith withdrawal (2004), Levin (December 30, 2004), and the al-Aulaqi opinion (2010–11). *(Chs. 2, 8, 10.)*

**OMB** — Office of Management and Budget (U.S.). Implemented the 2019 Ukraine aid hold. *(Ch. 6.)*

**OSCE SMM** — Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe, Special Monitoring Mission to Ukraine. Denied access to Crimea in 2014. *(Ch. 4.)*

**OSP** — Office of Special Plans. Pentagon ad-hoc intelligence-screening cell active in 2002–03. *(Ch. 10.)*

**OVP** — Office of the Vice President (U.S.). *(Ch. 10.)*

**OVV** — *Onderzoeksraad voor Veiligheid*. Dutch Safety Board; MH17 physical analysis (October 13, 2015). *(Ch. 4.)*

**PACE** — Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe. Resolution 1990 (April 10, 2014) on Crimea. *(Ch. 4.)*

**PPSNI** — Public Prosecution Service for Northern Ireland. One of the statutory authorities included in the Inquiry's aggregate Horizon-prosecution count. *(Ch. 7.)*

**PRA** — Prudential Regulation Authority (UK). Operates alongside the FCA under SMCR. *(Ch. 11.)*

**PROFS** — Professional Office System. The NSC's IBM email system, source of the Iran-Contra messages later recovered from backup. *(Ch. 12.)*

**PSI** — Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations (U.S. Senate). *(Ch. 6.)*

***RGBl*** — *Reichsgesetzblatt*. The German legal gazette in which the 1933 Reichstag Fire Decree (*RGBl* I 1933, p. 83), the Enabling Act (*RGBl* I 1933, p. 141), and the *Lex van der Lubbe* (*RGBl* I 1933, Nr. 28, p. 151) were published. *(Ch. 1.)*

**SASC** — Senate Armed Services Committee (U.S.). The Detainee Inquiry of November 20, 2008. *(Chs. 2, 10.)*

**SEC** — Securities and Exchange Commission (U.S.). Forum for SOX § 302 / § 906 certifications. *(Ch. 11.)*

**SMCR** — Senior Managers and Certification Regime (UK). Operative for banks, building societies, and PRA-designated investment firms from 2016; extended in 2018 and 2019. *(Ch. 11.)*

**SOFA** — U.S.–Iraq Status of Forces Agreement (2008). Repealed CPA Order 17 immunity prospectively from January 1, 2009. *(Ch. 4.)*

**SOX** — Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 (Pub. L. 107-204). §§ 302 and 906 attach criminal exposure to the CEO/CFO certification of internal controls. *(Ch. 11.)*

**UCC / UCIL** — Union Carbide Corporation / Union Carbide India Limited. The U.S. parent and Indian subsidiary in the Bhopal case. UCC South Charleston, WV held the methyl-isocyanate design authority; UCC Danbury, CT held parent control. *(Chs. 2, 5.)*

**UNGA** — United Nations General Assembly. Resolution 68/262 (March 27, 2014) on Crimea. *(Ch. 4.)*

**UNSC** — United Nations Security Council. Resolution 2166 (July 21, 2014, unanimous) on MH17; tribunal-draft S/2015/562 vetoed by Russia (July 29, 2015). *(Ch. 4.)*

**USAO-DC** — U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Columbia. Held the Capitol Breach docket; removed the case-tracking dashboard in January 2025. *(Ch. 8.)*

**VRA** — Voting Rights Act § 2 enforcement. The cited statutory authority for the proposed 2020 census citizenship question. *(Ch. 6.)*

**WHIG** — White House Iraq Group. The cross-agency communications cell active in 2002–03 on Iraq WMD messaging. *(Ch. 10.)*
