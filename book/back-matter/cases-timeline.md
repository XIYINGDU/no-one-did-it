# Cases in Chronological Order

A companion to *Cases at a Glance* — the same cases, sorted by date instead of by name. The list is meant to make the historical scope visible at one reading: ritual substitution from the sixth century BCE through the algorithmic alibi of the present decade is the same move at different altars.

Date ranges name the period over which the case operated in the public record (not necessarily the operative window of the laundering itself). Long arcs are placed by their starting date.

---

## Antiquity

**~6th c. BCE.** *Athenian pharmakos.* The annual rite recorded in Hipponax: a marginal person fed at public expense and driven out of the city carrying its pollution. *Ch. 1.*

## Early modern

**1530.** *Tyndale's* Pentateuch. William Tyndale's English translation, printed in Antwerp, coined the noun *scapegoate* in Leviticus 16. The King James translators kept the word in 1611; by 1824 it had migrated into general English use. *Ch. 1.*

## Late nineteenth century

**1894–1906.** *Dreyfus affair.* Captain Alfred Dreyfus convicted on the *bordereau*; Picquart's 1896 identification of Esterhazy; Zola's "J'accuse...!" (1898); Cour de Cassation quashes without remand on 12 July 1906. *Ch. 2.*

## Early twentieth century

**1910s–1970s.** *Stolen Generations.* Australian Commonwealth and state policies of forcible removal of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children from their families. *Bringing Them Home* report, 1997. *Ch. 2.*

**1920–1927.** *Sacco and Vanzetti.* Conviction at Dedham 14 July 1921; execution 23 August 1927. Frankfurter (1927); Lowell Committee (1927); Avrich (1991); Russell (1962, 1986); Dukakis proclamation 23 August 1977. *Ch. 2.*

**1933.** *Reichstag fire and* Lex van der Lubbe. Marinus van der Lubbe sets fire 27 February 1933; Reichstag Fire Decree (28 February); Enabling Act (23 March); *Lex van der Lubbe* of 29 March 1933 retroactively raises arson penalty to death; *Generalbundesanwalt* annulment 6 January 2008. *Ch. 1.*

## Mid- and late twentieth century

**1969–1971.** *Pentagon Papers.* Photocopying late 1969 (Ellsberg, Russo); *New York Times* publication 13 June 1971; *New York Times Co. v. United States*, 403 U.S. 713 (30 June 1971). *Ch. 12.*

**1970–1972.** *Knapp Commission.* Mayor Lindsay's EO No. 11, 21 May 1970; final report 27 December 1972. Serpico shot 3 February 1971. Office of the Special Prosecutor defunded by Governor Cuomo, 1990. *Ch. 2.*

**1971.** Citizens to Preserve Overton Park, Inc. v. Volpe, *401 U.S. 402.* The Administrative Procedure Act "hard look" standard installed. *Ch. 6.*

**1971–1981.** *Ford Pinto.* Production from model-year 1971; in-house crash tests c. 1970–71; *Grimshaw v. Ford Motor Co.* (1981) 119 Cal.App.3d 757. *Ch. 3.*

**1972–1974.** *Watergate.* DNC headquarters break-in 17 June 1972; Saturday Night Massacre 20 October 1973; Nixon resignation 9 August 1974. *Chs. 8, 12.*

**1975.** *Park doctrine.* United States v. Park, *421 U.S. 658 (9 June 1975), affirmed 6–3.* Continuing FDA use for half a century. *Ch. 11.*

**1984.** *Bhopal gas disaster.* Methyl isocyanate release night of 2–3 December 1984 at UCIL Bhopal. Warren Anderson charged in 1984, never tried, died 2014; seven UCIL officials convicted 2010 (two-year sentences). UCC South Charleston and UCC Danbury layers unadjudicated. *Chs. 2, 5.*

**1985–1987.** *Therac-25.* Six overdoses between June 1985 and January 1987 at six U.S. and Canadian medical centres. Voyne Ray Cox dies September 1986; the Leveson-Turner investigation published *IEEE Computer*, July 1993. *Ch. 2.*

**1986–1993.** *Iran-Contra.* Fawn Hall's shredding 21–25 November 1986; deletion of 736 PROFS messages by North and 5,012 by Poindexter, 22–29 November 1986; Tower Commission report February 1987; Walsh Final Report. *Chs. 8, 12.*

## Early 2000s

**1999–2025.** *Horizon (Post Office).* Rollout 1999; private Post Office prosecutions across the United Kingdom; Bates and Others Group Litigation (Fraser J's *Horizon Issues* judgment, 16 December 2019, [2019] EWHC 3408 (QB)); Court of Appeal *Hamilton & Others* judgment (April 2021); Post Office Horizon IT Inquiry Final Report Volume 1, HC 1119 (Sir Wyn Williams, July 2025) records approximately 1,000 prosecuted and convicted on Horizon evidence. *Chs. 2, 7, 12.*

**2002.** *Sarbanes-Oxley §§ 302 and 906.* Enacted 30 July 2002; certifications operative for filings covering periods ending after 14 August 2002. *Ch. 11.*

**2002–2003.** *Iraq WMD National Intelligence Estimate.* October 2002 NIE; Powell at the UN 5 February 2003; SSCI Phase I (2004) and Phase II (2008); Chilcot report 6 July 2016. *Chs. 10, 11.*

**2003–2004.** *Abu Ghraib detainee abuse.* Photographs surface 2004; Taguba (March), Schlesinger and Fay-Jones (August) reports; SASC Inquiry of 20 November 2008. OLC Bybee/Yoo memo of 1 August 2002 above; Sanchez authorisation of 14 September 2003. *Chs. 2, 10.*

**2004.** *Executive Order 13328.* Charters the Robb-Silberman Commission on intelligence-community capacity; scoped to intelligence-community product, not policymaker use. *Ch. 11.*

**2005.** *Inquiries Act 2005.* In force 7 June 2005. Section 21 compulsory-evidence power. *Ch. 11.*

**2005.** *CIA interrogation videotape destruction.* Ordered November 2005; investigated by special counsel John Durham, no charges. *Chs. 2, 12.*

## Mid-2000s through 2010s

**2006–2017.** *Volkswagen Dieselgate.* Defeat-device strategy adopted 2006; CAFEE West Virginia testing 2014; confession to CARB/EPA 3 September 2015; *United States v. Volkswagen AG* plea January 2017; May 2018 indictment of additional executives including former CEO Martin Winterkorn. *Chs. 3, 5.*

**2007–2020.** *Blackwater / Nisour Square.* Killings 16 September 2007; 2014 convictions; pardons by President Trump, 22 December 2020. CPA Order 17 (2004 revision); U.S.–Iraq SOFA (effective 1 January 2009). *Chs. 4, 10.*

**2009–2016.** *Iraq Inquiry (Chilcot).* Statutory inquiry under the Inquiries Act 2005; report 6 July 2016. *Chs. 11, 12.*

**2010–2011.** *al-Aulaqi memorandum.* OLC opinion authorising the September 30, 2011 drone strike that killed Anwar al-Aulaqi, a U.S. citizen, in Yemen. *Ch. 8.*

**2010–2017.** *DeCoster (Quality Egg).* *United States v. DeCoster*, 828 F.3d 626 (8th Cir. 2016), affirms three-month prison sentences under the Park doctrine; Supreme Court denial of certiorari 2017. *Ch. 11.*

**2013–2019.** *UK Senior Managers and Certification Regime.* Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (as amended); Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013; in force 2016 for banks, building societies, and PRA-designated firms; extended 2018–2019. *Ch. 11.*

**2014.** *Crimea annexation.* UN General Assembly Resolution 68/262 (27 March 2014); Council of Europe Venice Commission Opinion 762/2014; ECtHR Grand Chamber admissibility decision (16 December 2020). *Ch. 4.*

**2014–2025.** *MH17.* Destruction over eastern Ukraine on 17 July 2014; Joint Investigation Team identifies the Buk-TELAR of the Russian 53rd Brigade; Hague convictions in absentia 17 November 2022; JIT closing statement 8 February 2023; ICAO Council finding 12 May 2025. *Chs. 4, 7, 10.*

**2017–2018.** *Family separation.* DOJ "zero-tolerance" policy under 8 U.S.C. § 1325; *Ms. L. v. ICE* before Judge Sabraw; Executive Order 13841 of 20 June 2018; DHS OIG and Senate PSI findings on deterrence purpose. *Ch. 6.*

**2018–2019.** *Boeing 737 MAX.* Lion Air JT610 crash 29 October 2018 (189 dead); Ethiopian Airlines ET302 crash 10 March 2019 (157 dead); KNKT (Indonesia) and ECAA (Ethiopia) final reports; House Transportation Committee majority staff report September 2020; 2021 DPA; May 2024 breach determination; May 2025 NPA. *Chs. 3, 5.*

**2018–2019.** *Census citizenship question; Hofeller files.* Furman J findings of pretext 15 January 2019; Hofeller files filed publicly 30 May 2019; *Department of Commerce v. New York*, 588 U.S. ___ (27 June 2019); Commerce withdraws the question July 2019. *Chs. 6, 12.*

**2019–2020.** *Ukraine aid hold.* Trump–Zelenskyy call 25 July 2019; call memorandum released 24 September 2019; Sondland testimony 20 November 2019; House impeachment vote 18 December 2019; GAO Decision B-331564 of 16 January 2020; Senate acquittal 5 February 2020. *Ch. 6.*

## 2020s

**2020–2025.** *Williams Inquiry (Post Office Horizon IT Inquiry).* Statutory inquiry under the Inquiries Act 2005; Final Report Volume 1, HC 1119 (Sir Wyn Williams, published 8 July 2025): approximately 1,000 sub-postmasters prosecuted and convicted across the UK based on Horizon evidence (¶ 3.24); 59 contemplated suicide, 10 attempted, 13 suicides linked, with Williams stating the causal connection could not be ruled out (¶¶ 3.11–3.12). *Chs. 7, 12.*

**2022–.** *Russian transfer of Ukrainian children.* Russian Presidential Decree No. 330 of 30 May 2022 and Decree No. 585 of 11 July 2022; ICC Pre-Trial Chamber II arrest warrants for President Putin and Commissioner Lvova-Belova, 17 March 2023. *Chs. 2, 10.*

**2024–.** Bartz v. Anthropic. *Alsup J summary judgment 23 June 2025; $1.5 billion settlement announced 5 September 2025; fairness hearing 14 May 2026, final approval under submission.* *Ch. 9.*

**2025.** *January 6 prosecutions and the Capitol Breach dashboard removal.* Proclamation 10887 of 20 January 2025; USAO-DC dashboard removed between 24 and 28 January 2025; career line prosecutors file complaints with the Office of Special Counsel and the Merit Systems Protection Board. *Ch. 8.*

**2025.** *Alien Enemies Act invocation.* Proclamation 10903 of 14–15 March 2025. *Ch. 8.*

**2025.** *DOGE records litigation.* *CREW v. U.S. DOGE Service*, No. 1:25-cv-00511 (D.D.C., filed 20 February 2025); *American Oversight v. DOGE et al.* parallel. *Chs. 8, 12.*

**2025.** *Abrego Garcia removal.* Removed in March 2025 against a 2019 immigration-judge withholding order; *Noem v. Abrego Garcia*, 24A949 (10 April 2025, per curiam, unanimous on the holding). *Ch. 8.*

**2025.** Trump v. J.G.G.*, 24A931. *Per curiam Supreme Court order of 7 April 2025: habeas review required before any AEA removal. *Ch. 8.*

**2025.** *GPT-4o sycophancy post-mortem.* Updated GPT-4o released 24–25 April 2025; rollback within ~72 hours; OpenAI post-mortem published 29 April 2025. *Ch. 9.*

**2025.** *Llama-4-Maverick / LMArena.* Meta submits "Llama-4-Maverick-03-26-Experimental" to LMArena; benchmark organisation reforms disclosure policy approximately ten days later. *Ch. 9.*
