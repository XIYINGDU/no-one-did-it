# About the Author

Li Xiaolai (@xiaolai, [lixiaolai.com](https://lixiaolai.com)) is hard to define or categorize cleanly. He graduated from university and has never worked in the field he studied — accounting.

He has been self-employed since college. For seven years he taught at New Oriental Education & Technology Group ([NYSE: EDU](https://www.google.com/finance/quote/EDU:NYSE)), but even there the contract he signed was a service agreement, not an employment one — a contractor's arrangement rather than a staff position.

A bestselling and long-selling author. Over more than twenty years: more than ten titles, cumulative sales above five million copies. Some have been translated into Korean, Japanese, and traditional Chinese.

An investor — best known for long-term Bitcoin holding. Between 2013 and 2019 he was also a prominent angel investor in early-stage projects. He no longer participates in early-stage; his attention sits entirely with secondary markets. He is the designer and strategy-maintainer of the Hong Kong–listed ETFs [3056.HK](https://www.pandofinance.com.hk/en/what-we-offer/etf-3056hk/) and [3112.HK](https://www.pandofinance.com.hk/en/what-we-offer/etf-3112hk/).

A teacher. People address him as *Li Laoshi* — Teacher Li — but he has always thought of himself first as a lifelong student. He learns continuously, picking up whatever new thing seems necessary. He shares only what he has done himself. He maintains a Chinese-language community of more than ten thousand families focused on family education.

A builder. The arrival of capable AI made building at this scale possible for him. He authors and maintains several open-source projects, including [vmark](https://vmark.app), [claudepot](https://claudepot.com), and [nlpm](https://github.com/xiaolai/nlpm-for-claude). The writing of this book leaned heavily on tools he built himself — see [github.com/xiaolai](https://github.com/xiaolai).

A father. He was DINK for years. Now he is the father of more than one child.

A recent word fits well enough: solopreneur.
