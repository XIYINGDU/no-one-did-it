# Chapter Summaries — *No One Did It*

Thirteen summaries follow, each ~200-300 words. Each summary names the anchor case, the laundering mechanism installed or stress-tested, the recognition achieved at the recognition beat, and the capacity carried forward into subsequent chapters.

---

## Part I — The Mechanism

### Chapter 1 — The Altar Moves

**Anchor case.** Berlin, 27 February 1933. A twenty-four-year-old Dutch council communist named Marinus van der Lubbe is arrested inside the burning Reichstag, confessing to setting the fire as a one-man protest. By dawn, four thousand other people in Prussia are under arrest. None of them had been inside the chamber. Within thirty-one hours, an emergency decree suspends seven articles of the Weimar Constitution. The decree remains in operative legal force for the next twelve years.

**Laundering mechanism.** The visible figure on the front page absorbs the moral attention; the decree signed in the same week runs the regime. Chapter 1 is the metaphor-install chapter: civilisation did not stop sacrificing substitutes, it changed the altar. The ancient referent (Leviticus 16; the Athenian *pharmakos*; Roman *Februa*; Babylonian *kuppuru*) is walked at literary depth, with Tyndale's 1530 coinage of "scapegoat" as the etymological bridge that carries the ritual structure into modern English usage.

**Recognition beat.** The recognition arrives as image, not argument: the pattern is older than any case we will analyse, and the modern political case carries the ritual inside its rhythm. The Federal Republic of Germany annulled van der Lubbe's conviction in January 2008 — seventy-five years late, formal, not evidentiary, with no effect on the regime built atop the decree.

**Reader capacity.** Recognise that visible blame and structural responsibility are separable phenomena, and that the separation is older than modernity. Capacity for the analytic chapters that follow.

---

### Chapter 2 — The Four Goats

**Anchor cases.** Four anchor cases install four laundering categories. *Pure scapegoat* — Sacco and Vanzetti (1920-1927), with the Massachusetts trial's documented prejudicial conduct under Judge Thayer and the Lowell Committee's 1927 review; the Russell-revisionist reading is engaged at full strength in the chapter's counterargument beat. *Partial scapegoat* — Abu Ghraib, where the Senate Armed Services Committee inquiry declassified in April 2009 documents the upstream OLC memos, the OSD approvals, and the Sanchez and Miller "Gitmo-ization" recommendations, while seven convicted MPs absorb the prosecutions. *System/object alibi* — Therac-25 (1986-1987) co-anchored with the Boeing 737 MAX (2018-2025) deferred-prosecution sequence. *Cost-bearing goat* — the ICC arrest warrants of 17 March 2023 against Vladimir Putin and Maria Lvova-Belova on charges of unlawful deportation and transfer of Ukrainian children, treated at warrant stage with procedural-stage discipline.

**Laundering mechanism.** Four distinct blame moves, each carrying one anchor case at the installation point and one paired counter-case (Dreyfus; Knapp / Serpico; Horizon / *Bates v Post Office*; the Stolen Generations and the 2008 Australian National Apology) in the chapter's escape beat.

**Recognition beat.** The reader sees that laundering is not one thing the institution does; it is one of four named shapes, each requiring a different diagnostic move to pierce.

**Reader capacity.** Name which of the four shapes is in front of them on a given news story, court filing, corporate apology, or AI incident report.

---

### Chapter 3 — Who Could Have Stopped It?

**Anchor case.** Volkswagen Dieselgate (2015 onward), walked as a hybrid (system/object alibi + deliberate-fraud overlay + partial-scapegoat secondary), paired with the Ford Pinto case of the 1970s walked through the same eight questions.

**Laundering mechanism.** The eight-question diagnostic separates publicly named blame from actual responsibility: who was publicly blamed; who had control; who benefited; who had documented knowledge or should have had it; who could have prevented recurrence; who controlled the record; who bore the cost; what would responsibility look like if it followed control instead of visibility. The instrument is portable across eras, technologies, and regulatory architectures.

**Recognition beat.** The diagnostic is demonstrated by the asymmetry between two interceptions. VW's interception ran through both civil settlement and named-individual criminal convictions (Liang, Schmidt) while leaving the CEO unadjudicated in the United States; Pinto's interception succeeded civilly (*Grimshaw v. Ford Motor Co.*, 1981) and failed criminally (Indiana acquittal, 1980). Same diagnostic. Different escape channels.

**Reader capacity.** Run the eight-question walk in writing on any case, in roughly twenty minutes. The diagnostic is not a recipe; it is a discipline.

## Part II — The Patterns

### Chapter 4 — The Proxy and the Sponsor

**Anchor case.** MH17 (17 July 2014). A Russian 53rd Brigade Buk surface-to-air missile, transported across the Russia-Ukraine border and crewed in coordination with DPR-affiliated personnel, destroys Malaysia Airlines Flight 17 over eastern Ukraine; 298 people are killed. The Dutch-led Joint Investigation Team's September 2016 and June 2019 findings identify the Buk system and three named operators; the Hague convictions in absentia in November 2022; the ICAO Council finding in 2025.

**Laundering mechanism.** The three rungs of the proxy ladder: state-direct (Crimea 2014, unmarked uniforms); state-via-shell (MH17, Russian Buk crewed via a proxy chain); private-contractor (Blackwater at Nisour Square, with the post-conviction pardons treated as the re-laundering move). Three sponsoring entities retain control and benefit; three visible actors are constructed to absorb blame.

**Recognition beat.** Three procedural shells, three different interception outcomes — Crimea's record was fixed externally but the territory remains under sponsor control; MH17's three named operators were convicted but never delivered; the Blackwater convoy was tried, convicted, then pardoned in December 2020.

**Reader capacity.** Move from "knowing what kind of laundering is in front of them" to "recognising the pattern as an institutional method." Recognise that the sponsor's distance from the visible actor is the design, not the accident.

---

### Chapter 5 — The Guilty Goat

**Anchor case.** Bhopal, 2-3 December 1984. A methyl-isocyanate release from the Union Carbide India Limited facility kills at least 3,800 people on the first night, with subsequent deaths and injuries continuing for decades. CEO Warren Anderson is personified as the named actor across a decade of extradition theatre while the parent-corporation chain — design authority at UCC Danbury, the May 1982 audit, the cost-reduction directives, the forum non conveniens dismissal — remains substantively unadjudicated.

**Laundering mechanism.** The partial scapegoat: the named actor is genuinely in the chain, which is precisely what makes the laundering work. Public attention treats "the named actor really did it" as synonymous with "the case is closed." The diagnostic question that matters is whether the chain stopped at that actor or could have climbed.

**Recognition beat.** The Boeing 737 MAX pilot-blame attempt is pierced within five months by two host-state accident-investigation authorities operating under ICAO Annex 13 — and the chain then hits a second laundering layer (the certification-process system/object alibi). Partial-scapegoat layers stack.

**Reader capacity.** When one chain layer is pierced, recognise the next layer waiting. The diagnostic is not a one-application instrument; it walks the chain.

---

### Chapter 6 — The Pretext

**Anchor case.** The 2018 family-separation policy, presented to the public as a routine § 1325 criminal-enforcement decision while contemporaneous record documents the deterrence purpose. Pre-conditioned by the 2014 Obama-era family-detention precursor and the 2017 El Paso pilot. Pierced by *Ms. L. v. ICE* (S.D. Cal., June 2018) as a federal-court class action.

**Laundering mechanism.** The legal/administrative pretext: power recruits a clean statutory or procedural referent to carry a documented alternative purpose. Chapter 6 walks three procedural shells — criminal enforcement (§ 1325), administrative procedure (the 2020 census citizenship question's APA "reasoned explanation"), appropriations (the 2019 Ukraine aid hold's "interagency review").

**Recognition beat.** The same diagnostic question pierces all three: what would the administrative record have to show for the citation to be honest? Family separation was pierced by class action; census-citizenship required SCOTUS APA review plus a posthumous record-disclosure event (the Hofeller hard drives); Ukraine was pierced by the GAO at the appropriations-law level and by impeachment at the constitutional level, but the Senate acquittal preserved the political outcome.

**Reader capacity.** Recognise the pretext form across procedural domains. Pretext-laundering is survived only by mechanism diversity; no single institutional check is sufficient.

---

### Chapter 7 — The Record Is the Battlefield

**Anchor case.** The UK Post Office Horizon scandal (1999-2024). Post Office Ltd holds five conflated roles — complainant, private prosecutor, disclosure controller, audit-trail holder, beneficiary of the legal presumption that the tool's output is reliable — while Fujitsu Services Ltd controls the diagnostic tool itself. The five-role conflation operates for twenty years. More than 900 sub-postmasters and sub-postmistresses are wrongly convicted; multiple suicides documented. Fraser J's December 2019 *Horizon Issues* judgment breaks the disclosure asymmetry; the Court of Appeal's April 2021 *Hamilton & Others v Post Office Ltd* judgment quashes 39 convictions; the Post Office Offences Act 2024 quashes remaining convictions by Act of Parliament.

**Laundering mechanism.** The eight questions only work if you can see the record. The actor that controls the record controls future responsibility. Therac-25 and MH17 return as layered callbacks showing the same record-control mechanism at the artifact layer and the state layer.

**Recognition beat.** The first anti-laundering device is the record. The chapter closes Part II on this rule and translates it into reader-level diagnostics for record-asymmetric situations (algorithmic decision systems; employment arbitration; consumer-credit dispute; EHR access; body-cam control).

**Reader capacity.** Recognise record control as the meta-question that determines whether the diagnostic can be applied at all.

## Part III — The Stress Tests

### Chapter 8 — War Is the Perfect Laundry

**Anchor case.** Iraq WMD (2002-2003 production cycle; Phase I SSCI report 2004; Robb-Silberman Commission 2005; Chilcot Inquiry 2016). The strategic-justification architecture — the Bybee-Yoo OLC torture memoranda (1 August 2002); the Curveball intelligence-source node; the September 2002 NIE; the Powell UN presentation of 5 February 2003 — operates as a system/object alibi with a pure-scapegoat node embedded in the Curveball attribution.

**Laundering mechanism.** War stacks five laundering surfaces from earlier chapters in one operational theatre: chain-of-command secrecy (partial scapegoat, Abu Ghraib); proxy attribution (system/object alibi, MH17); contractor and legal-status shells (system/object alibi, Blackwater); civilian invisibility through euphemism (cost-bearing goat, Ukrainian children); strategic-justification reification (Iraq WMD).

**Recognition beat.** War is not a special domain that defeats the diagnostic; war is the domain where the diagnostic was most needed. The complexity is the design, and the design is what the diagnostic was built to name.

**Reader capacity.** Apply the eight-question walk to compound laundering surfaces where multiple mechanisms fire simultaneously. The diagnostic does not collapse under stack-depth.

---

### Chapter 9 — When Power Calls Itself the Goat

**Anchor case.** The second-term Trump administration's three stacked moves between 20 January 2025 and 10 April 2025: the Alien Enemies Act invocation and the CECOT removal of Kilmar Abrego Garcia (with a 2019 withholding-of-removal order on file); the January 6 pardons and the Department of Justice termination memo for J6 prosecutors; the DOGE / Presidential Records Act litigation. The intercept: *Trump v. J.G.G.* per curiam (7 April 2025) and *Noem v. Abrego Garcia* per curiam (10 April 2025); Judge Cooper's preliminary injunction on DOGE records (March 2025).

**Laundering mechanism.** Reverse-scapegoating. When officials publicly reframe accountability mechanisms — prosecutions, records statutes, judicial review, due-process statutes — as themselves the "laundering" being corrected, the reframing is the laundering. The diagnostic survives the inversion because control, benefit, knowledge, and preventability still point upward; only the rhetoric flipped. Two echoes carry partisan inoculation: Nixon's attempted self-pardon discussion plus the saturday-night-massacre framing; Bush 41's December 1992 Iran-Contra pardons plus the Walsh team's documented records-destruction reconstruction.

**Recognition beat.** The accountability mechanism is not the laundering. Calling the mechanism the laundering is the laundering.

**Reader capacity.** Recognise reverse-scapegoating as a named pattern across administrations. Hold the diagnostic against rhetorical inversion without losing the chain.

---

### Chapter 10 — The Model Did It

**Anchor case.** The AI stack walked at three structurally distinct layers across the April-to-September 2025 documentary window. Input layer: training-data litigation, including the $1.5 billion *Bartz v. Anthropic* settlement (June 2025); *Authors Guild v. OpenAI*; *NYT v. Microsoft*; *Getty v. Stability AI*; California AB-2013 and AB-853. Deployment layer: the GPT-4o sycophancy incident (24-29 April 2025), complete with OpenAI's own published post-mortem using system-agency grammar. Evaluation layer: the Llama 4 / LMArena benchmark controversy (5-11 April 2025), with LMArena's subsequent policy revision making the procedural shell visible.

**Laundering mechanism.** The "model decided" alibi is not a single laundering act. It is a layered architecture in which the same system/object alibi shape recurs three times — at the input layer (human creative work renamed "data"), at the deployment layer (the model's behaviour as the alibi), at the evaluation layer (the benchmark as the named arbiter).

**Recognition beat.** The AI cases are the latest instance of an older pattern, not a separate apocalypse. The algorithm is the newest altar. The diagnostic survives by being run three times per AI system, once at each layer.

**Reader capacity.** Apply the three-layer instrument to the next AI incident report encountered. Recognise system-agency grammar in published post-mortems.

## Part IV — The Anti-Laundering Rules

### Chapter 11 — Make Responsibility Follow Control

**Anchor cases.** Four worked examples of responsibility-follows-control by construction. The Park doctrine (*United States v. Park*, 421 U.S. 658, 1975) names the responsible corporate officer in food-and-drug enforcement. Sarbanes-Oxley §§ 302 and 906 (Pub. L. 107-204, 2002) name the CEO and CFO who must personally certify financial reports under criminal liability. The UK Senior Managers Certification Regime (FCA / PRA, 2016 onward) names every senior function-holder in a regulated firm and requires written allocation of responsibility. The Inquiries Act 2005 §21 gives a public inquiry compulsory-evidence power; the Iraq Inquiry used it to extract Cabinet papers no other UK body had compelled.

**Laundering mechanism (and its counters).** Each design has a counter-example where the rule was fenced — Park-style liability restricted by subsequent rulings; the Boeing DPA without individual prosecution; the Robb-Silberman charter's pre-fenced scope; the opinion-shopped OLC memoranda named in chapter 9. The design is the variable, not the case.

**Recognition beat.** Responsibility-follows-control is not a wish; it is already built into specific statutes, doctrines, charters, and contract clauses. The diagnostic delivers a vocabulary for advocating for the design and recognising when the design is being fenced.

**Reader capacity.** Hold at least one design move applicable to a role the reader actually inhabits — principal of a small organisation, board member, contract negotiator, civic voter on inquiry-statute design.

---

### Chapter 12 — Keep the Record

**Anchor cases.** Four record-survival cases. The Pentagon Papers (Daniel Ellsberg, Neil Sheehan, *New York Times Co. v. United States* 1971). The Walsh team's forensic reconstruction of the Poindexter and North destroyed Iran-Contra notes. The Iraq Inquiry's compulsory-evidence access to Cabinet papers under Inquiries Act 2005 §21. The Pennsylvania Hofeller hard drives in the redistricting record. Each survival turned on a record that outlasted an institution's interest in suppressing, classifying, destroying, or sequestering it.

**Laundering mechanism (and its counters).** Record discipline is not bureaucratic overhead. It is the precondition of every remedy chapters 1 through 10 documented. Three portable record-discipline rules: write it down (decisions and named makers enter the record on the same day, in writing); timestamp and chain-of-custody (contemporaneous notes; chain-of-custody logging; write-once or hash-chained storage where stakes warrant); make destruction visible (destruction events themselves enter the record).

**Recognition beat.** A named responsible party whose decisions leave no documented trace has named responsibility on paper only. The record is the precondition of responsibility-follows-control.

**Reader capacity.** Apply the three rules at work, in board service, in personal documentation of decisions where stakes warrant. Recognise destruction-without-trace as the signature of the laundering chain at work.

---

### Chapter 13 — A Reader's Field Guide

**Anchor cases.** No new anchor cases. Chapter 13 returns the instrument to the reader.

**Laundering mechanism (and its counters).** Six one-page artefacts designed for photocopy: the eight questions; the four-category taxonomy; the AI-stack three-layer diagnostic; the five-layer war-laundering stack; the reader-role-by-domain crosswalk (cost-bearer; bystander; institutional actor with some power); the chapter-by-chapter index of named mechanisms and their counter-cases. The artefacts are operational, not summative — designed to fit into a notebook and be carried into a meeting.

**Recognition beat.** The diagnostic is now portable into the next institutional event we encounter, without returning to the source.

**Reader capacity.** Apply the diagnostic to ourselves as the candidate goat. Ask in writing which named person made the decision. Refuse to sign a procedural disposition that names no individual where individuals had control. Withhold the signature, or sign with a written note recording who else was in the chain. The closing artefacts are portable into Monday morning.
