# Editor Pitch List — *No One Did It*

Six named editors I am confident about as of the date stamped above, ranked by fit with the manuscript's comp set (Snyder / Tooze / Coll / Mayer / Applebaum / Caro) and with the published-state ask (paperback partnership, foreign rights, serious-imprint secondary edition). Each line names the editor, the imprint, and one sentence on why this editor for this book. Where the editor's current placement has shifted recently, the shift is named so the recipient is not misaddressed.

## US

1. **Kristine Puopolo — VP and Editorial Director, Nonfiction, Doubleday (Penguin Random House).** Edited Anne Applebaum's *Gulag* (Pulitzer, 2003) and three further Pulitzer-winning nonfiction works on history, intelligence, and accountability — the exact register *No One Did It* writes in, with a documented appetite for long-horizon comparative nonfiction.

2. **Alex Star — Executive Editor, Farrar, Straus and Giroux.** Has edited three Pulitzer-winning nonfiction titles since 2013 and publishes "nonfiction concerned with current events, history and ideas"; FSG is the natural shelf for a comparative-method work that walks Iraq WMD, Horizon, and the AI stack in one apparatus.

3. **Eamon Dolan — VP and Executive Editor, Simon & Schuster.** Acquired *Fast Food Nation*, *The God Delusion*, and *Moonwalking with Einstein* — a track record on systems-level diagnostics that make institutional plumbing legible without flattening it; a good US fit for the eight-question instrument framing.

4. **Robert Weil — Executive Editor and VP, Liveright (W. W. Norton).** Editor of four Pulitzer-winning nonfiction titles; the Liveright list since the 2012 relaunch has carried exactly the literary-trade register the manuscript opens in (Tyndale, the Reichstag, the *pharmakos*).

## UK

5. **Stuart Proffitt — Publishing Director, Penguin Press UK (Allen Lane).** The UK counterpart to the US Penguin Press list and the standing home for serious comparative nonfiction in the Applebaum / Snyder register; Allen Lane is the natural UK shelf for the manuscript's long-horizon apparatus.

6. **Helen Conford — Publisher, Hutchinson Heinemann (Penguin Random House UK).** Returned to PRH UK in 2021 to lead the merged Hutchinson Heinemann list as publisher; long-standing reputation for upmarket nonfiction with international reach; the UK Horizon chapter and the Chilcot / Inquiries Act material map directly to her list's interests.

## Editors named in older drafts but no longer at the imprint

- **Wendy Wolf** has left Viking and is now Editor-at-Large at Harvard University Press; address through HUP if approaching her.
- **Sara Bershtel** is no longer at Metropolitan Books / Henry Holt (the imprint's staff was laid off in 2022).
- **Clive Priddle** stepped down as publisher of PublicAffairs in 2025 and joined Oxford University Press as Editor-at-Large; address through OUP if approaching him.
