# Comparable Titles — *No One Did It*

Eight comps. Six positive across four positioning axes; one adjacent-domain comp (Bevins); one preempted bad-fit (Pinker) likely to be misread for surface adjacency.

## 1. Timothy Snyder, *On Tyranny: Twenty Lessons from the Twentieth Century* (Tim Duggan / Crown, 2017; 128 pp)
**Shares.** A compressed, durable, portable diagnostic; one rule per chapter; severe nonpartisan voice; historical comparison as evidence.
**Differs.** *On Tyranny* is a pamphlet of distilled lessons; *No One Did It* is a 99,000-word comparative-method work with thirteen case-anchored chapters, a four-category taxonomy, an eight-question diagnostic, and Chicago-NB apparatus.

## 2. Adam Tooze, *Crashed: How a Decade of Financial Crises Changed the World* (Viking / Penguin Press, 2018; 706 pp)
**Shares.** Institutional plumbing made legible without losing precision; scholarly-discipline tier; multi-state, multi-year comparative reconstruction.
**Differs.** *Crashed* takes one domain (global finance) and walks it deep over a decade; *No One Did It* takes one mechanism (responsibility laundering) and walks it across four shapes over twelve documentary chapters from Leviticus 16 to *Bartz v. Anthropic* (June 2025).

## 3. Steve Coll, *Directorate S: The C.I.A. and America's Secret Wars in Afghanistan and Pakistan, 2001–2016* (Penguin Press, 2018; 784 pp)
**Shares.** Long-horizon, named-actor, public-record discipline against actors with the resources to defend themselves; declassified-cable-tier sourcing.
**Differs.** Coll is single-region, single-period; *No One Did It* is explicitly comparative across centuries, regions, and laundering shapes — the structural argument requires Bhopal, Boeing, and the GPT-4o sycophancy incident to sit in the same analytic frame.

## 4. Jane Mayer, *Dark Money: The Hidden History of the Billionaires Behind the Rise of the Radical Right* (Doubleday, 2016; 464 pp)
**Shares.** Chain-climbing past the visible actor; documented architecture of named individuals operating behind procedural shells; public-record discipline against well-resourced subjects.
**Differs.** *Dark Money* walks one network across one ideological project; *No One Did It* walks the mechanism itself across politically, technologically, and historically distant cases.

## 5. Anne Applebaum, *Twilight of Democracy: The Seductive Lure of Authoritarianism* (Doubleday, 2020; 224 pp)
**Shares.** Severe nonpartisan voice; analyst inside the analysis; refusal to flatter any tribal alignment.
**Differs.** Applebaum is interpretive and personal-essayistic; *No One Did It* is apparatus-driven — a named taxonomy and a portable eight-question instrument.

## 6. Robert A. Caro, *The Power Broker: Robert Moses and the Fall of New York* (Knopf, 1974; 1,344 pp)
**Shares.** Long-horizon institutional reconstruction; refusal to let the named actor stand alone where the chain extends behind him.
**Differs.** Caro takes one figure across one career; *No One Did It* takes the chain mechanism itself across centuries — narrower than Caro on single-case biographical depth, broader on structural sweep.

## 7. Vincent Bevins, *The Jakarta Method: Washington's Anticommunist Crusade & the Mass Murder Program That Shaped Our World* (PublicAffairs, 2020; 320 pp)
**Shares.** Comparative-method work across countries treating a mechanism (here, a US-coordinated programme) as the unit of analysis; primary-document and survivor-record discipline.
**Differs.** Bevins names a specific historical project; *No One Did It* names a structural mechanism continuous across regimes.

## Preempted bad-fit — Steven Pinker, *The Better Angels of Our Nature* (Viking, 2011; 832 pp)
**Why an editor will raise it.** Long-horizon comparative-violence treatment with ancient-rite opening.
**Why it is wrong.** *Better Angels* is a meta-historical optimism argument; *No One Did It* is mechanism-continuity. The institutional developments Pinker celebrates as progress (legal-administrative procedure, corporate form, regulatory delegation, algorithmic decision) are the same instruments inside which the laundering now operates.
