# Query — *No One Did It: Responsibility Laundering, from the Scapegoat to the Algorithm*

Dear [Agent / Editor],

A plane is in the sea, and the first explanation is the crew. A village postmistress is in prison on the say-so of accounting software she was told no one else had questioned. Hundreds had. A child taken at a border cannot be matched back to a parent because the record that would have matched them was never built. The harm is real. The cost is real. And when we ask who decided, who could have stopped it, we find the same modern emptiness: the pilots erred, the system flagged it, procedures were followed, the model decided. No one did it.

*No One Did It* names that move — **responsibility laundering** — and hands over an instrument to take it apart. The argument: civilisation did not stop sacrificing substitutes when it stopped sacrificing goats. It moved the altar — from the temple to the org chart, from the org chart to the legal entity, from the legal entity to the algorithm — and at each move the part where someone announces "this one will carry it for us" went further underground. Thirteen chapters install a four-category taxonomy (pure scapegoat, partial scapegoat, system or object alibi, cost-bearing goat), an eight-question diagnostic separating control from visibility, and a twelve-figure visual apparatus that walks the diagnostic across the Reichstag Fire, Bhopal, Boeing 737 MAX, Abu Ghraib, the UK Post Office Horizon scandal, Iraq WMD, MH17, Crimea, the 2018 family separation policy, the 2025 Alien Enemies Act invocation, and the AI stack at training, deployment, and evaluation. It closes with a six-artefact field guide.

The manuscript is complete at ~99,000 words and published by XIAOLAI BOOKS as a first edition (ISBN 978-1-80826-002-5) in 2026; the file ships as a Kindle/KDP-conforming EPUB 3 with full Chicago Manual of Style 17th edition Notes-Bibliography apparatus and 283 verified source-ledger cards. I am writing about partnership for a trade paperback, foreign rights, and a serious-press secondary edition — the work has cleared its own legal and evidence gates and is ready to enter a larger conversation.

Comp-wise, the work sits in the Snyder / Tooze / Coll / Applebaum / Mayer register — a portable diagnostic in Snyder's *On Tyranny* mode, applied with Tooze's institutional precision and Mayer's chain-climbing discipline. It is comparative across centuries and procedural domains rather than confined to one. Among AI titles it differs from O'Neil and Crawford: the algorithm is treated as the newest altar, not a separate apocalypse.

The reason to publish this now: "the model decided" is arriving, this decade, in the rooms that decide the loan, the job, the diagnosis, the benefit. The oldest move power knows has acquired its most modern instrument, and the two are converging fast.

Two chapters are attached — chapter 3 ("Who Could Have Stopped It?"), which installs the diagnostic on Volkswagen Dieselgate and the Ford Pinto, and chapter 10 ("The Model Did It"), which stress-tests it on the AI stack. I would welcome the chance to send the full manuscript.

With regards,
Xiaolai Li
