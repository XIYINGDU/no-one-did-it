# Overview — *No One Did It*

## The thesis

Civilisation did not stop sacrificing substitutes when it stopped sacrificing goats. It changed the altar — from temple to org chart, from org chart to legal entity, from legal entity to algorithm — and at each move the part where someone announces "this one will carry it for us" went further underground. *No One Did It* names that move — **responsibility laundering**: the recurring institutional method by which power keeps control and benefit while moving blame, liability, moral cost, and public suspicion onto a weaker bearer (a junior employee, a proxy, a contractor, a machine, a market, a committee, a procedure, a record). Thirteen chapters install the diagnostic, walk it across cases, and hand it over as a portable instrument.

## The structure

**Part I — The Mechanism (chs 1–3)** installs four shapes the laundering takes (the **four-category taxonomy**) — *pure scapegoat* (Reichstag Fire; Sacco-Vanzetti against the Russell counter-reading), *partial scapegoat* (Abu Ghraib's seven convicted MPs against the OLC/OSD chain), *system or object alibi* (Therac-25; Boeing 737 MAX), *cost-bearing goat* (Ukrainian children under the 17 March 2023 ICC warrants) — and the **eight-question diagnostic** that separates publicly named blame from actual responsibility: who was blamed; who had control; who benefited; who had documented knowledge or should have had it; who could have prevented it; who held the record; who bore the cost; what responsibility would look like if it followed control instead of visibility. Chapter 3 walks the eight questions through Volkswagen Dieselgate and the Ford Pinto.

**Part II — The Patterns (chs 4–7)** walks four laundering mechanisms across centuries and procedural domains: the proxy and the sponsor (Crimea, MH17, Blackwater); the partial scapegoat as design (Bhopal, Boeing 737 MAX); the legal-administrative pretext (the 2018 family separation policy, the 2020 census citizenship question, the 2019 Ukraine aid hold); and the record as battlefield (the UK Post Office Horizon scandal — 900+ wrongful convictions, resolved by an unprecedented Act of Parliament in 2024).

**Part III — The Stress Tests (chs 8–10)** holds the diagnostic against contested modern terrain: war (Iraq WMD as spine, Abu Ghraib + MH17 + Blackwater + Ukrainian children as the five-layer stack); reverse-scapegoating in the second-term Trump administration (Alien Enemies Act, the January 6 pardons, DOGE/records litigation, intercepted by the April 2025 SCOTUS per curiams); and the AI stack at three layers — training-data (*Bartz v. Anthropic*, $1.5 bn settlement), deployment (GPT-4o sycophancy), evaluation (Llama 4 / LMArena).

**Part IV — The Anti-Laundering Rules (chs 11–13)** turns the diagnostic into design: statutes that already embed responsibility-follows-control (the Park doctrine, Sarbanes-Oxley §§ 302/906, the UK Senior Managers Regime, the Inquiries Act 2005 §21); three portable record-discipline rules (write it down; timestamp and chain-of-custody; make destruction visible); and a six-artefact field guide.

Twelve named figures (one per documentary chapter) carry the visual apparatus through every Part.

## Why now

"The model decided" is arriving, this decade, in the rooms that decide the loan, the job, the diagnosis, the benefit. The oldest move power knows has acquired its most modern instrument, and the two are converging fast.

## Why this author

Xiaolai Li wrote under the same forensic discipline the manuscript demands of the institutions it analyses: 283 verified source-ledger cards across twelve documentary chapters, every claim graded A/B/C/D, every named living individual described in procedural-stage language (charged, indicted, settled, convicted — never adjudicative shorthand), and a complete Chicago Manual of Style 17th edition Notes-Bibliography apparatus. The discipline the manuscript demands externally is applied to itself.
