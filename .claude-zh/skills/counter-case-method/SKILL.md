# 反方案例方法

<!-- GENERATED:five-over-rules:start -->
1. **Evidence before elegance.** Never improve the story by weakening the evidence.
2. **Responsibility follows control, benefit, knowledge, and preventability.** Do not stop at the most visible actor.
3. **Keep the taxonomy intact.** Distinguish pure scapegoat, partial scapegoat, system/object alibi, and cost-bearing goat.
4. **Steelman before judgment.** Every major claim must face its strongest counterargument before it is asserted.
5. **Handoff cleanly.** Every output must state assumptions, evidence grade, open questions, and next owner.
<!-- GENERATED:five-over-rules:end -->

## 目的

为书中每个替罪羊案例找到一个类似洗涤模式的反方案例——其中羊逃脱了、被释放了或托辞被刺穿了。反方案例将书从诊断转化为工作手册：它展示模式是可打破的，并指明打破它的机制。反方案例**不是**好结局；它是一个有记录的洗涤企图的拦截。

## 四种拦截类型

1. 被昭雪的纯粹替罪羊。2. 归责爬升。3. 系统/对象托辞被刺穿。4. 承受代价的羊得到补偿或恢复。仅因运气逃脱不算反方案例——逃脱机制必须可命名、可转移。

## 案例文件结构

```text
Counter-case name: / Paired scapegoat case:
Laundering attempt: / Pattern / Goat-candidate / Alibi
Interception mechanism: / Type / Named institutional actor / Key act
Cost of escape: / Time / Cost to goat / Cost to cost-bearers / Cost to interceptor
Outcome: Full/partial reversal / recognition without remedy
Transferable lesson (one sentence, diagnostic not motivational):
Limits of the lesson:
Evidence grade: / Handoff owner:
```

规则：不浪漫化拦截行动者——机制是教训而非英雄主义。不将运气或无关政权更迭带来的逆转声称为反方案例。诚实标记缺失的反方案例——"此模式在此时期无有记录的逃脱"是该书的承重发现。反方案例需与其配对的替罪羊案例来自相同的洗涤模式。
