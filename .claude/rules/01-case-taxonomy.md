**Classify every case into exactly one of the four taxonomy categories; explicit mixing is the only allowed alternative.**

# Case Taxonomy Rule

Use these four categories consistently.

## Pure scapegoat
The blamed actor is substantially innocent or unrelated, and the blame assignment protects another actor or system.

## Partial scapegoat
The blamed actor is culpable or involved, but the attribution stops too low in the chain and prevents responsibility from climbing to designers, commanders, executives, or beneficiaries.

## System/object alibi
A tool, machine, algorithm, benchmark, corporation, legal entity, committee, market, or procedure becomes the named cause.

## Cost-bearing goat
A person or group absorbs harm, punishment, exile, reputational damage, bodily risk, or economic cost without being the real responsibility-holder.

Never mix these categories without saying so explicitly.

## Why this rule exists

The book's argument collapses if every case becomes "complicated." The taxonomy makes the differences between an innocent substitute, a too-low-stop, an institutional blame container, and a cost-bearing victim load-bearing rather than rhetorical. Case files that fuzz the category make later chapters fuzz the conclusion.
