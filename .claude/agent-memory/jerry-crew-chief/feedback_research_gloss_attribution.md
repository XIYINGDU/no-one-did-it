**Rule.** Where two numbers, two doctrinal categories, or two parallel-looking source statements are close enough to gloss together, the gloss must be **quoted-and-attributed** ("the gravity-of-the-crime argument supports the view that...") rather than asserted as the source's own language ("Article X deems...").

**Why:** Two distinct verification-cell findings on 2026-05-25 (Stephen on chapter-2 case files, post-Alan revisions + Boeing first-pass) showed the same structural error shape — researchers collapsing near-adjacent items into a single primary-source claim:

1. **Shirley, `boeing-737-max.md`:** conflated the $444.5M victim-family fund with the ~$445M compliance/safety/quality investment. Two separate NPA line items with similar dollar amounts written up as one figure ("$445M additional to victim families") — wrong on both counts. The fund and the compliance investment are independent.
2. **Selina, `abu-ghraib.md` (doctrinal additions):** wrote "Rome Statute Article 33(2) deems orders to commit torture per se manifestly unlawful." Art 33(2)'s text only names *genocide and crimes against humanity*. The torture extension is a defensible scholarly inference (CAT + GC Common Art 3 + Rome Statute Art 8) but is **not what Art 33(2) says**. The synthesis was written as the source's own language.

Both errors are exactly what rules `05-overclaim-language.md` and `06-quote-integrity.md` already forbid — the gap is application at the *researcher-drafting* stage rather than at *fact-checker-verification*. Stephen catches these, but his catches are after-the-fact; prevention should land upstream at researcher dispatch.

**How to apply:** When drafting case-file claims that touch two adjacent doctrinal categories or two close-looking numbers, treat the close-but-not-identical pair as a tripwire: name both items separately, attribute any synthesis to its scholarly origin, never let the synthesis read as the primary source's own language. At researcher-dispatch time (Delon → Shirley/Selina/Warren/Loki), flag this pattern when the brief touches NPA/DPA financial schedules, statutory article numbering with parallel paragraphs, or doctrinal categories that are conceptually adjacent (torture vs CAH, recklessness vs intent, settlement-admitted vs allegation).
