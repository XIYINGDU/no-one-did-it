# Part III sequencing — AUTHORIZED 2026-05-25

## Decision

**AUTHORIZED.** Part III informal reading order is **ch-9 → ch-10 → ch-8** for the purposes of chapter-brief drafting and intra-act cross-references. See [[project-part3-sequencing]] for Bonnie's full reasoning.

## Scope (what this decision binds)

- Bonnie's chapter briefs for ch-8/9/10 are written assuming this order. Cross-references and "in the next/previous chapter…" copy follow 9 → 10 → 8.
- Wayne drafts ch-8/9/10 with the same assumed reader-experience order. Any "as we saw in ch-X" callbacks must check against this sequence.
- Reader-value (beat 10) and counter-case (beat 9) copy in each chapter assumes the reader arrives with the diagnostic confidence built by the prior two stress tests.

## Scope (what this decision does NOT bind)

- `book/toc.yml` stays `parallel_chapters: true`. Structural parallelism is preserved; this is a reader-guidance recommendation, not a DAG change. Print-order will be a separate Blair/Bonnie call closer to proposal-pack time.
- The chapter file IDs stay numerically 8/9/10. No renumbering.
- If Stephen, Laura, or Nancy returns a finding that materially changes any of the three chapters' evidence weight (e.g., Ukrainian-children case downgraded by Nancy's legal review, or AI-model case anchored by a breaking adjudication), the order is re-openable. Bonnie owns the trigger.

## Reasoning

Bonnie's three-step ramp (institutional → technical-alibi → war/atrocity) maps cleanly onto Part III's reader-state objective (S2 → S2.5 PATTERN_STRESSED). The diagnostic gets cheapest-failure-cost test first (ch-9, lower legal heat, institutional self-blame as the laundering vector), inherits the artifact-alibi lesson from ch-2 Boeing into ch-10 (model-as-alibi reads as the lineal descendant of Therac-25 and Boeing's MCAS-the-system), then lands ch-8 last where Ukrainian-children's legal-discipline bar can rest on a reader whose diagnostic is fully proven rather than being asked to ratify the diagnostic on hardest terrain. The Abu Ghraib double-duty observation (ch-2 anchor + ch-8 primary) is a real narrative-weight problem; placing ch-8 last lets ch-2's Abu Ghraib treatment cool before ch-8 picks it up.

## Evidence grade for the decision

B. The reasoning is structurally sound and consistent with the spine's reader-state ladder, but it has not been pressure-tested against an actual chapter-2 prose draft or against a finished ch-8 counter-case search. If the counter-case for ch-8 fails to land (the "escape" beat is structurally non-negotiable), the order may need revisiting.

## Open questions

- Does the proposal-pack audience strategy (Blair, not yet started) prefer AI-first (10 → 9 → 8 or 10 → 8 → 9) for marketability? Flagged for Blair when she opens proposal work; this decision is revisable on her finding.
- Print order vs reading-order: if the publisher requires numerical print order, the cross-reference copy still follows 9 → 10 → 8 as the *recommended reading path*, with an explicit reader's note in part-III's opening.

## Why

Bonnie's call is right: the diagnostic needs to be tested in ascending order of legal and emotional heat, and the Abu Ghraib double-duty constraint is real. Authorizing keeps the chapter briefs internally coherent without locking the structural parallelism that the toc preserves for late-stage publisher flexibility.

## How to apply

When Bonnie next opens ch-8/9/10 chapter briefs (or when Wayne next drafts any of the three), this decision is the operative guidance. Reference this memo and `[[project-part3-sequencing]]` in the brief's frontmatter `assumptions` field.
