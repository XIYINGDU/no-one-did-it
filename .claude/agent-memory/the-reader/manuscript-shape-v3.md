The publication form (`dist/manuscript-v3.md`, and the later `no-one-did-it-v4.md`, ~2460 lines of prose + endnotes) is read cold. As a reader encounters it:

- **Preface** (`book/front-matter/preface.md`) — opens on three live objects: a plane in the sea (MH17), the postmistress in a cell (Horizon), a child who cannot be matched to a parent (Ukraine transfers). Names "responsibility laundering," the scapegoat/altar image, the eight questions + four shapes, the bureaucracy-as-altar frame, the "one day the only name on the document could be ours" hook, the both-parties / not-American disclaimer, and the AI ("the model decided") urgency. Promises the book will move: see the move → take it apart → design rules → field guide. Promises by the last page we cannot hear "no one did it" without asking who built the sentence.
- **Ch 1 The Altar Moves** — Reichstag fire / van der Lubbe + Leviticus/Athens/Babylon/Rome scapegoat etymology; installs the recognition (altar moves, substitution doesn't), no diagnostic yet. Ends with "If our face is the public face of a decision and we cannot name who authorised it, we are at the altar."
- **Ch 2 The Four Goats** — installs taxonomy: system/object alibi (Therac-25, Boeing 737 MAX — installed FIRST), pure scapegoat (Sacco/Vanzetti), partial (Abu Ghraib), cost-bearing goat (Ukrainian children/Decree 330). Four paired counter-cases (Dreyfus, Serpico/Knapp, Horizon, Stolen Generations). Ends with full Recognise/Diagnose/Act/Avoid block.
- **Ch 3 Who Could Have Stopped It?** — installs the EIGHT QUESTIONS diagnostic (VW Dieselgate + Ford Pinto + Schwartz objection + five-channel matrix).
- **Part II (4–7):** proxy/sponsor (Crimea/MH17/Blackwater + 3 questions + seven-channel matrix), guilty goat (Bhopal/Boeing partial scapegoat), pretext (family separation/census/Ukraine aid + 6 questions + Overton Park lineage), record-control (Horizon five-role conflation; "the record is first" / "first anti-laundering device is the record").
- **Part III (8–10):** power calling itself the goat (2025 proclamations: J6 pardons / DOGE / Alien Enemies Act–Abrego Garcia; Watergate/Iran-Contra/al-Aulaqi echoes), the model did it (AI three-layer: input/deployment/evaluation; Bartz/GPT-4o/Llama4-LMArena), war (five-layer stack; Iraq WMD anchor + Chilcot).
- **Part IV (11–12):** design rule (Park/SOX/SMCR/Inquiries Act, responsibility-follows-control + defensive-compliance steelman), record discipline (write it down / chain-of-custody / make destruction visible; Pentagon Papers / Iran-Contra PROFS tapes / Hofeller).
- **Ch 13 A Reader's Field Guide** — consolidation pass: 6 artefacts (8 questions, 4 categories, AI 3-layer, war 5-layer, role-by-domain crosswalk [8 roles × 6 moves], by-chapter index). Closes with second-person "algorithmic denial letter" walk and "We are the escape." Final line is a placeholder: `*[FINAL SENTENCE — xiaolai authors]*`.

**v4 chapter-close variation (changed from v3):** rule 04 was amended for v4 to break the uniform ten-beat close that read as monotonous across the middle. v4 does NOT stamp the identical Recognise/Diagnose/Act/Avoid four-part block on every chapter. Instead the reader-applicability beat varies: ch-2 uses the full role-walk; chs 3–12 mostly fold it into a 1–2 paragraph "what this might mean for us" landing keyed to that chapter (workplace investigation, algorithmic denial, the signature-as-seam). The comprehensive role-by-domain matrix lives once, in ch-13's crosswalk. The "signature is the seam" hammer-line and the three-role split (cost-bearer / bystander / institutional actor) still recur but are no longer a rigid stamped block.

**Recurring devices the reader meets repeatedly (by design, pedagogy):** the eight questions (installed ch-3, re-walked or referenced chs 5–13); "three official stories in sequence, each pierced before the next"; the channel/interception MATRIX table (appears ch-3, ch-4, ch-5, ch-6, ch-7, and as artefacts in ch-13 — at least 6 times); "the alibi climbs the stack" / "what is on the floor above"; "the record is first"; counter-case + "cost of escape" + "limit of the lesson" triplet; callbacks to Therac-25 / MH17 / Horizon / Boeing across multiple chapters.

**Back matter:** after ch-13's final-line placeholder comes `# Notes` (Chicago endnotes grouped by chapter, footnote-reference style `[^N]`) then a Selected Bibliography. A reader flips to these; does not read in sequence. Most endnotes carry archive URLs + "(verified ...)" annotations; a handful still carry visible `[CITE: slug — pending-stephen-lock]` workflow text in chapter prose (e.g. ch-8 DOJ Capitol Breach dashboard).
