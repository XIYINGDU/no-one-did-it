A cold reader flagged (2026-05-28) that every administration case in the book — historical echoes and contemporary anchors — is from a Republican administration. Verified against `dev-docs/.../master_case_matrix.csv`: all admin cases are Trump I / Trump II.

The sharper version of the problem: **Chapter 8 ("When Power Calls Itself the Goat") builds its entire partisan-inoculation strategy on two echoes the brief calls "cross-administration / cross-party" — Watergate (Nixon) and Iran-Contra (Reagan/Bush 41). Both are Republican.** They are cross-era, not cross-party. Ch-8 beat 10 asserts the diagnostic is "administration-agnostic"; with no Democratic case cited, a skeptical reader reads that as an unredeemed promise.

**Why:** The book's own over-rules (2: don't stop at the most visible actor; 4: steelman before judgment) turn on the author here — the rhetorically convenient case set is also the partisan-skewed one.

**How to apply:** When asked for any new administration case, prefer Democratic-administration candidates that fit an *existing* book pattern, graded honestly. Do NOT manufacture false equivalence — an honestly-graded B beats an inflated A because the book's authority is its grading discipline (rule 02). The fix is structural-pattern identity across administrations, not severity-matching. See [[project-cross-valence-candidate-packet]].
