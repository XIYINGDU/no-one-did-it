Chapter 3 installs the eight-question diagnostic on the reader. Its case files are not narrative anchors — they are the structural spine. Each case file must:

1. Fill all six chain dimensions (control, benefit, knowledge, preventability, record control, cost bearer) with one declarative paragraph each, primary-source supported.
2. Document the internal interception mechanism (the piercing artifact) inside the case at the same depth as a paired counter-case file, because chapter-3's beat 9 may use the in-case interception rather than a separate counter-case.
3. Add the seventh "What would responsibility look like if it followed control instead of visibility?" framing as a synthesis line at the end of the chain.

**Why:** chapter 3 is the diagnostic-installation chapter; if any dimension is hand-waved, the diagnostic doesn't land and chapters 4-13 lose their toolkit.

**How to apply:** when Delon dispatches a case for chapter 3 or any chapter that walks the eight-question diagnostic explicitly, treat the six dimensions as load-bearing rather than scene-supporting. Format as named subheadings inside `Actual responsibility chain:`.

Primary demonstrator (VW Dieselgate, 2026-05-25 build) is the cleanest such case because every dimension has documented public-record answers — EPA NoV, ICCT-WVU study, DOJ plea, indictments, internal Jones Day work product. Older-echo (Ford Pinto) carries the same structure with looser primary-source coverage on the internal memo's authorship chain — flag in open questions.
