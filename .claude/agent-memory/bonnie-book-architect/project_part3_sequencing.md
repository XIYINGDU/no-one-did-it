# Part III stress-test ordering — observation 2026-05-25

## Observation

Part III ("The Stress Tests", chapters 8–10) is currently `parallel_chapters: true` in `book/toc.yml`. Two facts surfaced while reviewing the ch-2 anchor case files:

1. **Abu Ghraib is doing double duty.** It is the partial-scapegoat anchor for ch-2 *and* the obvious primary case for ch-8 ("War is the perfect laundry"). Cross-act reuse is fine — patterns repeat — but ch-8's S1→S2.5 stress-test work needs a *different* case carrying the weight, with Abu Ghraib as motif/callback, not headline.
2. **Ukrainian-children is live and Nancy-gated.** Putting it at the front of part III sets the highest legal-discipline bar early in the act, which the reader's diagnostic isn't fully ready for.

## Recommendation

Reading-order recommendation (informal, not strict): **9 → 10 → 8.**

- Ch-9 ("When power calls itself the goat") — easiest stress test, institutional/lower-legal-heat, builds reader confidence in the diagnostic against contested terrain.
- Ch-10 ("The Model Did It") — technical alibi stress, references Bhopal (now in ch-5) as historical ancestor.
- Ch-8 ("War is the perfect laundry") — hardest stress test, highest legal heat, last in the act so the reader hits it after two successful diagnostic stress tests.

## Status

**Not authorized by me** — this is a recommendation to `jerry-crew-chief`, since cross-act sequence is his routing call, not mine. Flagged in the ch-2 anchor decision handoff to xaiolai.

**Why:** Part III `parallel_chapters: true` says any order works in principle. But narrative ramp matters: easy → medium → hard stress tests build reader confidence; the reverse order risks reader exit at ch-8 before the diagnostic is fully proven.

**How to apply:** When drafting ch-8, ch-9, ch-10 briefs, assume the recommended reading order above unless Jerry overrides. Cross-references between the three chapters should be written as if 9 precedes 10 precedes 8 in the reader's experience.
