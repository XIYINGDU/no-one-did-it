Chapter 4 ("The Proxy and the Sponsor"), Part II opener, built 2026-05-25
using Crimea (state-direct) + MH17 (state-via-shell, anchor) + Blackwater
(private-contractor). Three structural calls worth keeping:

1. **Analytical anchor ≠ chronological opener when interception strength
   varies.** MH17 is the anchor (carries hidden architecture in beat 3;
   strongest documented interception: JIT + Hague + ECtHR + ICAO).
   Crimea opens beats 1–2 because the unmarked-uniform image is the
   chapter's master visual and re-instantiates on the Buk convoy and the
   contractor protective detail. Blackwater closes beat 9 because the
   pardon is the chapter's clearest re-laundering move. Splitting
   "analytical anchor" from "visual opener" prevented the chapter from
   leading with its weakest interception.

2. **Pre-draft Nancy gate is a separate gate.** With four live HIGH-
   defamation subjects (Putin × 2 cases, Erik Prince, Trump), Nancy's
   sweep is a precondition for promotion to `status: draft`, not a
   downstream audit. Wayne's prose choices in beats 1–9 depend on
   Nancy-cleared phrasing being in hand. Distinguish from chapter 3's
   single-gate Nancy pass.

3. **Mechanistic vs moral equivalence is the partisan-symmetry defense.**
   Two Russian state cases + one US private-contractor case looks like
   false equivalence; the chapter survives the critique by drawing
   *mechanistic* equivalence (same control-benefit-knowledge shape) and
   *refusing* moral equivalence. The distinction must hold in language
   the reader can audit, especially in beats 4 (echo) and 6
   (counterargument). Laura attacks this directly at red-team.

**Why:** Part II's job is to push S1.5 (diagnostic-operating) → S2
(pattern-recognized as institutional method). A three-case ladder
makes the pattern visible in one chapter; weaker structural choices
(parallel panels, single-case anchor with two echoes, chronological
ordering) all leak the S2 transition.

**How to apply:** Subsequent Part II chapters (5, 6, 7) likely face
similar choices when running stress-tests on a mechanism. Default to:
analytical anchor where evidence + interception is strongest; visual
opener wherever the master image is sharpest; closing case where the
re-laundering / reversal move is documented. Pre-draft Nancy gate
whenever ≥2 living subjects sit at HIGH defamation surface.
