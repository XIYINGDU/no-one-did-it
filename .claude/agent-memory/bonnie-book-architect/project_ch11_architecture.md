# Chapter 11 architecture — Part IV opener (2026-05-26)

**Design rationale:** ch-11 is the first argumentative chapter of the book (no scapegoat anchor; no new case file). It turns the diagnostic + four-category taxonomy into portable design rules. The chapter's load-bearing teaching device is the design-asymmetry matrix in beat 7, not a case walk.

## Four worked examples (chosen; alternatives held out)

| Layer | Worked example | Why this and not the alternative |
|---|---|---|
| US corporate criminal | *United States v. Park*, 421 U.S. 658 (1975) | responsible-corporate-officer doctrine still operative; FDA enforcement record A-grade |
| US financial certification | SOX §§ 302 (15 U.S.C. § 7241) + 906 (18 U.S.C. § 1350) | clean statutory text; Stephen-anchor by codified-section |
| UK regulatory duty | SMCR — SMR layer specifically (FSMA s.66A(5); FCA SYSC 4.7 + SYSC 24) | three-pillar regime (SMR / Certification / Conduct Rules) named in beat 3 per Alan; design-rule operates at SMR layer only |
| Inquiry charter | Inquiries Act 2005 §21 vs EO 13328 | UK statute vs US executive order; compulsion-set-by-Parliament vs charter-fenced-by-Minister contrast |

**Cases held out:** ch-4's MH17 / Blackwater / Ukrainian-children callbacks and ch-10's AI training-data / GPT-4o sycophancy / Llama-4 LMArena content do not map cleanly onto the four design moves. Including them would have forced either a fifth design move (the AIA / EU AI Act, deferred to ch-13) or a strained mapping.

## Counter-positions installed at full strength (Laura HARD-gate compliance)

1. **Libertarian / deadweight (Manne / Easterbrook / Becker)** — beat 6 paragraph 1. Most prominent VW prosecutions reached under existing fraud/conspiracy statutes, not Park-style or SOX-style certification. The chapter concedes the empirical and narrows: the design rule is for or against laundering, not for or against regulation.
2. **Regulatory capture (Stigler / Kolko)** — beat 6 paragraph 2 (added in Laura HARD-gate revision). Named-attribution regimes get colonised; the certifying officer becomes the firm's compliance theatre lead. Chapter's response: the design produces named-attribution as a precondition, which a captured regulator can still betray. Added cell in beat 9's "cost of escape" panel acknowledging capture risk.
3. **Survivorship bias** — paragraph after beat 7 matrix titled "How often each design fires." Park enforcement rare; SOX § 906 convictions in low single digits over 24 years; SMCR Final Notices below regulator expectations; Inquiries Act §21 used selectively. Chapter holds that the design is the precondition of named accountability, not its guarantee. `[EVIDENCE NEEDED — Stephen anchor]` on the operating-frequency numbers.
4. **Displacement landing sites (Laura SOFT axis 6)** — beat 7 / beat 9. SOX displacement landed in ratings-agency models + structured-product opacity + compliance-industry bloat; SMCR displacement landed in non-bank shadow finance + certification-function bloat. `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`.
