# Downstream pure-scapegoat anchor gate

**Set:** 2026-05-25, after `book/chapters/02-the-four-goats.md` reached `status: draft`.

**Trigger to close:** fires once when each gated chapter brief is first written; closes for that chapter once the brief's anchor selection has been audited against the rule and the result recorded in the brief.

## Rule (verbatim — quote this when applying)

> Any chapter whose internal argument depends on pure-scapegoat as a load-bearing taxonomy category must use, as its pure-scapegoat anchor, a case where the documentary record is *not* partially self-contested. The conviction must have been legally vacated, the policy formally recognised as wrongful by the responsible authority, or the exoneration must rest on documentary evidence that the historiography no longer contests at the level of the underlying act. Cases like Sacco/Vanzetti, where the verdict stops short of vacatur and a serious within-case counter-reading survives in the scholarship, may be used as *echoes* but not as *anchors* in these chapters. Dreyfus-shaped cases (Cour de Cassation quashed the conviction in 1906 without remand) are the gold standard; Tuskegee (Presidential apology 1997 + Congressional record) and similar formally-recognised cost-bearing cases are also acceptable. The gate exists because chapter 2 installs pure-scapegoat with a partially self-contesting anchor honestly named as such; downstream chapters that depend on the category to do load-bearing argumentative work cannot also carry that ambiguity without compounding it.

## Chapters subject to the gate

- **ch-005 ("The Guilty Goat" — partial-scapegoat patterns).** Pure-scapegoat appears as the contrast category that defines what "partial" means; if the contrast example is contested, the partial-scapegoat install slips.
- **ch-007 ("The Record Is the Battlefield" — record-control patterns).** Pure-scapegoat cases turn on record-control failures (Dreyfus = surviving Statistical Section archive); the chapter will likely reach for a pure-scapegoat case to anchor a record-control argument and must reach for a Dreyfus-shaped one.
- **ch-011 ("Make Responsibility Follow Control" — anti-laundering rules).** Where the chapter cites a pure-scapegoat case to demonstrate a corrective rule's mechanism, the case must be one whose corrective outcome is itself documented (vacatur, apology, settlement) — otherwise the rule lacks its closing demonstration.
- **ch-013 ("A Reader's Field Guide").** Reader-facing diagnostics; the worked examples must be ones a reader can verify without entering a contested-historiography wormhole.

ch-006 ("The Pretext") and ch-012 ("Keep the Record") are not currently expected to depend on pure-scapegoat as load-bearing, but if their briefs reach for a pure-scapegoat anchor the gate applies.

## Why

[[feedback-installing-chapter-rule]] permits the installing chapter (ch-2) to use Sacco/Vanzetti as anchor with the Russell counter-reading in slot 6 — Laura R-2's in-chapter telegraph names the limit explicitly. That works for ch-2 because the chapter's job is to install the category, not to do load-bearing work *with* it. Downstream chapters do load-bearing work with it. If those chapters' anchors are also partially self-contested, the compounding ambiguity turns the diagnostic into a hedge.

## How to apply

When ch-005, ch-007, ch-011, or ch-013 enters chapter-brief stage, the brief's anchor-selection section runs through this gate. The brief explicitly states: "Pure-scapegoat anchor candidate: [name]. Documentary record status: [vacated / formally recognised / contested]. Gate result: [pass / replace]." If the candidate fails the gate, the brief either swaps to a Dreyfus-shaped anchor or demotes the case to echo and selects a different anchor for the load-bearing slot.

The domain researcher (Selina for ch-005/007 cases that touch labor/criminal-justice history; Warren for ch-008-adjacent reach into ch-007; Loki for ch-013's reader-facing cases) sources the documentary status.
