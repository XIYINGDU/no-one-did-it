# Chapter 7 — "The Record Is the Battlefield" architecture (2026-05-25)

**Decision 1 — Single-anchor + dual-callback structure, not anchor + two echoes.**
The brief uses Horizon as ANCHOR (full eight-question walk; the chapter's
load-bearing spine) and treats Therac-25 and MH17 as deliberate *callbacks*,
not echo cases. This is a departure from chapter 3's anchor+older-echo
production model, and it is the right call because chapter 7's load-bearing
move is teaching a layer relationship across previously-installed cases:
artifact layer (Therac-25, ch-2) → institutional layer (Horizon, ch-7) →
state layer (MH17, ch-4). Echo cases would compete with this layer-walk;
callbacks reinforce it.

**Why:** The chapter installs record-control as the diagnostic, and the
mechanism only becomes visible when the reader sees it operating at three
different scales of institution. Horizon must carry the full eight-question
weight; the callbacks must do work in beats 4 and 5 only.

**How to apply:** Wayne should NOT walk the eight questions through Therac-25
or MH17 in chapter 7. He should reference what chapter 2 and chapter 4
already established and use that prior installation to do new work at the
layer-comparison level. If Wayne is tempted to re-prove Therac-25 or MH17,
route back to Bonnie — that's the checklist drift risk.

**Decision 2 — Beat 9 is asymmetric-interception, modeled on ch-3, not
counter-case-anchor (ch-5 model).**
The within-case Horizon interception is rich enough to carry beat 9 on its
own: Bates GLO → Fraser J December 2019 → Hamilton April 2021 → 2024 statutory
exoneration. Mechanism: independent technical examiner (Coyne) + court with
disclosure power (Fraser J). Cost: 20+ years; documented suicides;
sub-postmasters dying before exoneration. This makes ch-7 a chapter-3-shaped
brief structurally, not a chapter-5-shaped one.

**Why:** The case file (`horizon-post-office.md`) explicitly carries both the
laundering architecture AND the interception. Adding a separate counter-case
file would split focus and weaken the layer-walk.

**How to apply:** Beat 9 lives entirely inside Horizon. No separate counter-
case slot. The asymmetry-as-lesson framing is: civil settlement succeeded

statutory exoneration succeeded (2024 Act); criminal accountability for
Post Office/Fujitsu personnel FAILED as of May 2026 (Operation Olympos
ongoing, no charges). Same record-control diagnostic; multiple interception
channels; different outcomes per channel. This is the chapter-3 production
model applied to ch-7.
