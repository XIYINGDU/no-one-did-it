# 第 8 章双语翻译稿 — 当权力自称山羊

## 题记

**原文：**
> the strong do what they can and the weak suffer what they must.
>
> — Thucydides, *History of the Peloponnesian War* 5.89, trans. Richard Crawley

**译文：**
> 强者行其所能为，弱者受其所必受。
>
> ——修昔底德，《伯罗奔尼撒战争史》5.89，理查德·克劳利英译

**翻译笔记：**
- 引用修昔底德标准中译本措辞（徐松岩译本）。"the strong do what they can and the weak suffer what they must"是雅典将军在米洛斯对话中的著名表述，中文学界已有通译。

The text of the proclamation describes the act as ending "a grave national injustice that has been perpetrated upon the American people over the last four years."

According to reconstructions in the Associated Press, ProPublica, and CBS News, approximately 137 Venezuelan nationals were transferred to the Centro de Confinamiento del Terrorismo, known as CECOT, a maximum-security facility operated under President Nayib Bukele's "estado de excepción" detention program[<sup>9</sup>](#c8-9). **翻译笔记：**

## 三个框架，同一动作（第 27–40 行）

**原文：**
The official frame is consistent across the three acts and forms a single argument.

**译文：**
官方框架在这三项行为中是一致的，并构成一个单一的论点。

**原文：**
The January 6 pardons, in the language of the proclamation itself, "end a grave national injustice." Public statements from administration officials in early 2025, as indexed at whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/, characterized the prosecutions as politically motivated and described convicted January 6 defendants using language including the term "hostages"[<sup>11</sup>](#c8-11).

**译文：**
1月6日赦免令，用公告本身的措辞来说，"结束了一桩严重的国家不公。"行政当局官员在2025年初的公开声明——收录于 whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/——将检控描述为政治动机驱动，并使用包括"人质"一词在内的语言描述被定罪的1月6日被告[<sup>11</sup>](#c8-11)。

**原文：**
The records litigation against the Department of Government Efficiency was, in the administration's filings and public communications, an obstruction of an authorized reform program. Executive Order 14158, signed January 20, 2025 and published in the Federal Register on January 29, 2025, established the U.S. DOGE Service to "modernize federal technology and software to maximize governmental efficiency and productivity"[<sup>12</sup>](#c8-12). When Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington filed CREW v. U.S. DOGE Service, No. 1:25-cv-00511, in the District Court for the District of Columbia, the administration's position was that the new service was not an "agency" subject to the Freedom of Information Act, that records of personnel embedded inside other agencies belonged to those agencies, and that encrypted-messaging use was not official business requiring preservation[<sup>13</sup>](#c8-13).

**译文：**
针对政府效率部的记录诉讼，在行政当局的法庭文件和公开沟通中，是对一项经授权改革计划的阻挠。2025年1月20日签署、2025年1月29日刊登于《联邦公报》的第14158号行政命令，建立了美国政府效率部（U.S. DOGE Service），旨在于"实现联邦技术和软件的现代化，以最大化政府效率和生产力"[<sup>12</sup>](#c8-12)。当华盛顿责任与道德公民组织（CREW）在哥伦比亚特区地区法院提起"CREW诉美国政府效率部案"时，行政当局的立场是：该新部门不是受《信息自由法》约束的"机构"；嵌入其他机构的人员的记录属于那些机构；使用加密消息不是需要保存的公务行为[<sup>13</sup>](#c8-13)。

**翻译笔记：**
- [SPLIT] 原文第33行的长句包含了行政当局的三种立场，中文拆分为三个并列分句
- "encrypted-messaging use" → 使用加密消息（指使用Signal等加密通讯应用）
- "not official business requiring preservation" → 不是需要保存的公务行为

**原文：**
The Alien Enemies Act invocation, in the language of Proclamation 10903, responded to an "invasion." Subsequent administration communications indexed at whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/ characterized the removed individuals using language including "terrorists" and "the worst of the worst," and senior administration officials publicly criticized federal judges who enforced due-process limits, in some instances using language including "activist judges"[<sup>14</sup>](#c8-14).

**译文：**
敌对外侨法的援引，用第10903号公告的措辞来说，是针对一场"入侵"。随后收录于 whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/ 的行政当局沟通，使用包括"恐怖分子"和"最坏中的最坏"在内的语言描述被驱逐者，高级行政官员公开批评执行正当程序限制的联邦法官，在某些情况下使用了包括"激进法官"在内的语言[<sup>14</sup>](#c8-14)。

**原文：**
The third frame already contains the fact that breaks it. One of the men removed to El Salvador on March 15, 2025 was Kilmar Armando Abrego Garcia, and a 2019 immigration-judge order forbidding his removal to El Salvador was on file in the federal immigration-record system when the flight left[<sup>10</sup>](#c8-10). The government removed him anyway, in defiance of the binding order[<sup>10</sup>](#c8-10). In subsequent court filings in Abrego Garcia v. Noem, the government's own characterization of that removal was "administrative error"[<sup>15</sup>](#c8-15). The frame names due-process review as the prior abuse; the government's own filing names the act the review existed to prevent.

**译文：**
第三个框架已经包含了打破它的事实。2025年3月15日被送往萨尔瓦多的人中有一个是基尔马·阿曼多·阿布雷戈·加西亚，当航班起飞时，一份2019年移民法官签发的禁止将其遣返至萨尔瓦多的命令已存在于联邦移民记录系统中[<sup>10</sup>](#c8-10)。政府还是把他送走了，无视具有约束力的命令[<sup>10</sup>](#c8-10)。在随后的"阿布雷戈·加西亚诉诺姆案"的法庭文件中，政府自己对这次驱逐的描述是"行政错误"[<sup>15</sup>](#c8-15)。框架将正当程序审查称为先前的不公；政府自己的文件所称的正是该审查旨在防止的行为。

**翻译笔记：**
- [TERM-NOTE] "administrative error" → "行政错误"（政府自己的法庭文件中的描述词，加引号保留为直接引语）。这是全章的关键证据暴露点——框架将正当程序审查称为"先前的不公"，而政府的文件将正当程序审查本应防止的行为称为"行政错误"。

**原文：**
The three frames stack into one move. In each case, an accountability mechanism — prosecutions, records statutes, due-process review — is named as the prior abuse, and dismantling the mechanism is named as the correction. The actor with control of the executive branch is positioned as the goat-bearing reformer; the institutions that exist to check that actor are positioned as the wrongdoers. This is the system/object alibi turned inside out: instead of routing blame onto a procedure or a machine, power claims the goat's role for itself and recasts the checking institutions as the chain that did the wrong.

**译文：**
三个框架叠加为一个动作。在每一个案例中，一个问责机制——检控、记录法、正当程序审查——被命名为先前的不公，而 dismantle 这一机制被命名为纠正。掌控行政部门的行动者被定位为承载山羊角色的改革者；那些本应制约该行动者的制度被定位为犯错者。这是系统/对象托辞的翻面（turned inside out）：不再是归咎于程序或机器，而是权力自己声称山羊的角色，并将制约它的制度重新框架为犯错的责任链。

**翻译笔记：**
- [TERM-NOTE] 第39行是全章核心段落。"system/object alibi turned inside out" → "系统/对象托辞的翻面"。"turn inside out"在此处是本章核心分析概念——不是简单的"反转"，而是"翻面"（将口袋翻出，内面变成外面）
- "power claims the goat's role for itself" → "权力自己声称山羊的角色"——goat在此处指"替罪羊角色"(scapegoat)，而非字面意义的山羊
- "goat-bearing reformer" → "承载山羊角色的改革者"——bear/goat在本书中已有cost-bearing goat的先例，此处译为"承载...角色"

**原文：**
This is not a misreading of the public communications. It is what the public communications, on the record, claim.

**译文：**
这不是对公开沟通的误读。这正是公开沟通——在有记录的情况下——所声称的。

## 链仍然攀爬（第 43–78 行）

**原文：**
The case to walk through is the Alien Enemies Act invocation, because it produced the cleanest record-hardening pair. We pick up the same eight questions installed in chapter 3 — blame, control, benefit, knowledge, preventability, record-control, cost, and what responsibility would look like if it followed control instead of visibility — and run them here without modification.

**译文：**
我们要逐步审视的案例是敌对外侨法的援引，因为它产生了最清晰的记录固化对。我们拿起第3章已安装的同一个八问——归咎、控制、受益、知情、可预防性、记录控制、代价、以及如果让责任跟随控制权而非可见度会是什么样子——在这里不加修改地运行它们。

**翻译笔记：**
- "record-hardening pair" → "记录固化对"——指一个"框架+反记录"的成对结构（官方框架 vs. 制度性记录）
- 八问的中文必须与第3章完全一致。此处为缩写提示，正文中展开为完整句子

**原文：**
#### Who was publicly blamed?

Abrego Garcia, characterized by the administration in post-removal statements as a member of the MS-13 gang, with the characterization disputed in filings in Abrego Garcia v. Noem[<sup>16</sup>](#c8-16). The 2019 immigration-judge order on file did not find him to be a gang member; the withholding-of-removal grant was based on a particularized threat against him by gangs in his home town of Los Nogales, San Salvador, as reflected in subsequent court filings[<sup>17</sup>](#c8-17). The approximately 137 other Venezuelan nationals removed on March 15, 2025, were each alleged by the government to be members of Tren de Aragua; the allegations have not been individuated to adjudicated finding as of May 2026, per the J.G.G. v. Trump docket and parallel proceedings[<sup>18</sup>](#c8-18).

**译文：**
#### 谁或什么被公开归咎？

阿布雷戈·加西亚，行政当局在驱逐后的声明中将其描述为 MS-13 黑帮成员，该描述在"阿布雷戈·加西亚诉诺姆案"的文件中受到争议[<sup>16</sup>](#c8-16)。存档的2019年移民法官命令并未认定他是黑帮成员；禁止遣返保护（withholding of removal）的授予是基于对其在圣萨尔瓦多的洛斯诺加莱斯家乡的黑帮对他构成的特定威胁，如随后的法庭文件所反映[<sup>17</sup>](#c8-17)。2025年3月15日被驱逐的其他约137名委内瑞拉国民，均被政府声称是"阿拉瓜列车"（Tren de Aragua）的成员；截至2026年5月，根据"J.G.G.诉特朗普案"的案卷和并行程序，这些指控尚未个别化为经裁判的认定[<sup>18</sup>](#c8-18)。

**翻译笔记：**
- 八问第一问的中文必须与第3章完全一致："谁或什么被公开归咎？"
- withholding of removal grant → 禁止遣返保护的授予（glossary译法：withholding of removal → 禁止遣返保护）
- MS-13 gang → MS-13 黑帮（保留英文缩写，中文媒体通用）
- J.G.G. v. Trump → "J.G.G.诉特朗普案"

**原文：**
#### Who had control?

The executive branch. Proclamation 10903 was the operative act. The Department of Homeland Security executed the removals. Immigration and Customs Enforcement constructed the manifests, filed into the J.G.G. v. Trump and Abrego Garcia v. Noem dockets[<sup>19</sup>](#c8-19).

**译文：**
#### 谁有控制权？

行政部门。第10903号公告是操作性的行为。国土安全部执行了驱逐。移民和海关执法局（ICE）编制了舱单，已提交至"J.G.G.诉特朗普案"和"阿布雷戈·加西亚诉诺姆案"的案卷[<sup>19</sup>](#c8-19)。

#### The pardon power is plenary.

The Constitution states, at Article II, Section 2, Clause 1, that the President "shall have Power to grant Reprieves and Pardons for Offences against the United States, except in Cases of Impeachment."
