## 第 6 章 — 借口

**原文：**
> The fable shows that against those whose disposition is to do wrong, not even a just defense prevails. (translation)
>
> — Aesop, *The Wolf and the Lamb* (Perry 155; Greek, Chambry)

**译文：**
> 这则寓言表明，对于那些意图作恶之人，连正当的辩护也无法奏效。（英译）
>
> ——伊索，《狼和小羊》（Perry 155；希腊文，Chambry 编）

**翻译笔记：**
- 题记引用伊索寓言《狼和小羊》——故事中狼吃羊时为自己的行为寻找借口（"你在下游把水搅浑了"、"去年你说过我坏话"等），羊的每一条辩护都被狼以借口驳回。与本章"借口"主题完美呼应。
- (translation) 标记保留——原注说明这是英译。
- "those whose disposition is to do wrong" → "那些意图作恶之人"。"disposition"译为"意图"而非"倾向"，更准确地反映原文中"定意要作恶"的主动含义。

## 开场场景

**原文：**
> On June 18, 2018, ProPublica published a recording.
>
> The file was about eight minutes long. It had been captured the previous week at a U.S. Customs and Border Protection processing facility. The reporter who released it, Ginger Thompson, named what was audible: the voices of children, ages four through ten, who had been separated from their parents within the previous twenty-four hours. A Border Patrol agent's voice could be heard in the background. At one point the agent made a joke. He said the room sounded like an orchestra. He said it was missing only a conductor.
>
> The recording reached the public through a journalist, not through a court, not through an inspector general, not through Congress. By the time ProPublica published, the federal government's position was that no separations policy existed. Kirstjen Nielsen, the Secretary of Homeland Security, had stated this position the day before — on Twitter, at 2:08 p.m. on June 17 — using the formulation: "We do not have a policy of separating families at the border. Period."[^201]
>
> By the time the recording had been heard a hundred thousand times, two arguments had already been built around the audible facts. The first argument was advanced by Attorney General Jeff Sessions — most prominently in his May 7, 2018 San Diego remarks and his April 6, 2018 memorandum to U.S. Attorneys — by the Department of Homeland Security Secretary, and by the White House. It rested on a federal statute. It went like this: the United States Code, at Title 8, Section 1325, makes improper entry across the border a federal misdemeanor. The Department of Justice enforces federal criminal statutes. Defendants in federal criminal cases are taken into federal criminal custody. Children may not accompany their parents into federal criminal custody. Therefore, when parents who entered the United States with their children were prosecuted under § 1325, the separation that followed was not a policy choice. It was a consequence of the law.[^202]

**译文：**

2018 年 6 月 18 日，ProPublica 发布了一段录音。

文件长约八分钟。是前一周在美国海关与边境保护局的一处处理设施中录制的。发布它的记者金杰·汤普森（Ginger Thompson）指出了录音中可以听见的内容：四岁至十岁的孩子们的声音——他们在前二十四小时内刚被与父母分离。背景中可听到一名边境巡逻队员的声音。有一刻，这名边境巡逻队员开了个玩笑。他说这间屋子听起来像一支管弦乐队。他说只差一个指挥。

这段录音通过一名记者到达了公众——而非通过法院、并非通过监察长、并非通过国会。到 ProPublica 发布之时，联邦政府的立场是：不存在分离政策。国土安全部部长克尔斯滕·尼尔森（Kirstjen Nielsen）在前一天——6 月 17 日下午 2 时 08 分——在 Twitter 上以这样的措辞陈述了这一立场："我们没有在边境分离家庭的政策。句号。"[^201]

到这段录音被听了十万次之时，已有两套围绕这些听得见的事实构建起来的论证。第一套论证由司法部长杰夫·塞申斯（Jeff Sessions）提出——最突出的是在他 2018 年 5 月 7 日的圣地亚哥讲话和同年 4 月 6 日给美国联邦检察官的备忘录中——由国土安全部部长和白宫共同推进。它依靠一条联邦制定法。它大致是这样的：《美国法典》第 8 卷第 1325 条将越境非法入境定为联邦轻罪。司法部执行联邦刑事制定法。联邦刑事案件中的被告被送入联邦刑事羁押。儿童不能随父母进入联邦刑事羁押。因此，当带子女进入美国的父母依据第 1325 条被起诉时，随之而来的分离不是政策选择。它是法律的后果。[^202]

**原文：**
> The Attorney General said this in nearly those words on May 7, 2018, to a Border Patrol audience in San Diego. The sentence that has lasted in the public record from that day is the one he aimed at parents directly: "If you don't want your child separated, then don't bring them across the border illegally."[^203] The grammar in the operative sentence elsewhere is the move worth watching. The law required it. The statute compelled the result. The separation was something the statute did, not something a Secretary signed, not something an Attorney General memorandum directed, not something a coordination meeting at the White House had decided to scale.
>
> The grammar was the alibi.
>
> We have three cases in which a cited authority — a statute, a regulation, a procedure — was put forward as the cause of an action that a named decision-maker had chosen. Family separation, in 2017 and 2018, is the first. The 2020 census citizenship question, decided in 2018 and litigated through 2019, is the second. The Office of Management and Budget's 2019 hold on Ukraine security assistance is the third. The three cases sit at three different layers of American administrative law: criminal enforcement; administrative procedure; appropriations. The argument is not about any one of them. It is about the form they share. When the cited authority is named as the agent, the named invoker has been removed from the sentence. The diagnostic question, by the end, is not whether the statute exists. The diagnostic question is who recruited the statute, against whom, at what intensity, and for what documented alternative purpose.

**译文：**

司法部长在 2018 年 5 月 7 日几乎以同样的措辞向圣地亚哥的边境巡逻队员说了这番话。那天在公共记录中留存下来的那句话，是他直接对准父母们说的："如果你不想要你的孩子被分离，那就不要非法带他们穿越边境。"[^203] 另一处操作语句中的语法结构，才是值得关注的手法。**法律要求如此。制定法迫使了这个结果。分离是制定法做的事**，不是某位部长签署的决定，不是某份司法部长备忘录的指令，不是白宫某次协调会议决定扩大的规模。

这语法就是托辞。

我们有三个案例，在其中，一个被引用的权威——一条制定法、一项规章、一套程序——被作为已发生的行动的原因提出来，而该行动实际上是某个被指名的决策者选择的。家庭分离，发生在 2017 和 2018 年，是第一个。2020 年人口普查公民身份问题，在 2018 年做出决策、在 2019 年通过诉讼审理，是第二个。管理与预算办公室 2019 年对乌克兰安全援助的暂扣，是第三个。三个案例位于美国行政法的三个不同层面：刑事执法；行政程序；拨款。此处的论证不是关于其中任何一个。而是关于它们共享的形式。**当被引用的权威被命名为行动者时，被指名的援引者已经从句子中被移除了。** 诊断问题，到头来，不是制定法是否存在。诊断问题是：谁援引了该制定法，针对谁，以什么强度，以及为了什么有记载的替代目的。

**翻译笔记：**
- "the grammar was the alibi" → "这语法就是托辞"。"语法"(grammar)在此是隐喻——指把制定法放在主语位置的语法结构本身就是一种托辞（alibi，已有术语）。"这"比"这个"更短、更有力。
- "operative sentence" → "操作语句"。区别于装饰性修辞——这是实际在起论证作用的语句。
- "The law required it. The statute compelled the result." → 三句短句加粗处理，保留原文道德转折短句的节奏。
- "named invoker" → "被指名的援引者"。invoke/invoker 是本章核心动词/名词——指援引法律作为托辞的人。
- "hold on Ukraine security assistance" → "对乌克兰安全援助的暂扣"。"hold"在此处指 OMB 的扣留行为，非一般意义的"冻结"。

## 三套制定法，同一套语法

**原文：**
> ## Three statutes, one grammar
>
> Three statutory referents. Three administrations of grammar. The dates and texts matter.
>
> #### Family separation, criminal-enforcement shell.
>
> On April 6, 2018, Attorney General Jeff Sessions issued a memorandum to United States Attorneys along the southwest border directing that they "adopt immediately a zero-tolerance policy for all offenses referred for prosecution under section 275(a) of the INA" — that is, 8 U.S.C. § 1325.[^204] On May 4, 2018, Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kirstjen Nielsen signed a memorandum directing Customs and Border Protection to refer all amenable adults — explicitly including those traveling with children — for § 1325 prosecution.[^205] The public framing through April, May, and June characterized the resulting separations as a mechanical consequence of prosecution. The cited cause was a misdemeanor statute that had existed in the U.S. Code since 1929.
>
> #### Census citizenship question, administrative-procedure shell.
>
> On March 26, 2018, Commerce Secretary Wilbur Ross issued a memorandum to the Acting Director of the U.S. Census Bureau reinstating a citizenship question on the 2020 decennial census.[^206] The cited reason was a request from the Department of Justice. The DOJ request — a letter dated December 12, 2017, signed by Arthur Gary, General Counsel of the Justice Management Division — characterized improved citizenship data as necessary for the Department's enforcement of Section 2 of the Voting Rights Act.[^207] Two real federal statutes paired naturally. DOJ enforces the VRA. Commerce administers the decennial census. The cited authority was the Voting Rights Act of 1965; the cited mechanism was the routine administrative procedure for evaluating an interagency data request.
>
> #### Ukraine aid hold, appropriations shell.
>
> On July 18, 2019, the Office of Management and Budget placed a hold on $391 million in Ukraine security assistance funds. Of that, approximately $250 million belonged to the Department of Defense Ukraine Security Initiative (USAI), and $141 million to State Department Foreign Military Financing (FMF). Congress had appropriated the funds for Ukraine's military defense against Russian forces that had occupied Crimea since 2014 and were active in the Donbas at the time, as documented in the Government Accountability Office's January 16, 2020 opinion B-331564.[^208] The hold was implemented through an OMB apportionment footnote signed by OMB Associate Director Michael Duffey. The cited rationale was an "interagency policy review" — characterized in OMB communications as an evaluation of burden-sharing by European allies, of corruption in Ukraine, and of whether the assistance was an appropriate use of taxpayer funds. The cited authority was the executive branch's recognized practice of apportionment review under 31 U.S.C. § 1512. The cited mechanism was a procedural pause for evaluation.

**译文：**

## 三套制定法，同一套语法

三个制定法参照物。三组语法施用。日期和文本至关重要。

#### 家庭分离，刑事执法的外壳。

2018 年 4 月 6 日，司法部长杰夫·塞申斯向西南边境沿线各联邦检察官发出备忘录，指示他们"立即对所有依据《移民与国籍法》第 275(a)条移送起诉的违法行为采取零容忍政策"——即《美国法典》第 8 卷第 1325 条。[^204] 2018 年 5 月 4 日，国土安全部部长克尔斯滕·尼尔森签署备忘录，指示海关与边境保护局将一切符合条件的成年人——明确包括带子女旅行的成年人——移送第 1325 条起诉。[^205] 从四月到六月的公开陈述将由此产生的分离定性为起诉的机械性后果。被引用的原因是一条自 1929 年起就存在于《美国法典》中的轻罪制定法。

#### 人口普查公民身份问题，行政程序的外壳。

2018 年 3 月 26 日，商务部长威尔伯·罗斯（Wilbur Ross）向美国人口普查局代理局长发出备忘录，要求在 2020 年十年一次人口普查中恢复公民身份问题。[^206] 被引用的理由来自司法部的一项请求。该司法部请求——一封 2017 年 12 月 12 日的信函，由司法管理司总法律顾问阿瑟·加里（Arthur Gary）签署——将改善公民身份数据描述为司法部执行《投票权法案》第 2 条所必需的。[^207] 两条真实的联邦制定法自然配对。司法部执行《投票权法案》。商务部管理十年一次人口普查。被引用的权威是 1965 年的《投票权法案》；被引用的机制是评估跨部门数据请求的常规行政程序。

#### 乌克兰援助暂扣，拨款的外壳。

2019 年 7 月 18 日，管理与预算办公室对 3.91 亿美元的乌克兰安全援助资金实施暂扣。其中，约 2.5 亿美元属于国防部的乌克兰安全倡议（USAI），1.41 亿美元属于国务院的外国军事融资（FMF）。国会已拨付这些资金用于乌克兰的军事防御，以对抗自 2014 年起占领克里米亚、当时正在顿巴斯活动的俄罗斯军队——如政府问责局 2020 年 1 月 16 日发布的 B-331564 号意见书所记载。[^208] 该暂扣通过一项由 OMB 副主任迈克尔·达菲（Michael Duffey）签署的拨款分配脚注实施。被引用的理由是"跨部门政策审查"——在 OMB 的通讯中被描述为对欧洲盟国负担分摊、乌克兰腐败状况以及援助是否为纳税人资金的适当用途的评估。被引用的权威是行政部门根据《美国法典》第 31 卷第 1512 条进行拨款分配审查的公认惯例。被引用的机制是用于评估的程序性暂停。

**翻译笔记：**

- "USAI" / "FMF" → 保留英文缩写，首次出现时括号注中文全称。

- "zero-tolerance policy" → "零容忍政策"。标准中文用法。

## 检测引用权威的六问

**原文：**
> ## Six questions for a citation
>
> Pretext analysis is older than any of these three cases. The federal courts have been running a version of it since at least 1971. The Administrative Procedure Act, at 5 U.S.C. § 706(2)(A), allows a federal court to set aside agency action found to be "arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law."[^209] In *Citizens to Preserve Overton Park v. Volpe*, 401 U.S. 402 (1971), the Supreme Court held that the reviewing court must consider whether the agency's decision was based on a consideration of the relevant factors and whether there has been a clear error of judgment.[^210] The doctrine that descends from *Overton Park* — the requirement that an agency offer a "reasoned explanation" that is supported by the administrative record — is the procedural tool federal courts use when a citation is offered as a cause. It is the courts' way of asking: does the record show what you said the record shows?
>
> That doctrine is the older echo we need. Pretext is not a novel pathology of any single administration. It is an administrative-law adversary that the courts have been piercing, slowly, since the Nixon era. The cases that follow are not new in form. They are visible because the form was contested across three layers in a compressed period.
>
> The diagnostic that follows is a refinement of the eight-question instrument we installed earlier. It specializes those questions for the case in which the cited cause is not a system, not an algorithm, not a deceased actor — but a legal authority invoked by a living official.

**译文：**

## 检测引用权威的六问

借口分析比上述三个案例都更古老。联邦法院至少自 1971 年起就在运行其一个版本。《行政程序法》，在《美国法典》第 5 卷第 706(2)(A)条，允许联邦法院将"任意且反复无常的、滥用裁量权的、或以其他方式不符合法律的"行政行为撤销。[^209] 在 *Overton Park 保护公民诉沃尔普案*（Citizens to Preserve Overton Park v. Volpe），401 U.S. 402（1971）中，最高法院认定，审查法院必须考虑该机构的决定是否基于对相关因素的考量，以及是否存在明显的判断错误。[^210] 从 *Overton Park* 案传承下来的原则——要求行政机构提供有行政记录支持的"有理由的解释"——是联邦法院在面对以引用为由的论证时使用的程序工具。它是法院的发问方式：记录是否显示了你所说的记录所显示的内容？

这个原则就是我们需要的那段更古老的回声。借口不是某个特定政府的新病理。它是一个行政法层面的对手，法院自尼克松时代起就在缓慢地穿透它。以下案例在形式上并不新奇。它们之所以可见，是因为该形式在一个压缩的时间段内在三个不同行政法层面受到了挑战。

接下来的诊断工具是对我们之前安装的八问工具的细化。它将这些问题的适用范围专门化到以下场景：被引用的原因不是系统、不是算法、不是已故的行动者——而是一位在世官员援引的法律权威。

**翻译笔记：**
- "arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law" → "任意且反复无常的、滥用裁量权的、或以其他方式不符合法律的"。此为 APA §706(2)(A) 标准用语的中文译法。
- "reasoned explanation" → "有理由的解释"（新增 glossary 术语）。保留"有理由的"（reasoned）的限定——不是任意解释。
- "the older echo" → "那段更古老的回声"。"echo"在本书中用于指代历史先例——类似案例在更早的历史中已有回响。
- "piercing" → "穿透"。与 glossary 新增术语"piercing channel"（穿透渠道）保持一致。

**原文：**
> Ask the six questions in order. Ask them all.
>
> > **1. What does the cited authority actually require?** Read the text. Does it *require* the specific action taken, or does it *permit* the action under conditions the invoker chose to satisfy?
> >
> > **2. Who recruited the cited authority?** The cited authority does not invoke itself. There is always a named invoker. Find the name.
> >
> > **3. What was the documented alternative purpose?** What does the administrative record, the internal communications, the inspector-general report, the trial findings, or the contemporaneous documentary record show was the actual reason for the action?
> >
> > **4. What does the cited authority's prior use pattern look like?** Has the same authority been invoked, at this intensity, against this population, before? Or is this an invocation at a novel intensity, dressed in the form of routine enforcement?
> >
> > **5. Which institutional venues are available to test the citation?** Federal-court class action? APA review? Inspector general report? GAO opinion? Congressional inquiry? Sworn testimony? Constitutional sanction? Posthumous record-disclosure?
> >
> > **6. What would responsibility look like if it followed the invoker instead of the cited authority?** Re-run the case with the named invoker as the responsibility-holder. Where does the chain now stop?

> The doctrine that lets a court see through the form is older than these cases. The lineage matters briefly. The lineage is also our defense against the easy charge that pretext analysis is a partisan tool aimed at one administration.
>
> In *Citizens to Preserve Overton Park v. Volpe*, 401 U.S. 402, decided in 1971, the Supreme Court reviewed the Secretary of Transportation's decision to authorize construction of a six-lane interstate highway through a park in Memphis. The federal statute permitted construction through a park only if no feasible and prudent alternative existed. The Secretary had not stated his reasons. The Court held that the reviewing court must consider whether the decision was based on a consideration of the relevant factors, and that the agency must offer a contemporaneous explanation of its reasoning.[^227] The Court remanded for a more developed administrative record.
>
> The doctrine grew. *Motor Vehicle Manufacturers Association v. State Farm*, decided in 1983, articulated the modern version: the agency must "examine the relevant data and articulate a satisfactory explanation for its action including a 'rational connection between the facts found and the choice made.'"[^228] The phrase "reasoned explanation" entered the working vocabulary of administrative-law practitioners. The standard sits inside 5 U.S.C. § 706(2)(A), the Administrative Procedure Act's authorization for a court to set aside agency action that is arbitrary or capricious. The phrase "arbitrary and capricious" sounds severe in the ordinary sense. The doctrine it carries is procedural. It asks whether the agency considered what it was required to consider, and whether the stated reasons are supported by the record.
>
> Our three case-anchors fall inside a single US administration (2017–2020). That selection is a function of evidentiary density — three cases at A-grade with full inspector-general, federal-court, and GAO records hardened inside one compressed period — and not a claim that the pretext form is partisan. The doctrine pierced Nixon-administration highway routing in *Overton Park*; it pierced Reagan-administration regulatory rescission in *State Farm*; it has been applied across executive branches of both parties before and since. The form is older and wider than the three cases we walk. The cases are chosen for record density, not for political target.

## 制度运作，但又没有

以上每个案例都被严肃的人描述为制度性回应足够充分的案例。这个反对论点并非无聊。它必须在被回应之前以全力陈述。

#### 家庭分离，反对论点。

零容忍政策在公众曝光后数周内即告终结。政府行政部门自己的第 13841 号行政令，于 2018 年 6 月 20 日签署，推翻了该政策。[^242] 联邦法院于 2018 年 6 月 26 日开始按法院时间表命令团聚。三份监察长报告和一份参议院委员会报告产生了异常详尽的公共记录。问责性可见地攀升：从一线官员到国土安全部和司法部部长们，再到整个政策本身，在不到两个月内。如果把这称为洗白，反对论点认为，那就夸大了制定法借口成功吸收责任的程度。该借口被拒绝了，而且很快。

这个反对论点在政治存续层面最强。该政策没有存续。这个反对论点在被指名决策者的问责层面最弱。被指名的决策者——司法部长、国土安全部部长、在司法部监察长报告的协调认定中被点名的副司法部长——截至 2026 年 5 月 25 日，没有面临联邦刑事起诉、没有受到各自律师协会的公开职业纪律处分、也没有就其个人能力范围内的行为承担民事责任。针对上述被指名官员的 Bivens 式损害赔偿诉讼，包括 *K.O. 诉美国案*及相关案件，一再遭遇**豁免资格**驳回。[^243] 据 *Ms. L.* 案指导委员会报告，截至 2026 年 1 月，仍有数百名儿童未团聚。第 13841 号行政令在数周内终结了该政策；制定法借口让被指名的决策者得以将伤害定性为机械性的法律后果；他们保住了自己的职业地位；代价承担者继续承受着伤害。

#### 人口普查公民身份问题，反对论点。

#### 乌克兰援助暂扣，反对论点。

## 三次崩塌，没有共享的模态

**原文：**
> ## Three collapses, no shared modality
>
> Three pretexts collapsed in different ways, on different days, at different venues. The dates and the modalities of collapse are our evidence for what follows.
>
> #### Family separation collapsed across eight days.
>
> On June 18, 2018, ProPublica released the audio. On June 20, 2018, the President signed Executive Order 13841, ending the family-separation operational lever administratively. On June 26, 2018, Judge Sabraw issued the preliminary injunction in *Ms. L. v. ICE*, ordering reunification within fourteen days for children under five and within thirty days for older children. Three different modalities of collapse: an audible artifact released by a journalist; an executive order signed by the same President who had presided over the policy; a federal court order entered after class certification. The audio reached the public from outside the institutional venues. The executive order reached the policy from inside the same executive branch that had implemented it. The court order reached individual children at the level of the court's continuing supervisory jurisdiction.
>
> #### Census citizenship collapsed across a five-month arc.
>
> On January 15, 2019, Judge Furman entered findings of fact characterizing Ross's stated rationale as pretextual.[^244] On May 30, 2019, the Hofeller files were filed publicly in the D. Md. litigation; the redistricting-advantage purpose entered the public court record through a posthumous disclosure that no institution had subpoenaed and that became available because a private party chose to deliver the documents. The Supreme Court did not adjudicate that purpose; its holding rested on the inadequacy of the administrative record. The Hofeller purpose is documented; the Court's holding is procedural. On June 27, 2019, the Supreme Court issued *Department of Commerce v. New York*; Chief Justice Roberts's opinion held that the administrative record could not be adequately explained by the rationale the Secretary had given.[^245] In July 2019, the Commerce Department withdrew the question. Three modalities: a federal trial court finding of pretext; a posthumous documentary disclosure delivered by a family member; a Supreme Court holding on the procedural adequacy of the administrative record.
>
> #### Ukraine aid hold collapsed across a five-month arc as well.
>
> On September 24, 2019, the White House released the call memorandum recording the "favor though" sequence. On November 20, 2019, Ambassador Sondland testified under oath before the House Intelligence Committee that the request to Ukraine to announce investigations was made at the express direction of the President. On January 16, 2020, the GAO issued its opinion finding the hold violated the Impoundment Control Act. Three modalities: the release of a primary documentary record (the call memorandum); sworn public testimony by a participating actor (Sondland); a formal appropriations-law adjudication by a non-political body (GAO). A fourth event lay between them — the House impeachment vote of December 18, 2019, applying the constitutional sanction at the chamber assigned to initiate it.
>
> Three pretexts. Three different collapse modalities. Audio plus executive order plus court order. Trial finding plus Supreme Court holding plus posthumous disclosure. Documentary release plus sworn testimony plus statutory adjudication. No single modality fired in more than one of the three cases. Where the audio mattered for separation, no audio existed for the census question. Where a posthumous file delivery mattered for the census, no posthumous file delivery occurred for Ukraine. Where a GAO opinion mattered for Ukraine, GAO had no jurisdiction over the criminal-enforcement decisions that produced family separation. The collapses are layered because the venues are layered.

## 三次崩塌，没有共享的模态

三个借口以不同的方式、在不同的日子、在不同的场所崩塌。崩塌的日期和模态是我们接下来要讨论的证据。

#### 家庭分离在八天内崩塌。

2018 年 6 月 18 日，ProPublica 发布了录音。2018 年 6 月 20 日，总统签署了第 13841 号行政令，在行政层面终止了家庭分离的操作杠杆。2018 年 6 月 26 日，萨布拉夫法官在 *Ms. L. v. ICE* 案中发出了临时禁令，命令五岁以下儿童在十四天内团聚，年龄较大的儿童在三十天内团聚。三种不同的崩塌模态：一名记者发布的录音制品；一位同一总统（该政策正是在其治下实施的）签署的行政令；集体诉讼认证后由联邦法院发出的命令。录音从制度性场所外部到达了公众。行政令从同一行政分支内部到达了政策——该分支曾实施过该政策。法院命令在法院持续的监督管辖权层面到达了每个儿童。

#### 人口普查公民身份问题在五个月的弧线中崩塌。

2019 年 1 月 15 日，弗曼法官做出了事实认定，将罗斯所述理由定性为借口性的。[^244] 2019 年 5 月 30 日，霍弗勒文件在马里兰州联邦地区法院的诉讼中被公开提交；选区重划优势的目的通过一份没有制度发出传票的死后披露进入了公共法庭记录——该披露之所以成为可能，是因为一个私人方选择了传递文件。最高法院没有裁决该目的；其裁定基于行政记录的不充分。霍弗勒所揭示的目的是有记载的；法院的裁定是程序性的。2019 年 6 月 27 日，最高法院就 *商务部诉纽约州案* 做出判决；罗伯茨首席大法官的意见裁定，部长给出的理由无法充分解释行政记录。[^245] 2019 年 7 月，商务部撤回了该问题。三种模态：联邦审判法院对借口的认定；由家属传递的死后文件披露；最高法院对行政记录程序充分性的裁定。

#### 乌克兰援助暂扣也在五个月的弧线中崩塌。

2019 年 9 月 24 日，白宫发布了记录了"不过帮个忙"对话的通话备忘录。2019 年 11 月 20 日，桑德兰大使在众议院情报委员会宣誓作证，表示对乌克兰宣布启动调查的要求是在总统的明确指示下做出的。2020 年 1 月 16 日，GAO 发布了其意见书，认定该暂扣违反了《扣留控制法》。三种模态：一份主要文件记录（通话备忘录）的发布；一名参与行动者（桑德兰）的公开宣誓证词；一个非政治性机构（GAO）的正式拨款法裁定。它们之间还躺着一个第四事件——2019 年 12 月 18 日的众议院弹劾投票，在被宪法指定发起该程序的议院中施加了宪法性制裁。

三个借口。三种不同的崩塌模态。录音加行政令加法院命令。审判认定加最高法院裁定加死后披露。文件发布加宣誓证词加法制定法裁定。没有一种模态在两个以上的案例中开了火。在录音对分离重要之处，对于人口普查问题没有录音存在。在死后文件交付对人口普查重要之处，对于乌克兰没有死后文件交付发生。在 GAO 意见书对乌克兰重要之处，GAO 对产生家庭分离的刑事执法决定没有管辖权。崩塌是分层的，因为场所是分层的。

## 将被引用的权威视为一个假说

**原文：**
> ## Treat the citation as a hypothesis
>
> We have one rule. It is portable. We carry it into the next case we encounter.
>
> > When you see a citation to a law, a regulation, or a procedure offered as the cause of an action, treat the citation as a hypothesis to be tested, not as a closed account. Ask the six diagnostic questions in order. If the cited authority's text does not require the action; if the cited authority was recruited rather than self-invoked; if the documentary record shows an alternative purpose; if the prior-use pattern shows a discontinuous spike — the citation is functioning as a pretext, and the question is which institutional venue is available to test it. The citation is the first step in the analysis, not the last. Find the invoker. Demand the documentary record. Map the available piercing channels by administrative-law layer. No single institutional venue is sufficient.
>
> This is the rule of action. It does not say "stay informed." It does not say "demand transparency." It states what a competent investigator does with a citation. It names what to ask, in what order, and what condition on the answers is sufficient to conclude that the citation is doing pretext work. It is checkable. It is teachable. It is portable to cases we never name.
>
> Three pretexts ran into three different piercing channels, and no single channel pierced all three. Family separation was reached by a federal-court class action, an executive-order reversal, inspector-general reports, and a Senate committee — not by Supreme Court APA review, impeachment, or the GAO. Ukraine was reached by the GAO at the statutory layer and by impeachment at the constitutional layer, with the removal remedy withheld. The census question was reached only by the combination of a Supreme Court APA holding and a record-disclosure no institution had designed in advance.
>
> The piercing channels are not interchangeable. The diagnostic instrument's value lies precisely here: it tells us where each channel's reach ends, and it names the channels that do not exist. The pretext form survives single-venue analysis. It collapses only against mechanism diversity.

**译文：**

## 将被引用的权威视为一个假说

我们有一条规则。它是可移植的。我们把它带入我们遇到的下一个案例。

> 当你看到一个法律、法规或程序被引用为某行动的原因时，**将该引用视为一个需要检验的假说，而非一个已完结的交代。** 按顺序问那六条诊断问题。如果被引用的权威的文本不要求该行动；如果被引用的权威是被援引来的而非自行出现的；如果文件记录显示了一个替代目的；如果先前使用模式显示了一个不连续的尖峰——那么该引用正在作为借口运作，而问题在于：哪个制度性场所可用于检验它。引用是分析的第一步，不是最后一步。找到援引者。要求文件记录。按行政法层面映射可用的穿透渠道。没有单个制度性场所是足够的。

这是行动规则。它不说"保持关注"。它不说"要求透明度"。它说明一个称职的调查者如何对待一个引用。它指出要问什么、按什么顺序问、以及答案达到什么条件就足以断定该引用在做借口的工作。它是可核查的。它是可传授的。它是可移植到我们从未命名的案例中的。

三个借口撞上了三个不同的穿透渠道，没有单个渠道穿透了全部三个。家庭分离被一个联邦法院集体诉讼、一项行政令逆转、监察长报告和参议院委员会所触及——而非被最高法院 APA 审查、弹劾或 GAO 所触及。乌克兰被 GAO 在制定法层面和弹劾在宪法层面所触及，但免职救济被扣留了。人口普查问题只通过最高法院 APA 裁定和一个没有制度预先设计的记录披露的结合才被触及。

穿透渠道不是可互换的。诊断工具的价值恰恰在此：它告诉我们每个渠道的延伸范围在哪里结束，它指出不存在的渠道。借口的形态能经得起单一场所分析的检验。它只在**机制多样性**面前崩塌。

**翻译笔记：**

- "removal remedy" → "免职救济"。指弹劾的最终制裁——免去总统职务。
- "mechanism diversity" → "机制多样性"（新增 glossary 术语）。首次出现加粗标记。

**原文：**
> A note on the channels themselves. Two of the channels that fired in these cases are themselves vulnerable. The Intelligence Community Inspector General who certified the whistleblower complaint was removed; the executive order that ended family separation was issued by the same executive that had implemented the policy; the Hofeller disclosure depended on a private choice that no institutional procedure could compel. Mechanism diversity is the recommendation; mechanism durability is not the guarantee. Knowing that the diagnostic detects the asymmetry does not entail that the asymmetry is permanently resolved by the existence of multiple venues. The venues themselves are subject to pressure. We return to this in later chapters on record control.
>
> When a case is described as "resolved" or "unresolved," ask which institutional venue resolved it. Ask which venues did not fire. Ask which venues had no jurisdiction. Ask whether the venue that fired could be removed, defunded, or politically disciplined. The map of unfiring venues is our evidence that pretext survives single-venue checks. The map of vulnerable venues is what follows next.
>
> The pretext form does not appear only at the level of presidential administrations. It appears wherever an action is justified by citing a rule rather than by naming the decision-maker who chose the action. The grammar is the recognition trigger. When the cited authority is grammatically the agent — *the law requires; the policy requires; the system requires; the regulations require us to* — the named invoker has been removed from the sentence. Our first move is to put the invoker back. The places to watch are ordinary: a workplace policy change "required by new HR guidelines," a contract clause invoked as "standard," an automated denial attributed to "the system" — each names a rule where a person, somewhere, made the choice and was written out of the sentence.
>
> The signature is the seam. The first person to put pen to paper invoking the cited authority carries the blame chain, even when they did not choose the invocation. When we cannot read the authority's text and find that it requires the specific action we are being asked to take, refuse to be the named executor. Insist that the named invoker sign first. In writing.

**译文：**

对渠道本身的说明。在这些案例中开火的渠道中有两条本身是脆弱的。认证了举报人投诉的情报界监察长被免职了；终结家庭分离的行政令是由同一行政分支——该分支曾实施过该政策——发出的；霍弗勒披露依赖于没有制度程序能够强制执行的私人选择。**机制多样性**是建议；**机制持久性**不是保证。知道诊断工具能检测出不对称性，并不意味着不对称性会因多个场所的存在而被永久解决。场所本身也会受到压力。我们在后面关于记录控制的章节中会回到这一点。

当一个案例被描述为"已解决"或"未解决"时，问哪个制度性场所解决了它。问哪些场所没有开火。问哪些场所没有管辖权。问开火的场所是否可以被免职、被削减资金、或被政治性制裁。未开火场所的地图，是我们的证据——证明借口的形态能经得起单一场所检查。脆弱场所的地图是接下来要讨论的。

借口的形态不仅出现在总统政府层面。它出现在任何通过引用规则而非命名做出该选择的决策者来为行动辩护的地方。语法就是识别触发。当被引用的权威在语法上占据了行动者的位置——*法律要求；政策要求；制度要求；规章要求我们*——被指名的援引者已经从句子中被移除了。我们的第一步是把援引者放回去。值得关注的地方是日常的：一个"根据新的人力资源指南要求的"工作场所政策变更，一个被援引为"标准"的合同条款，一个被归咎于"系统"的自动拒绝——每个都命名了一条规则，而某个地方的某个个人做出了这个选择，并被从句子中写掉了。

签名就是接缝。拿起笔援引被引用的权威的第一个人承载着归责链，即使他们没有选择该援引。当我们无法阅读该权威的文本并发现它要求我们被要求采取的具体行动时，拒绝做这个被指名的执行者。坚持让被指名的援引者先签字。以书面形式。

**原文：**
> Three pretexts. Three administrative-law layers. Three different piercing channels. One diagnostic question that pierces all three: *what would the administrative record have to show for the citation to be honest?*
>
> For the census question, the answer arrived from outside the institution entirely. The administrative record the Supreme Court reviewed showed only that the stated voting-rights rationale was inadequate; it would have sent the question back to the agency for a cleaner re-proposal. What surfaced the documented purpose underneath the citation was a set of hard drives — the files Thomas Hofeller's daughter delivered to plaintiffs' counsel after his death, filed publicly on May 30, 2019. The pretext was pierced not by a designed institutional check but by a custody change no procedure could compel and no agency could have anticipated. That is the escape: the citation held until a record outside the originating institution's control made the alternative purpose unanswerable.
>
> When we cannot answer that question from the public record, we are looking at a pretext. Next: what happens when the public record itself is the thing being controlled.

**译文：**

三个借口。三个行政法层面。三个不同的穿透渠道。一个穿透了全部三个的诊断问题：**行政记录需要显示出什么，该引用才算是诚实的？**

对于人口普查问题，答案完全来自制度之外。最高法院审查的行政记录只显示所述投票权理由是不充分的；它本会将问题发回行政机构，要求更干净的重新提案。把引用之下有记载的目的暴露出来的，是一套硬盘——托马斯·霍弗勒的女儿在他死后交给原告律师的文件，于 2019 年 5 月 30 日公开提交。该借口不是被一个设计的制度性制衡穿透的，而是被一个没有程序能够强制、没有任何机构能够预料到的保管权变更所穿透。这就是摆脱方式：该引用一直站立，直到一个处于起源机构控制之外的记录使替代目的变得无可辩驳。

当我们不能从公共记录中回答那个问题时，我们面对的是一个借口。接下来：当公共记录本身正是那个被控制的东西时，会发生什么？

**翻译笔记：**
- "escape" → "摆脱方式"。指借口被穿透的具体机制——不是制度设计的结果，而是外部因素。
- "custody change" → "保管权变更"。指文件从原持有人（霍弗勒）到其女儿再到原告律师的保管链变化。
- "unanswerable" → "无可辩驳"。指替代目的变得无法被否认。
