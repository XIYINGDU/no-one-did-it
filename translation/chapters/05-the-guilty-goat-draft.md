## 第 5 章 — 有罪的山羊

**原文：**
> > He complains of the tyrant who lives in your reign;
> > For each wrong he commits unto you will pertain.
> >
> > — Saʿdi, *Būstān*, trans. G. S. Davie (*The Garden of Fragrance*, 1882)

**译文：**
> 他抱怨暴君，那暴君活在你的统治之中；
> 因为他对每一项错行，都将归于你的权柄。
>
> ——萨迪，《果园》，G. S. 戴维英译（《芬芳的花园》，1882年）

**翻译笔记：**
- 萨迪（Saʿdi，1208-1292），波斯诗人。《果园》（Būstān）为其代表作之一，成书于1257年。英译者为 G. S. Davie，1882年出版的英译本名 *The Garden of Fragrance*。
- 此处题记暗喻暴君所做的错事将归于统治之下的人民——与本章关于归责链被迫停在低层的主题呼应。
- 译文采用略带古风的二联句形式，匹配1882年英译的古雅风格。

### 开场场景

**原文：**
> On December 7, 1984, Warren M. Anderson stepped off a Pan American flight at Bhopal's airport and into the custody of the Madhya Pradesh state police. He was the chairman of Union Carbide Corporation. Four days earlier, on the night of December 2–3, approximately forty tonnes of methyl isocyanate had escaped from Tank E610 at the Union Carbide India Limited pesticide plant on the city's northern edge. The cloud had drifted south over the J.P. Nagar settlement. The Government of India would later tell its own Supreme Court that more than three thousand people had died in the first days.

**译文：**
1984年12月7日，沃伦·M·安德森从泛美航空的一架航班步出博帕尔机场，随即被中央邦警方拘留。他是联合碳化物公司（Union Carbide Corporation，以下简称 UCC）的董事长。四天前，12月2日至3日的夜间，约四十吨甲基异氰酸酯从该市北郊联合碳化物印度有限公司（Union Carbide India Limited，以下简称 UCIL）农药厂的 E610 储罐泄漏。毒气云向南飘过 J.P. 纳加尔定居区。印度政府后来向本国最高法院陈述，仅最初几天内就有三千余人死亡。

**翻译笔记：**
- Warren M. Anderson → 沃伦·M·安德森。保留中间名首字母 M。
- Union Carbide Corporation → 联合碳化物公司。首次出现括号附英文 UCC，后续可用简称"UCC"。
- Union Carbide India Limited → 联合碳化物印度有限公司。首次出现括号附英文 UCIL，后续可用简称"UCIL"。
- methyl isocyanate → 甲基异氰酸酯。化学品标准中文名。
- "more than three thousand" → "三千余人"，保留原文的模糊数量级。

---

**原文：**
> Anderson had flown to India to inspect, to apologize, to be seen. The Madhya Pradesh police charged him with culpable homicide. He was driven to a Union Carbide guest house, photographed under guard, and within hours released on bail of twenty-five thousand rupees. The same day he boarded a state government aircraft to Delhi, and from Delhi he flew home. He did not return to India. India's formal extradition request, filed in 2003 and pursued through 2011, was never honored by the United States. Anderson died at his home in Vero Beach, Florida, on September 29, 2014, at the age of ninety-two, the named bearer of corporate blame for the worst industrial accident in modern history.

**译文：**
安德森飞往印度是为了视察、道歉、被看见。中央邦警方以**应受惩罚的过失杀人**（culpable homicide，印度刑法典第304A条）对他提出指控。他被带到联合碳化物的一间招待所，在警卫看守下拍照，数小时内以两万五千卢比获保释。同日，他乘坐邦政府飞机前往德里，从德里飞回美国。他再未返回印度。印度于2003年正式提出**引渡**请求并持续追索至2011年，但美国从未批准该请求。安德森于2014年9月29日在佛罗里达州维罗海滩的家中去世，享年九十二岁，作为现代史上最严重工业事故的企业责任被指名承担者。

**翻译笔记：**
- culpable homicide → 应受惩罚的过失杀人。首次出现需完整格式：中文 + 括号附英文及 IPC 来源。参考 glossary 新术语第 5 条。
- extradition → 引渡。标准法律术语。中文读者熟悉，无需中英对照。
- "named bearer of corporate blame" → "企业责任被指名承担者"。保留 named 的"被指名"含义。

---

**原文：**
> The arrest at Bhopal airport is the single most photographed moment of corporate-executive accountability in the 1980s. It is also where we begin. A man in the chain of authority was named. A man in the chain of authority was charged. A man in the chain of authority left, and the case was, in important senses, considered closed.

**译文：**
博帕尔机场的逮捕是1980年代企业高管问责制中最具标志性的拍照瞬间。这也是我们开始的地方。一个在权力链中的人被指名。一个在权力链中的人被起诉。一个在权力链中的人离开了，而案件在重要意义上被认为已经了结。

**翻译笔记：**
- "single most photographed moment" → "最具标志性的拍照瞬间"。原文强调 photograph 的视觉符号意义，中文保留此含义。
- 三个排比句（was named / was charged / left — considered closed）使用中文排比保持节奏。

---

**原文：**
> There is another document. In May 1982, a Union Carbide team based in the United States completed an Operational Safety Survey of the UCIL Bhopal plant. The survey identified sixty-one hazards, of which thirty were classified major. It was transmitted to UCC management in Danbury, Connecticut.[^180] It was not in the public domain on December 7, 1984. It would not be in the public domain for another decade and a half. The arrest came first. The audit came later. The order matters.

**译文：**
还有另一份文件。1982年5月，联合碳化物公司设在美国的一个团队完成了对 UCIL 博帕尔工厂的**操作安全调查**（Operational Safety Survey）。该调查识别出61项危险，其中30项被归为重大危险。调查结果被递交给康涅狄格州丹伯里的 UCC 管理层[^180]。1984年12月7日，这份文件不在公共领域。此后十五年，它仍不在公共领域。逮捕在先。审计在后。顺序很重要。

**翻译笔记：**
- Operational Safety Survey → 操作安全调查。专有名称，首次出现括号附英文。
- [^180] 脚注编号原样保留。
- "The order matters" → "顺序很重要"。短句保留原文的道德转折重量。
- Danbury, Connecticut → 康涅狄格州丹伯里。美国地名标准格式。

**原文：**
> We are about to look at what happens when the named actor is genuinely in the chain. It is the hardest case the diagnostic of chapter 3 has to handle, because the easy answer — *he did it* — is in some narrow sense true.

**译文：**
我们即将看到的，是当被指名的行动者确实身处责任链中时会发生什么。这是第3章诊断框架必须面对的最难案例，因为那个轻松的答案——*他确实做了*——在某种狭义上是真实的。

**翻译笔记：**
- "named actor" → "被指名的行动者"。本章核心概念，约出现20次，必须保持一致。
- "*he did it*" → "*他确实做了*"。斜体保留，强调这是简化叙事。

### 蓄意破坏、子公司、董事长

**原文：**
> Three official stories ran from the night of the release into the second decade after it. Each is dated. Each was pierced. Each protected what the prior story had not yet been required to surrender. The sequence is our first instrument.

**译文：**
三个官方叙事从泄漏之夜延续到泄漏后的第二个十年。每个均已过时。每个均被戳穿。每个都守护着前一个叙事尚无需放弃的东西。这个顺序是我们手中的第一件工具。

**翻译笔记：**
- "official stories" → "官方叙事"。story 在本书中不是"故事"，而是"叙事/说法"。
- 三个排比句（Each is dated. Each was pierced. Each protected...）在中文中保持排比：每个均已...每个均被...每个都...

---

**原文：**
> The first story was *sabotage by a disgruntled employee*. In March 1985, Union Carbide commissioned an internal investigation that became the Kalelkar report; in 1988 it commissioned a more detailed analysis from Arthur D. Little, Inc., titled *Report on the Bhopal Methyl Isocyanate Incident*.[^181] Both attributed the release to deliberate **sabotage** — water introduced into Tank E610 by an unnamed worker. The Indian Central Bureau of Investigation did not accept the theory and did not adopt it as the basis of any charging decision. Indian courts did not adopt it. No Indian or US adjudication has ever rested on it. As an explanation it functioned not as a finding but as a hypothesis offered into the journalistic and corporate-legal documentation where the absence of any contrary finding allowed it to persist.

**译文：**
第一个叙事是*心怀不满员工的**蓄意破坏***。1985年3月，联合碳化物公司委托进行了一项内部调查，即卡莱尔卡报告；1988年，它又委托阿瑟·D·利特尔公司进行更详细的分析，题为《博帕尔甲基异氰酸酯事故报告》[^181]。两份报告均将泄漏归因于蓄意破坏——一名未具名工人将水注入 E610 储罐。印度中央调查局不接受这一理论，也未将其作为任何起诉决定的基础。印度法院不接受它。印度或美国的任何司法裁决从未以它为依据。作为一种解释，它的作用并非作为调查发现，而是作为一个被置入新闻和企业法律档案的假说——由于没有任何相反调查发现的存在，它得以持续存在。

**翻译笔记：**
- sabotage → 蓄意破坏。glossary 新术语第1条。原文以斜体强调。中文采用"蓄意"，保留 intent 要素。
- Kalelkar report → 卡莱尔卡报告。人名音译。
- Arthur D. Little, Inc. → 阿瑟·D·利特尔公司。注意英文原名为 Inc.，非 Inc。
- "absence of any contrary finding allowed it to persist" → 将假说的被动持久性翻译为中文的因果表达。

---

**原文：**
> The second story was *the plant failed, not the corporation*. Union Carbide Corporation, the US parent, framed Union Carbide India Limited as an Indian-managed subsidiary operating under Indian regulation. UCC held a 50.9 percent equity interest. The framing acknowledged ownership and denied operational authority. In the United States it took legal form in *In re Union Carbide Corp. Gas Plant Disaster at Bhopal*, 809 F.2d 195 (2d Cir. 1987), affirming the Southern District's dismissal of consolidated US tort suits on forum non conveniens grounds.[^182] The Bhopal disaster, the Second Circuit held, would be litigated in India against UCIL primarily. The chain that ran to UCC Danbury would stay in Danbury; the case would stay in Bhopal.

**译文：**
第二个叙事是*工厂出了事，不是公司的问题*。联合碳化物公司——美国的母公司——将联合碳化物印度有限公司描述为一家在印度监管下运营的印度管理子公司。UCC 持有50.9%的股权。这一框架承认所有权而否认运营权。在美国，这一框架以法律形式出现在*关于联合碳化物公司博帕尔工厂事故案*（In re Union Carbide Corp. Gas Plant Disaster at Bhopal，联邦第二巡回上诉法院，1987年）中——该判决以**不方便法院原则**为由，维持了地区法院对合并美国侵权诉讼的驳回[^182]。第二巡回上诉法院认定，博帕尔事故将在印度以 UCIL 为主要被告进行诉讼。通往丹伯里 UCC 的责任链将留在丹伯里；诉讼将留在博帕尔。

**翻译笔记：**
- forum non conveniens → 不方便法院原则。glossary 新术语第3条。首次出现保留拉丁文原文。
- *In re Union Carbide Corp. Gas Plant Disaster at Bhopal* → 案例名以斜体保留英文，括号附中文翻译。
- 809 F.2d 195 (2d Cir. 1987) → 美国案例引用格式，保留英文。
- "The chain that ran to UCC Danbury" → "通往丹伯里 UCC 的责任链"。chain 译为"责任链"——与 glossary 一致。

---

**原文：**
> The third story was *Anderson is the responsibility-holder*. The decade-long extradition pursuit, formally filed in 2003, displaced into public attention the question of *whether Anderson would be brought to court* and away from the question of *what UCC Danbury had known and decided*. The pursuit was real. The personification was real. The two stood in for each other.

**译文：**
第三个叙事是*安德森是责任承担者*。长达十年的引渡追索——于2003年正式提起——将公众注意力转移到*安德森是否会被带上法庭*的问题上，从而偏离了*丹伯里 UCC 知情并决定了什么*的问题。追索是真实的。人格化是真实的。两者互为替代。

**翻译笔记：**
- "responsibility-holder" → "责任承担者"。注意与 cost-bearing goat 的"承担代价"不完全相同，此处指"被认定为负有责任的人"。
- "The two stood in for each other" → "两者互为替代"。原文的微妙含义：追索行动和人格化互相支撑、互相替代。

---

**原文：**
> Three stories. Each ran for years. Each moved blame one layer — to a saboteur, to the subsidiary, to the chairman. Together they constructed the partial-scapegoat alibi.

**译文：**
三个叙事。每个持续多年。每个将责任向上移动一层——给蓄意破坏者、给子公司、给董事长。共同构建了部分替罪羊的托辞。

**翻译笔记：**
- "moved blame one layer" → "将责任向上移动一层"。原文 one layer 并非指横向移动，而是沿着责任链向上推一层。
- partial-scapegoat → 部分替罪羊。glossary 已有术语。

### 1982年5月的安全调查

**原文：**
> The diagnostic of chapter 3 — its eight-question structure — works on cases where the named actor is innocent or peripheral. The harder case is the one in front of us. The named actor is in the chain. He really did sign things. He really was in charge. The pure-scapegoat shape that closes a case by accusing a wholly innocent substitute is the easier laundering to detect; the partial-scapegoat shape that closes a case by accusing a genuinely involved actor is the laundering we install here.

**译文：**
第3章的诊断框架——它的八问结构——在被指名的行动者是无辜或边缘角色的案例中行之有效。更难的案例是眼前的这一个。被指名的行动者在责任链中。他真的签过东西。他真的负责过。纯粹替罪羊的形态——通过指控一个完全无辜的替代者来结案——是更容易被识别的洗白；部分替罪羊的形态——通过指控一个确实有参与的行动者来结案——是我们在这里要安装的诊断工具。

**翻译笔记：**
- pure-scapegoat → 纯粹替罪羊。glossary 已有。
- partial-scapegoat → 部分替罪羊。glossary 已有。
- "the laundering we install here" → "我们在这里要安装的诊断工具"。install 是本书方法论隐喻（安装诊断能力），保留此隐喻。

---

**原文：**
> A definition, plainly. A **partial scapegoat** is a named actor whose culpability at the execution or local-decision layer is real, but at whom the chain of attribution is halted by design so that responsibility cannot climb to the design layer, the cost-cutting layer, the regulatory layer, the documentation-curating layer above. The diagnostic question is not *did the named actor do anything?* but *did the chain stop where it stopped because the named actor's culpability is commensurate with the harm, or because their culpability is the boundary the chain was designed to halt at?*

**译文：**
一个明确定义。**部分替罪羊**，是一个被指名的行动者——其在执行层或地方决策层的可责性是真实的，但归责链被刻意截停在该行动者处，使责任无法向上攀爬到设计层、成本削减层、监管层和档案编纂层。诊断问题不是*被指名的行动者做了什么吗？*而是*归责链停在了它所停之处，是因为该行动者的可责性与危害相当，还是因为该行动者的可责性是这条链被设计来截停的边界？*

**翻译笔记：**
- "culpability" → "可责性"（区别于 guilt 的"有罪"——culpability 是更广的责任概念）。
- "by design" → "被刻意"（保留原文的意图含义，暗示这是制度设计的结果而非偶然）。
- "halted" → "截停"（与 interception 的"拦截"区分——此处是主动停止）。
- "commensurate with" → "与...相当"。

---

**原文：**
> The Bhopal chain, walked one layer at a time:
>
> At the level of *control*, design authority for the methyl-isocyanate unit lived at UCC's engineering department in South Charleston, West Virginia. UCC controlled, by majority equity and by technical service agreements, the safety standards and the budget envelope inside which UCIL operated. UCIL local management exercised day-to-day operational control of the plant, including the decision to keep the Tank E610 refrigeration unit off through 1984.
>
> At the level of *benefit*, dividend extraction flowed from UCIL to UCC. The cost-reduction program that reduced safety staffing and maintenance at Bhopal in 1983 and 1984 saved money at the subsidiary that was reported at the parent.
>
> At the level of *knowledge*, the May 1982 UCC Operational Safety Survey identified sixty-one hazards at Bhopal, thirty of them major, and was transmitted to UCC management at Danbury. The transmittal chain inside Danbury — who at the parent received the report, what action followed — is the load-bearing document our evidence still partially depends on. *Sahu v. Union Carbide Corporation*, litigated in the Southern District of New York from 1999 through 2014, introduced internal UCC engineering documents into the US court docket. The 2014 dismissal turned on statute-of-limitations grounds rather than on the substantive parent-knowledge question.
>
> At the level of *preventability*, four decisions stand out. The refrigeration system on Tank E610 was disabled in mid-1984. The vent gas scrubber was undersized for the actual release rate by design. The flare tower was offline for maintenance on the night of release. The storage volume in Tank E610 exceeded UCC's own operational guidance. Each is a decision; each was made; each could have been made differently.
>
> At the level of *record*, the relevant archives sit in jurisdictions whose interests in closing the case did not align with the interests of the cost bearers. UCC archives lived at Danbury, then in Dow Chemical's custody after the 2001 acquisition. UCIL records were seized by the Government of India. The 1989 settlement record was controlled jointly by UCC and the Government of India; the survivor groups did not control it.
>
> At the level of *cost*, the Government of India told its Supreme Court in 1991 that more than three thousand people had died in the first days. Indian Council of Medical Research longitudinal studies and state government figures place total disaster-attributable deaths across the years 1984 to 1994 in a range of fifteen thousand to twenty-five thousand. More than five hundred thousand people were exposed and entered the ICMR cohorts. The 1989 **settlement** was $470 million. Site contamination at the abandoned plant has been documented by Greenpeace in *The Bhopal Legacy* (1999) and by the Centre for Science and Environment in its 2009 study, *Contamination of Soil and Water inside and outside the Union Carbide India Limited, Bhopal*. Remediation cost has not been accepted.[^183]

**译文：**
博帕尔的责任链，逐层走一遍：

在*控制*层面，甲基异氰酸酯装置的设计权限在西弗吉尼亚州南查尔斯顿的 UCC 工程部。UCC 通过多数股权和技术服务协议，控制着 UCIL 运营的安全标准和预算空间。UCIL 地方管理层行使工厂的日常运营控制权——包括在1984年全年保持 E610 储罐制冷系统关闭的决定。

在*受益*层面，股息提取从 UCIL 流向 UCC。1983和1984年间减少博帕尔安全人员和维护的**成本削减**计划，在子公司层面节省了资金，而这些节省被报告给了母公司。

在*知情*层面，1982年5月的 UCC 操作安全调查在博帕尔识别出61项危险——其中30项为重大危险——并被递交给丹伯里的 UCC 管理层。丹伯里内部的传递链——母公司中谁收到了报告、后续采取了什么行动——是我们的证据仍部分依赖的支撑性文件。*萨胡诉联合碳化物公司案*（Sahu v. Union Carbide Corporation），自1999年至2014年在纽约南区法院诉讼，将 UCC 内部工程文件引入了美国法庭记录。2014年的驳回以**诉讼时效**为由，而非实体性的母公司知情问题。

在*可预防性*层面，四项决定尤为突出。E610 储罐的制冷系统在1984年中停用。排气洗涤器在设计上对实际泄漏率而言尺寸不足。火炬塔在泄漏当晚因维护而离线。E610 储罐的储存量超过了 UCC 自身的操作指引。每一项都是一个决定；每一项都被做出了；每一项本可以有不同的结果。

The single sharpest move against it is the one the Lion Air families made when they pressed for MCAS to be in the training manual and the AOA Disagree alert to be standard: aim one floor up, in writing. When a workplace investigation names a person as the cause, ask, in writing, what decision was upstream and who made it. A record that names the next floor cannot easily close at yours without saying so.

**译文：**
当一份附有我们名字的结案到来时，这个模式就触及了我们自己的楼层。我们在一条责任链中确实有参与——这条链有拥有更多控制、受益、知情和可预防性的上游行动者；记录控制者正将我们定位为一个事件的最可见行动者——而该事件的原因是在我们之上的层面决定的；并且在我们之上的人们没有被要求签署任何东西。警示信号正是这种不对称——在一条顶部空白的链的底端，要求一个唯一的签名。对抗它的最尖锐的一招，正是狮子航空遇难者家属所做的——当他们在训练手册中加入 MCAS、将 AOA 不一致警示设为标准配置时所做的：向上一个层面瞄准，以书面形式。当一个职场调查将一个人指认为原因时，以书面形式问，上游是什么决策、谁做的。一份指名了上一层的记录，不能轻易——不声明的话——在你这一层结案。
