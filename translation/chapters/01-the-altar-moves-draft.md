# 第 1 章 — 祭坛移动

## 题记

**原文：**
> The killer of a learned Brāhmaṇa throws his guilt on him who eats his food, an adulterous wife on her (negligent) husband, a (sinning) pupil or sacrificer on (their negligent) teacher (or priest), a thief on the king (who pardons him).
>
> — The Laws of Manu 8.317, trans. Georg Bühler, *Sacred Books of the East*, vol. 25 (1886)

**译文：**
> 杀害博学婆罗门者，将其罪投于食其食之人；通奸之妻，将其罪投于（疏忽之）夫；（有罪之）弟子或献祭者，将其罪投于（疏忽之）师（或祭司）；盗贼，将其罪投于（宽恕其之）国王。
>
> ——《摩奴法典》8.317，格奥尔格·比勒译，《东方圣书》第 25 卷（1886 年）

**翻译笔记：**
- [TERM-NOTE] "Brāhmaṇa" 保留梵文长音符，中文通用「婆罗门」
- "throws his guilt on" 译为「将罪投于」而非「归罪于」，保留原文的「投掷/转移」动作隐喻
- 括号中的修饰语（negligent, sinning）在原文中即带括号，保留此形式

## 开场场景

**原文：**
> Shortly after nine o'clock on the night of 27 February 1933, the Reichstag in Berlin was on fire. The glow was visible from the Brandenburg Gate. Police entered the building through a side door; smoke was already coming out under the cornices. In one of the lower chambers they found a young man, bare-chested, his shirt and jacket abandoned in a corridor where he had used them to start the flames. He was carrying matches and a packet of firelighters. He did not run.
>
> His name was Marinus van der Lubbe. He was twenty-four years old, Dutch, a former bricklayer, a council communist whose first language was not German, who could not see well, and who had walked across half of Europe to be in Berlin that month. At arrest he gave his name and confessed at once. He had set the fire himself, he said, alone, as a signal to the German working class to rise against the new chancellor, who had been in office for less than four weeks. He repeated the confession through the night.
>
> By dawn, four thousand other people in Prussia were under arrest. None of them had been inside the Reichstag.

**译文：**
1933 年 2 月 27 日夜间，刚过九点，柏林的国会大厦燃起大火。火光从勃兰登堡门就能看见。警察从侧门进入大楼；檐口下已经冒出烟来。在一个下层房间中，他们发现了一个年轻男子，赤着上身，他的衬衫和夹克被丢弃在走廊里——他曾用它们引火。他身上带着火柴和一包火种。他没有跑。

他叫马里努斯·范·德·卢贝。二十四岁，荷兰人，曾做过砖瓦工，是一名委员会共产主义者，母语不是德语，视力不好，在那个月步行穿越了半个欧洲来到柏林。被捕时他报上姓名，立刻认罪。火是他放的，他说，独自一人，作为向德国工人阶级发出的信号，号召他们起来反抗那位上任不到四周的新总理。他整夜重复着这个供词。

破晓时分，普鲁士另有四千人被捕。他们中没有一个人进过国会大厦。

**翻译笔记：**
- 原文用短句、具体细节构建场景感。中文保留同样节奏：短句，具体，不煽情。
- "council communist" 译为「委员会共产主义者」——指反对列宁主义先锋党模式、主张工人委员会直接民主的共产主义流派。这是政治学术语，非「苏维埃共产主义者」。
- "firelighters" 译为「火种」——在此语境中指引火物/点火棒，非「打火机」。
- "He did not run." 单独成句。原文以三个词的短句结束第一段叙事——这是道德转折的隐含信号。中文「他没有跑」同样三字短句，保留这种戛然而止的节奏。
- "None of them had been inside the Reichstag." 单独成段。这是一个关键的对位——一个人认罪，四千人被捕，但四千人都不在案发现场。中文保留单独成段和短句形式。

## 四千人破晓被捕

**原文：**
> The first version of the story did not wait for the fire to be out. Within hours, Hermann Göring, who was both Reich Minister without Portfolio and Reich Commissioner for the Prussian Interior, announced that the fire was the opening signal of a Communist insurrection. Van der Lubbe, the official statement went, was an agent of the Communist Party of Germany (the KPD), coordinating with the Communist International in Moscow. The KPD had been planning the uprising for weeks. The fire was the match.
>
> The Prussian political police acted on the announcement before they finished investigating the building. Arrest lists already drawn up for emergency use were issued through the night of 27 / 28 February. By the morning of the 28th, roughly four thousand KPD officials, deputies, journalists, and members had been taken into custody in Prussia alone — a figure reconstructed by Richard J. Evans in *The Coming of the Third Reich* (2003) from the surviving Reich Security Main Office files held at the Bundesarchiv as record group R 58[<sup>1</sup>](#c1-1). Newspapers in friendly hands carried the conspiracy framing the same morning.
>
> That morning, 28 February 1933, President Paul von Hindenburg signed the *Verordnung des Reichspräsidenten zum Schutz von Volk und Staat* — the Reichstag Fire Decree — published in *Reichsgesetzblatt* I 1933, page 83[<sup>2</sup>](#c1-2). The decree, two pages long, suspended seven articles of the Weimar Constitution: personal liberty, freedom of expression, freedom of the press, freedom of association and assembly, the privacy of postal and telephonic communication, the inviolability of the home, the inviolability of property. It authorised the Reich government to override the constituent states. It provided the legal scaffolding for preventive detention of political opponents. It contained no sunset. It was renewed routinely and remained in operative legal force for the next twelve years.
>
> Three weeks later, on 23 March 1933, the Reichstag — meeting at the Kroll Opera House because its own building was uninhabitable — passed the *Gesetz zur Behebung der Not von Volk und Reich*, the Enabling Act, conferring legislative authority on the Reich Cabinet for four years and permitting cabinet decrees that deviated from the Constitution itself. The Act was published the following day in *Reichsgesetzblatt* I 1933, page 141[<sup>3</sup>](#c1-3). The vote was carried by 444 to 94. The 94 dissenters were Social Democrats. The 81 deputies of the KPD, who had been elected on 5 March, were absent; almost all of them had been arrested under the decree signed three weeks earlier, under the legal authority created by the fire one man had confessed to setting.
>
> This was the story as the regime presented it through the spring and summer of 1933. A fire. A foreign communist. A conspiracy. A decree. A law. A new order. Each step had a name. The name at the centre of the story, the name on which the rest of the structure was hung, was van der Lubbe's.

**译文：**
这个故事的第一个版本没有等到火被扑灭。几小时内，同时担任不管部部长和普鲁士内政专员的赫尔曼·戈林宣布，这场火是共产主义起义的开场信号。根据官方声明，范·德·卢贝是德国共产党（KPD）的特工，与莫斯科的共产国际协调行动。德共已经策划这场起义数周。火就是火柴。

普鲁士政治警察在尚未完成对大楼的调查之前便依据这一声明采取了行动。早已备好供紧急使用的逮捕名单在 2 月 27 日至 28 日的夜间签发。到 28 日上午，仅在普鲁士就有约四千名德共干部、议员、记者和党员被拘禁——这一数字由理查德·J·埃文斯在《第三帝国的到来》（2003 年）中根据现存于联邦档案馆的帝国保安总局档案 R 58 号记录组重建[<sup>1</sup>](#c1-1)。立场友善的报纸在同日早晨刊载了阴谋论框架。

同日上午，1933 年 2 月 28 日，总统保罗·冯·兴登堡签署了《保护人民和国家的帝国总统令》——即国会纵火法令——刊载于《帝国法律公报》1933 年第一卷第 83 页[<sup>2</sup>](#c1-2)。这份两页的法令中止了魏玛宪法的七个条款：人身自由、言论自由、新闻自由、结社和集会自由、邮政和电信通信隐私、住宅不可侵犯、财产不可侵犯。它授权帝国政府凌驾于各组成邦之上。它为政治反对者的预防性拘留提供了法律框架。它没有日落条款。它被例行延展，并在接下来的十二年中持续有效。

三周后，1933 年 3 月 23 日，国会——因自己的大楼无法使用而在克罗尔歌剧院开会——通过了《消除人民和国家痛苦法》，即授权法，将立法权授予帝国内阁四年，并允许内阁颁布偏离宪法本身的法令。该法于次日刊载于《帝国法律公报》1933 年第一卷第 141 页[<sup>3</sup>](#c1-3)。投票以 444 票对 94 票通过。94 名反对者是社民党人。3 月 5 日当选的 81 名德共议员缺席；几乎所有人都在三周前签署的法令下被捕——而该法令的法律依据，正是一场由一个认罪者设置的火所创造的。

这就是该政权在 1933 年春夏所呈现的故事。一场火。一个外国共产党人。一场阴谋。一份法令。一部法律。一个新秩序。每一步都有一个名字。故事中心的名字，整个结构悬挂于其上的名字，是范·德·卢贝的。

**翻译笔记：**
- "Reich Minister without Portfolio" 译为「不管部部长」——中文政治学术语中「不管部」即无特定部门的部长
- "The fire was the match." ——原文在此处使用双关（match = 火柴/引爆点）。中文「火就是火柴」保留了这种简短有力的判断句形式，但双关含义有所损失 `[AMBIGUOUS: 原文 match 兼有"火柴"和"引爆点/导火索"之意，中文"火柴"无法完全传达。可能的替代："火就是那根火柴"、"这把火就是引信"。建议 Glossary Master 裁决]`
- "It contained no sunset." ——「sunset」在此指法律中的日落条款（自动失效日期）。中文直接译为「它没有日落条款」，并在术语表中不需要单独收录——这是通用法律术语。
- "A fire. A foreign communist. A conspiracy. A decree. A law. A new order." ——原文以六个短名词短语收束此段，形成锤击节奏。中文保留六句短促的名词判断，不拉长，不加连词。
- "the name on which the rest of the structure was hung" ——「hung」在此是悬挂/依附的隐喻。译为「悬挂」保留了这个空间隐喻（整个法律/政治结构悬挂在一个人名上）。

## 廷代尔的山羊之词

**原文：**
> There is a word in English for what the Nazi government did with van der Lubbe in the first week of March 1933, and the word is older than the German state.
>
> In 1530, in Antwerp, an exiled English priest named William Tyndale was translating the Pentateuch from Hebrew into English for the first time. When he came to a passage in Leviticus describing an annual rite of purgation — two goats brought before the priest, one slaughtered as a sin offering, the other sent alive into the wilderness carrying the iniquities of the community — he needed a word for the second goat. The Hebrew text gave the destination of that second goat as *Azazel*, a word the rabbinic tradition had already been arguing over for a thousand years. Tyndale read *Azazel* as a compound: *ez ozel*, the goat that goes away, the escape-goat. In his English Pentateuch of 1530 he wrote *scapegoate*. Eighty years later the King James translators kept the word; the transmission is documented in David Daniell's 1992 Yale critical edition of Tyndale's Old Testament[<sup>4</sup>](#c1-4).
>
> Much modern Hebrew Bible scholarship treats Tyndale's compound etymology as philologically strained. Jacob Milgrom's Anchor Bible commentary reads *Azazel* as the name of a demonic being inhabiting the wilderness, not a phrase describing the goat itself; the demon-name reading is widely held but not universal among contemporary commentators[<sup>5</sup>](#c1-5). The word is, by current scholarly judgement, a misreading. But the misreading went into English at the historical moment English was acquiring its modern shape, and it remained there. By the early nineteenth century the noun *scapegoat* had migrated out of the Bible into general use to name any person made to carry blame that belongs to others; the *Oxford English Dictionary* dates the first figurative use to 1824, in a passage describing country-boys who "bear their fate as scape-goats, (for all sins whatsoever are laid as matters of course to their door,..), with amazing resignation"[<sup>6</sup>](#c1-6).
>
> This is how the English word scapegoat arrived. It carries, in its morphology, the Bronze Age purgation ritual that produced it. It is a ritual term that became a political one without anyone deciding to translate it again.
>
> The practice the word names is the same practice, and it has not stopped. What has changed is the altar.
>
> The ancient rite was a substitution that named itself. The priest declared it. The goat was the goat. The community watched. The modern case does not declare it; it does not need to. The substitution has gone underground into the morning's headline, into the press release, into the docket number. The same machine, the same product — only the part where the substitution is named has been deleted.

**译文：**
英语中有一个词，用来描述纳粹政府在 1933 年 3 月第一周对范·德·卢贝所做的这件事，而这个词比德意志国家本身更古老。

1530 年，在安特卫普，一位流亡的英国神父威廉·廷代尔正在首次将摩西五经从希伯来语翻译成英语。当他译到《利未记》中描述的一项年度净罪仪式时——两只山羊被带到祭司面前，一只被宰杀为赎罪祭，另一只被活着送入旷野，背负整个社群的罪孽——他需要一个词来指称第二只山羊。希伯来原文将第二只山羊的去处写为 *Azazel*（阿撒泻勒），一个拉比传统已经争论了一千年的词。廷代尔将 *Azazel* 解读为一个合成词：*ez ozel*，离开的山羊，the escape-goat。在他 1530 年的英语摩西五经中，他写下了 *scapegoate*。八十年后，钦定本的译者保留了这个词；这一传承在大卫·丹尼尔 1992 年耶鲁版廷代尔旧约校订本中有据可查[<sup>4</sup>](#c1-4)。

现代希伯来圣经学界大多认为廷代尔的合成词源学在语文学上是牵强的。雅各布·米尔格罗姆的锚圣经（Anchor Bible）注释将 *Azazel* 解读为居住在旷野中的一个恶魔存在的名字，而非描述山羊本身的短语；恶魔名解读在当代注释者中被广泛接受但并非一致[<sup>5</sup>](#c1-5)。按照当前的学术判断，这个词是一个误读。但这个误读在英语获得其现代形态的历史时刻进入了英语，并留在了那里。到十九世纪初，名词 *scapegoat* 已经从圣经迁移到一般用法中，用来指代任何被迫承担属于他人的指责的人；《牛津英语词典》将其首次比喻用法追溯至 1824 年，一段描述乡村男孩的文字，他们"以惊人的顺从承受自己的命运，作为替罪羊（scape-goats），（因为一切罪孽无论如何都理所当然地算在他们头上……）"[<sup>6</sup>](#c1-6)。

这就是英语单词 scapegoat 的由来。它在词形中携带着造就它的青铜时代净罪仪式。它是一个仪式术语，在没有人决定再次翻译它的情况下变成了一个政治术语。

这个词所命名的实践是同一个实践，而它从未停止。改变的是祭坛。

古代仪式是一种自我命名的替代。祭司宣告它。山羊就是山羊。社群观看。现代案例不宣告它；它不需要。替代已经潜入地下，潜入清晨的头条新闻，潜入新闻稿，潜入案卷编号。同样的机器，同样的产品——只是替代被命名的那个部分被删除了。

**翻译笔记：**
- "scapegoate" → Tyndale 1530 年创造的原始拼写，保留英文原文
- "escape-goat" → 保留英文原文，因为这是 Tyndale 的原创合成词，是理解 scapegoat 词源的关键
- "Azazel" → 首次出现时保留原文并括号注中文「阿撒泻勒」
- "the goat that goes away" → 译为「离开的山羊」。这是 Tyndale 对 Azazel 的解读，不是正式的翻译，而是一种词源学猜想。保留英文 "the escape-goat" 以显示 Tyndale 的原始措辞。
- "The practice the word names is the same practice, and it has not stopped." → 这是本章的核心论点句之一。译为「这个词所命名的实践是同一个实践，而它从未停止。」保留原文的重复结构（practice...practice）和短促的判断句。
- "What has changed is the altar." → 「改变的是祭坛。」全书的标题句。保持单独成句，简短有力。
- "The same machine, the same product — only the part where the substitution is named has been deleted." → 译为「同样的机器，同样的产品——只是替代被命名的那个部分被删除了。」保留原文的破折号和机器隐喻。「deleted」译为「删除」而非「消失」——强调这是一个主动的删除动作，而非自然的消失。

## 利未记、雅典、巴比伦、罗马

**原文：**

> Leviticus 16 is a ritual text. It does not argue; it instructs. On the tenth day of the seventh month, the Day of Atonement, Aaron the priest is to bring two goats to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting and to cast lots over them. One lot reads "for the LORD" — that goat is offered as a sin offering. The other lot reads "for Azazel" — that goat is "set alive before the LORD, to make atonement over it, to send it away into the wilderness to Azazel" (v. 10, JPS translation)[<sup>7</sup>](#c1-7).
>
> Then come the two pivotal verses:
>
> > Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat and confess over it all the iniquities and transgressions of the Israelites, whatever their sins, putting them on the head of the goat; and it shall be sent off to the wilderness through a designated man. Thus the goat shall carry on it all their iniquities to an inaccessible region; and the goat shall be set free in the wilderness[<sup>8</sup>](#c1-8).
>
> The architecture is exact. The priest is named. The hands are placed. The iniquities are confessed aloud and transferred to the goat by physical contact. The destination is named — *Azazel*, the demon or the place, the text will not say which. A specific man, designated, leads the goat out. The land is "cut off". The goat does not return. The community is purged for the year. Next year it is performed again. The ritual is annual; the cycle does not close.
>
> This is openly substitutionary. The community knows. The priest knows. The goat is not pretending to be anything other than what it is. Iniquities are not the goat's iniquities. They are the community's. The goat is the bearer.
>
> Roughly a thousand miles to the west, on the sixth day of the month Thargelion, the Athenians performed a related rite. The earliest surviving description is from Hipponax of Ephesus, who wrote in the sixth century BCE; the surviving fragments are collected in M. L. West's Oxford Classical Text[<sup>9</sup>](#c1-9). A marginal person — destitute, deformed, sometimes criminal, often a foreigner — was selected to serve as the city's *pharmakos*. Hipponax says he was fed at public expense in the days before the rite. On the day, he was led in procession through the city, beaten with branches of squill and figs, and driven out beyond the walls. In some sources he was killed; in others he was only expelled; the scholarship is divided on which formed the standard practice, as Jan Bremmer set out in *Harvard Studies in Classical Philology* in 1983 and as Dennis Hughes mapped at book length in *Human Sacrifice in Ancient Greece* in 1991[<sup>10</sup>](#c1-10). The function is undisputed: the *pharmakos* was understood to carry the city's pollution out with him. He absorbed it physically. He was the *pharmakos*; the city was the city; nobody confused which was which.
>
> In Babylon a similar grammar was performed at the *Akitu*, the New Year festival, in a rite the Akkadian scribes called *kuppuru* — to wipe clean. A *mašmaššu* priest cleansed the temple by passing a goat or sheep over the surfaces, the animal taking on the impurities of the place; the carcass was then disposed of in the Euphrates. The *Anchor Bible Dictionary*'s entry on Atonement sets out the *kuppuru* ritual and its etymology; Walter Burkert's *Structure and History in Greek Mythology and Ritual* (1979) reads the Babylonian rite alongside the Greek and Levantine parallels[<sup>11</sup>](#c1-11). The Akkadian *kuppuru* and the Hebrew *kipper* — atone — share a root.
>
> In Rome the same impulse named a month. The *Februa* were the lustration rites by which the city periodically purged itself; the Luperci ran the boundaries; the month of February took its name from the verb *februare*, to purify, as Ovid sets out in *Fasti* II.31 and following and as Plutarch describes in his *Life of Romulus*, chapter 21[<sup>12</sup>](#c1-12).
>
> The grammar is regional. Anatolia, Syria-Palestine, Mesopotamia, the Aegean, Italy — the substitution rite is a Bronze Age and Iron Age technology of communal hygiene, available in several languages at once. It survives in Leviticus 16 because Leviticus 16 was canonised; the others survive in fragments because their cultures were canonised by others. But the rite itself, as a way for a community to discharge accumulated pollution onto a designated bearer, was something the ancient world took as obvious.
>
> In every version, the substitution is named. The community holds the rite knowingly. The priest, the elders, the *mašmaššu*, the Luperci, the citizen body of Athens — these are not deceiving themselves. They have a problem (sin, plague, miasma, civic crisis), they have a method (transfer the problem to a bearer), and they have an outcome (the bearer leaves; the community continues). The ritual's openness is not a flaw. It is the technology.
>
> The point of the long detour to the ancient world is not that Berlin in 1933 was a city performing the Thargelia. Berlin did not know it was performing anything. The point is that the practice predates the politics by three thousand years, and that the modern case lost only one feature of it. The modern case lost the naming.

**译文：**

《利未记》第 16 章是一份仪式文本。它不是论证；它是指令。七月初十，赎罪日，祭司亚伦要带两只山羊到会幕门口，为它们抽签。一签写着"归耶和华"——那只山羊献为赎罪祭。另一签写着"归阿撒泻勒"——那只山羊"要活着安置在耶和华面前，用以赎罪，送到旷野去，归阿撒泻勒"（第 10 节，JPS 译本）[<sup>7</sup>](#c1-7)。

然后是两个关键的经文：

> 亚伦要把双手按在那只活山羊的头上，承认以色列人的一切罪孽和过犯，就是他们一切的罪恶，把这些都归在羊头上，然后通过所派的人将其送到旷野里去。这样，山羊就把他们的一切罪孽都带到与世隔绝之地；那人就把山羊放到旷野里[<sup>8</sup>](#c1-8)。

这个架构是精确的。祭司有名有姓。双手按上去。罪孽被大声承认，通过身体接触转移到山羊身上。去处被命名——*阿撒泻勒*，要么是恶魔要么是地名，经文不会告诉我们究竟是哪一个。一个特定的人，被指定的人，带领山羊出去。那片土地是"隔绝的"。山羊不返回。社群被洁净一年。下一年再执行一次。仪式是年度的；循环不闭合。

这是一个公开的替代仪式。社群知道。祭司知道。山羊并不假装自己是什么别的东西。罪孽不是山羊的罪孽。它们是社群的。山羊是承担者。

向西大约一千英里，在塔格利昂月（Thargelion）的第六天，雅典人举行了一个相关的仪式。最早的现存描述出自以弗所的希波纳克斯（Hipponax of Ephesus），他在公元前六世纪写作；残存片段收录在 M·L·韦斯特（M. L. West）的牛津古典文本中[<sup>9</sup>](#c1-9)。一个边缘人物——贫困、畸形、有时是罪犯、常常是外国人——被选中充当城邦的 *pharmakos*（替罪人）。希波纳克斯说他在仪式前几天由公费供养。到了那一天，他被列队穿城游行，被海葱和无花果的枝条抽打，然后被驱逐出城墙之外。在一些记载中他被杀死；在另一些记载中他只是被驱逐；学术界对哪一种才是标准做法存在分歧，正如扬·布雷默（Jan Bremmer）在 1983 年的《哈佛古典语言学研究》中所论述，以及丹尼斯·休斯（Dennis Hughes）在 1991 年的专著《古希腊的人祭》中系统整理的那样[<sup>10</sup>](#c1-10)。其功能没有争议：*pharmakos* 被认为将城邦的污染随他一同带走。他以身体吸收这些污染。他是 *pharmakos*；城邦是城邦；没有人搞混谁是谁。

在巴比伦，类似的语法在 *Akitu*（新年节庆）中上演，其仪式被阿卡德文士称为 *kuppuru*——意为擦净。一位 *mašmaššu*（净化祭司）通过用山羊或绵羊擦拭殿堂表面来洁净庙宇，动物承载此处的污秽；尸体随后被投入幼发拉底河。《锚圣经词典》（Anchor Bible Dictionary）"赎罪"词条记录了 *kuppuru* 仪式及其词源；沃尔特·伯克特（Walter Burkert）的《希腊神话与仪式的结构与历史》（1979）将巴比伦仪式与希腊和黎凡特的对应物并列解读[<sup>11</sup>](#c1-11)。阿卡德语 *kuppuru* 和希伯来语 *kipper*（赎罪）共享一个词根。

在罗马，同样的冲动为一个月份命了名。*Februa* 是城邦用来定期自我净化的净礼；Luperci 祭司团沿边界奔跑；二月（February）这个月的名称来自动词 *februare*（净化），如奥维德（Ovid）在《岁时记》（*Fasti*）第二卷第 31 行及之后所阐述，普鲁塔克（Plutarch）在其《罗慕路斯传》第 21 章中也有描述[<sup>12</sup>](#c1-12)。

这种语法有其地域性。安纳托利亚、叙利亚-巴勒斯坦、美索不达米亚、爱琴海、意大利——替代仪式是一种青铜时代和铁器时代的社群卫生技术，同时在多种语言中存在。它在《利未记》第 16 章中存续下来，是因为该章被纳入了正典；其他的则以碎片形式存续，是因为它们的文化被他者纳入了正典。但仪式本身——作为一个社群将积累的污秽排放到指定承担者身上的方式——是古代世界视为理所当然的事。

在每个版本中，替代都被命名。社群明知地举行仪式。祭司、长老、*mašmaššu*、Luperci 祭司团、雅典的公民体——这些人不是在欺骗自己。他们有一个问题（罪孽、瘟疫、污染、城邦危机），他们有一个方法（将问题转移给一个承担者），他们有一个结果（承担者离开；社群继续）。仪式的公开性不是一个缺陷。它就是技术本身。

绕道古代世界的漫长旅程，其要点并不在于 1933 年的柏林是一座正在举行塔格利昂仪式的城市。柏林并不知道自己在举行任何仪式。要点在于，这种实践比政治早了三千年，而现代案例只丢失了它的一个特征。现代案例丢失了命名。

## 一部为一个人制定的追溯性法律

**原文：**

> After the fire, in the months that followed, the alibi went into the record.
>
> The Prussian political police — Section IA, the predecessor of the Gestapo, under Rudolf Diels — controlled the investigative file. The Reich Propaganda Ministry, established 13 March 1933 under Joseph Goebbels, controlled the press framing. The *Reichsgericht*, the Reich Supreme Court at Leipzig, controlled the legal record. Each of these institutions did its work. None of them were independent in any sense the Weimar Constitution would have recognised six months earlier.
>
> In the weeks after the arrest the Prussian prosecutors widened the charge from arson to *Hochverrat*, high treason, and joined four more defendants to van der Lubbe's case: Ernst Torgler, the KPD parliamentary leader, who had turned himself in to clear his name; and three Bulgarians who had been in Berlin on Comintern business — Georgi Dimitrov, Blagoi Popov, and Vasil Tanev. None of them had been in the Reichstag on the night of the fire. They were the human shape of the alleged conspiracy.
>
> On 29 March 1933, six weeks after the fire, the regime passed a retroactive amendment to the Reich Penal Code. The *Gesetz über Verhängung und Vollzug der Todesstrafe* — published in *Reichsgesetzblatt* I 1933, Nr. 28, page 151[<sup>13</sup>](#c1-13) — raised the penalty for arson against public buildings from penitentiary confinement to death and made the higher penalty applicable to acts committed before the law was passed. The law is known in the historical literature as the *Lex van der Lubbe*. It existed so that one man, already in custody, could be killed for an act that, on the night he committed it, did not carry the death penalty.
>
> The trial opened in Leipzig on 21 September 1933 and ran until 23 December[<sup>14</sup>](#c1-14). It was reported across Europe. Dimitrov, defending himself from the dock in German he had taught himself in jail, cross-examined Göring and reduced the prosecution's conspiracy case to public embarrassment. Van der Lubbe sat through most of it with his head down. The court acquitted Dimitrov, Popov, Tanev, and Torgler for want of evidence. It convicted van der Lubbe alone of *Hochverrat* and arson against a public building. He was sentenced to death.
>
> The acquittal of the four did the alibi a service the prosecution had not intended. A court that lost four-fifths of its own case had, in the foreign press, acquired the look of one that weighed the evidence. In the foreign press the trial began to look fair. Four communists had walked. Only the man with the matches had been condemned. The international story shifted in the regime's favour: this was not a show trial; this was a court doing its work; the man who had set the fire had been convicted, the men against whom there was no case had been let go. The regime did not need to maintain the conspiracy in detail any longer. By the time of the Leipzig verdict, the Enabling Act had been operative for nine months. The decree on which it stood had been operative for ten. The justification could fade. The legal architecture would not.
>
> On 10 January 1934, three days before his twenty-fifth birthday, van der Lubbe was guillotined in the prison courtyard at Leipzig. He had spent the trial nearly silent. He had spent the night of the fire shouting his confession.
>
> The case did not stay closed.
>
> After 1945, with the Reich gone and its files moved into the *Bundesarchiv* and into the captured-records collection at the United States National Archives, German historians began to reconstruct what had happened in the building on the night of 27 February. The reconstruction continues. It is not, by 2026, a settled question.
>
> The first major postwar challenge to the received story came from an unlikely place. Fritz Tobias, an official in the Lower Saxony state office for the protection of the constitution, published *Der Reichstagsbrand: Legende und Wirklichkeit* in 1962[<sup>15</sup>](#c1-15). Tobias argued, against the prevailing Cold War-era assumption on both sides of the Iron Curtain that the Nazis had set the fire themselves, that the simplest reading of the surviving evidence was the one the regime itself had abandoned by 1934: van der Lubbe had acted alone. The thesis was unwelcome in both East Germany, where Nazi authorship of the fire was a doctrinal pillar, and in much of the West, where the convenience of Nazi authorship had hardened into common sense. Hans Mommsen, in a 1964 *Vierteljahrshefte für Zeitgeschichte* article later expanded for an English-language volume in 1972, defended Tobias's reading on different evidentiary grounds[<sup>16</sup>](#c1-16).
>
> Other historians disagreed. Walther Hofer led an international scholarly committee — the *Internationaler Komitee zur wissenschaftlichen Erforschung der Ursachen und Folgen des Zweiten Weltkrieges* — which from the early 1970s onwards published multivolume documentation pressing the Nazi co-conspiracy thesis[<sup>17</sup>](#c1-17). In 2001 Alexander Bahar and Wilfried Kugel returned to the contesting side with *Der Reichstagsbrand: Geschichte einer Provokation*, arguing for Nazi involvement on the basis of newly available archival material[<sup>18</sup>](#c1-18).
>
> In 2008 the journalist and historian Sven Felix Kellerhoff published *Der Reichstagsbrand: Die Karriere eines Kriminalfalls*, a synthesis drawing on Berlin Court archive material that had become available in the years immediately preceding. Kellerhoff concluded for the alone-perpetrator thesis[<sup>19</sup>](#c1-19). The Hofer committee and the Bahar–Kugel volume remain the standing counter-positions. Each side has read the available archives and reached a different conclusion; the question is open..
>
> We do not take a side here. Whether the match was struck by one man or by several is a question of historical evidence on which qualified scholars have read the same archives and reached different conclusions; the question is open and may stay open. What is not open, and what every position in the historiographical debate agrees on, is what the regime did with the fire in the first hours and days after it occurred. The Prussian arrest orders were issued before any investigation. The conspiracy framing was announced before the fire was out. The decree was signed within hours. The four thousand arrests were made before any defendant had been charged. The Reichstag elections of 5 March were held while the KPD vote was under preventive detention. Whoever set the fire, the response was prepared. Whoever set the fire, the response did not follow from it; the response was waiting.
>
> That is the point we rest on, and that point does not depend on resolving the historiography. The substitution would have worked whether the act was authentic or staged. The substitution is what makes the response convertible into law.

火灾之后，在随后的几个月中，托辞进入了记录。

普鲁士政治警察——IA 处，盖世太保的前身，由鲁道夫·迪尔斯（Rudolf Diels）领导——控制了调查档案。帝国宣传部，1933 年 3 月 13 日成立，由约瑟夫·戈培尔（Joseph Goebbels）领导，控制了新闻框架。*Reichsgericht*（帝国法院），位于莱比锡的帝国最高法院，控制了法律记录。这些机构各自做了自己的工作。在六个月前魏玛宪法所承认的任何意义上，它们中没有一家是独立的。

1933 年 3 月 29 日，火灾后六周，该政权通过了一项对帝国刑法的追溯性修正案。*Gesetz über Verhängung und Vollzug der Todesstrafe*（《关于死刑宣告与执行的法律》）——刊载于《帝国法律公报》1933 年第 28 卷第 151 页[<sup>13</sup>](#c1-13)——将对公共建筑纵火的刑罚从监禁提升为死刑，并使更高的刑罚适用于该法通过之前实施的行为。这部法律在史学文献中被称为 *Lex van der Lubbe*（范·德·卢贝法）。它的存在，是为了使一个已经被关押的人，能够为他在犯下之夜尚不构成死刑的罪行而被处死。

审判于 1933 年 9 月 21 日在莱比锡开庭，持续至 12 月 23 日[<sup>14</sup>](#c1-14)。整个欧洲都进行了报道。季米特洛夫，用他在狱中自学的德语在被告席上为自己辩护，交叉质询了戈林，并将控方的共谋案变成了公开的难堪。范·德·卢贝在审判的大部分时间里低着头坐着。法院以证据不足为由宣告季米特洛夫、波波夫、塔涅夫和托格勒无罪。仅范·德·卢贝一人被判犯有 *Hochverrat* 和公共建筑纵火罪。他被判处死刑。

四个被告的无罪释放为托辞提供了一项起诉方未曾预料的服务。一个输了五分之四案情的法院，在外国媒体眼中，获得了一种看似权衡过证据的面貌。在外国媒体中，审判开始显得公正。四名共产党人无罪释放了。只有那个带着火柴的人被定了罪。国际舆论转而对该政权有利：这不是一场作秀审判；这是一座法院在履行其职责；放火的人被定了罪，没有证据的人被释放了。该政权不再需要维持阴谋论的细节。到莱比锡判决之时，授权法已生效九个月。授权法所依赖的法令已生效十个月。正当性理由可以淡去了。法律架构不会。

1934 年 1 月 10 日，范·德·卢贝在二十五岁生日前三天，在莱比锡监狱院子里被斩首。他在审判中几乎一言不发。他在火灾之夜大声喊着认罪。

战后对公认叙事的第一次重大挑战来自一个出人意料的来源。弗里茨·托比亚斯（Fritz Tobias），下萨克森州宪法保护办公室的一名官员，于 1962 年出版了《国会纵火案：传奇与现实》（*Der Reichstagsbrand: Legende und Wirklichkeit*）[<sup>15</sup>](#c1-15)。托比亚斯认为，与冷战时期铁幕两侧普遍持有的假设——即纳粹自己放了火——相反，对现存证据的最简单解读正是该政权自己在 1934 年之前已经放弃的那个：范·德·卢贝是单独行动的。这一论点在东德（纳粹纵火是教条性支柱）和西方大部分地区（纳粹纵火这一便利解释已经凝固为常识）都不受欢迎。汉斯·蒙森（Hans Mommsen）在 1964 年《当代史季刊》（*Vierteljahrshefte für Zeitgeschichte*）的一篇文章中——后来在 1972 年扩展为一本英文卷——以不同的证据基础为托比亚斯的解读进行了辩护[<sup>16</sup>](#c1-16)。

其他历史学家不同意。瓦尔特·霍费尔（Walther Hofer）领导了一个国际学者委员会——*Internationaler Komitee zur wissenschaftlichen Erforschung der Ursachen und Folgen des Zweiten Weltkrieges*（第二次世界大战原因与后果国际科学研究委员会）——自 1970 年代初开始出版多卷本文档，支持纳粹共谋论[<sup>17</sup>](#c1-17)。2001 年，亚历山大·巴哈尔（Alexander Bahar）和维尔弗里德·库格尔（Wilfried Kugel）以《国会纵火案：一场挑衅的历史》（*Der Reichstagsbrand: Geschichte einer Provokation*）一书回归争论方，基于新近可得的档案材料论证纳粹参与[<sup>18</sup>](#c1-18)。

2008 年，记者兼历史学家斯文·费利克斯·克勒霍夫（Sven Felix Kellerhoff）出版了《国会纵火案：一个刑事案件的身世》（*Der Reichstagsbrand: Die Karriere eines Kriminalfalls*），综合运用了此前几年刚刚开放的柏林法院档案材料。克勒霍夫的结论支持单独作案论[<sup>19</sup>](#c1-19)。霍费尔委员会和巴哈尔-库格尔卷册仍然是既有的对立立场。每一方都阅读了现有的档案并得出了不同的结论。

我们在此不作选择。火柴是由一个人还是由几个人划燃的，这是一个历史证据问题——合格的学者阅读了相同的档案，得出了不同的结论；这个问题是开放的，并且可能保持开放。不开放的是——且历史编纂学辩论中每一个立场都同意的是——该政权在火灾发生后的最初几个小时和最初几天内，用这场火做了什么。普鲁士的逮捕令是在任何调查之前签发的。阴谋论框架是在火被扑灭之前宣布的。法令在数小时内签署。四千人逮捕是在任何人被起诉之前实行的。3 月 5 日的国会选举是在德共选票被预防性拘留的情况下进行的。无论谁放了火，应对措施是预先准备好的。无论谁放了火，应对措施并非源于火灾；应对措施一直在等待。

这就是我们所立足的那一点，而那一点并不依赖于对史学争论的解决。这个替代机制，无论这场行为是真实的还是设计好的，都会运作。替代正是使应对措施能够转化为法律的东西。

## 法令的十二年

**原文：**

> The Reich fell in May 1945. The decree had been operative for twelve years.
>
> The records survived. Not all of them — the regime had time to burn its files before the Red Army reached the *Reichssicherheitshauptamt* — but most of the architecture of legal administration was captured intact. The *Bundesarchiv* in Koblenz and later Berlin received the Reich Ministry of Justice files (R 3001) and the surviving Reich Security Main Office files (R 58). The Berlin Landesarchiv received the Berlin court records. The *Sächsisches Staatsarchiv* Leipzig held the *Reichsgericht* trial record itself. The United States National Archives, in College Park, held the captured German records returned in the 1950s as Record Group 242. The papers were not in one place; they did not need to be. They were durable.
>
> In 1981 Johannes van der Lubbe, brother of the executed man, petitioned the Berlin *Landgericht* to set aside his brother's death sentence. The court did so, narrowly: the sentence had rested on a retroactive criminal law, and a retroactive criminal law was, in the postwar Federal Republic, incompatible with the elementary principles of criminal justice. The conviction itself was not, at that stage, fully annulled.
>
> Seventeen years later, in 1998, the Bundestag passed the *Gesetz zur Aufhebung nationalsozialistischer Unrechtsurteile in der Strafrechtspflege* — the NS-AufhG, the Law on the Annulment of National Socialist Unjust Judgments — published in *Bundesgesetzblatt* I 1998 at page 2501[<sup>20</sup>](#c1-20). The statute provided for the automatic annulment of National Socialist criminal judgments contrary to elementary principles of justice. The Bundestag amended it in 2002 and 2009 to broaden its scope. It was not specific to van der Lubbe; it was specific to the category of injustice the *Reichsgericht* had perfected. The category included him.

**译文：**

帝国于 1945 年 5 月覆灭。该法令已生效十二年。

记录保存了下来。不是全部——该政权有足够时间在红军抵达帝国保安总局（*Reichssicherheitshauptamt*）之前焚烧文件——但法律行政体制的大部分被完整缴获。位于科布伦茨和后来柏林的联邦档案馆接收了帝国司法部档案（R 3001）和帝国保安总局的现存档案（R 58）。柏林州档案馆（Landesarchiv）接收了柏林法院记录。莱比锡的萨克森州档案馆（*Sächsisches Staatsarchiv*）持有帝国法院自身的审判记录。位于科利奇帕克（College Park）的美国国家档案馆持有了 1950 年代归还的被缴获德国档案，编为第 242 记录组。这些文件不在同一处；它们也不需要是。它们是持久存在的。

1981 年，被处决者的兄弟约翰内斯·范·德·卢贝（Johannes van der Lubbe）向柏林州法院（Landgericht）申请撤销其兄弟的死刑判决。法院以微弱优势照办了：该判决基于一项追溯性刑法，而追溯性刑法在战后的联邦共和国中与刑事司法的基本原则不相容。定罪本身在那个阶段并未被完全废止。

十七年后，1998 年，联邦议院（Bundestag）通过了《关于撤销国家社会主义刑事司法中不公正判决的法律》——即 NS-AufhG（《纳粹不公正判决撤销法》）——刊载于《联邦法律公报》1998 年第一卷第 2501 页[<sup>20</sup>](#c1-20)。该法规定了对违反正义基本原则的国家社会主义刑事判决的自动撤销。联邦议院在 2002 年和 2009 年对其进行了修订以扩大适用范围。该法并非专门针对范·德·卢贝；它是针对帝国法院所完善的这一不公正类别。而这个类别包括了他。

## 祭坛移动，替代没有消失

**原文：**

> We can go back now to the opening scene. The fire above the chamber, the bare-chested young man, the matches, the four thousand arrests by dawn. Read it again, and something the first reading did not show comes forward.
>
> The man was visible because someone needed him to be visible. The decree was almost invisible because nobody needed to notice it for a generation. The story attached itself to the face at speed; the law attached itself to the country slowly. Both attachments were the same operation, performed at different volumes.
>
> The altar moves. The substitution does not.
>
> This is our rule so far, and it is not yet a method. It is a posture. When blame attaches to a single visible face faster than investigation could have produced it — within hours, before anyone has examined the building, before the medical examiner has filed, before the docket is complete — watch what is being signed in the same week. The substitution is older than any specific case, and the photograph on the front page is not the only document with a signature on it.

**译文：**

现在我们可以回到开场的场景。议会厅上方的火焰，赤着上身的年轻男子，火柴，破晓的四千逮捕。再读一遍，第一次阅读没有显现出的东西显现出来了。

那个男人之所以可见，是因为有人需要他可见。那道法令之所以几乎不可见，是因为没有人需要在一代人的时间里注意它。故事以极快的速度附着在那张脸上；法律以缓慢的速度附着在这个国家身上。两种附着是同一个操作，以不同的音量进行。

祭坛移动了。替代没有消失。

到目前为止，这是我们的规则，但它还不是一种方法。它是一种姿态。当指责以快于调查所能产生的速度——在数小时内，在任何人检查大楼之前，在法医提交报告之前，在案卷完备之前——附着在一张单独的可见面孔上时，注意同一周正在签署的是什么。替代比任何具体案例都古老，而头版上的照片不是唯一带有签名的文件。

**翻译笔记：**
- "posture" → 译为"姿态"——不是"方法"（method）而是一种"姿态"（看问题的方式/倾向），这两者的区分是本章的分析性收获
- "the photograph on the front page is not the only document with a signature on it" → "signature"在此有双层含义：字面的签名（法律文件上的签署）和隐喻的"署名"（归咎于可见面孔的叙事）。中文"带有签名的文件"保留了这个双层含义的潜力
- "volumes" → 在此意为"音量/响度"（loudness），非"卷册"——操作相同，只是执行的音量不同

## 莱比锡，2008 年 1 月

**原文：**

> In a determination dated 6 December 2007, publicly announced in a press release of 10 January 2008 (Aktenzeichen 2 AR 187/07), the Federal Prosecutor General of Germany — the *Generalbundesanwalt*, Monika Harms in office at the time — found that van der Lubbe's 1933 conviction was annulled by operation of the *Gesetz zur Aufhebung nationalsozialistischer Unrechtsurteile in der Strafrechtspflege* of 25 August 1998 (the NS-AufhG).
>
> The Bundesanwaltschaft's own formulation was direct: *"Die Bundesanwaltschaft hat am 6. Dezember 2007 festgestellt, dass das Urteil gegen den im 'Reichstagsbrandprozess' verurteilten Marinus van der Lubbe aufgehoben ist"* — the Federal Public Prosecutor's Office determined on 6 December 2007 that the verdict against the man convicted in the Reichstag fire trial was annulled (author's translation).
>
> The annulment rested on the fact that van der Lubbe's death sentence was grounded in two specifically National Socialist injustice provisions: the *Verordnung des Reichspräsidenten zum Schutz von Volk und Staat* of 28 February 1933, and the *Gesetz über Verhängung und Vollzug der Todesstrafe* of 29 March 1933, the so-called *Lex van der Lubbe*[<sup>21</sup>](#c1-21). The conviction had rested on a retroactive criminal statute. That was the legal ground for the annulment. The annulment was automatic; the statute had been doing this work since 1998. Van der Lubbe's was one judgment among many.
>
> This is what a partial interception of the laundering pattern looks like at its best.
>
> The mechanism has a name. It is statutory record durability across regime change. The Federal Republic of Germany, three constitutional generations downstream of the Third Reich, legislated a category of annullable injustice and then applied the category to a specific record that had been signed by a court of the regime it had succeeded. The German state of 2008 reached back into the legal output of the German state of 1933 and unmade one of its judgments. The mechanism took three regimes — the Reich, the Federal Republic of the founding period, the reunified Federal Republic — and a continuous archival custody across all of them. It took a parliament willing to legislate the category. It took prosecutors willing to apply it. The archives survived. The lawyers were available. The legislative will was found.
>
> It is also the kind of interception that can only be described honestly with the cost named in the same paragraph.
>
> Seventy-five years late. Van der Lubbe was executed on 10 January 1934. The annulment came in January 2008. Three generations of his family lived and died between the two dates. He himself was beyond the reach of the document by seventy-four years.
>
> Formal, not evidentiary. The 2008 annulment did not reopen the historical question of who lit the fire, nor did it pronounce on the substitution by which his conviction was used. It annulled the conviction on the narrow ground that it had rested on a retroactive statute incompatible with elementary justice. The deeper laundering — the use of his individual confession to authorise a constitutional emergency against four thousand other people and then against a continent — is not what the 1998 NS-AufhG addresses. The statute fixes verdicts. It cannot fix the regime built on the verdicts.
>
> Not restorative. The four thousand KPD officials, deputies, journalists, and members arrested in Prussia on the night of 27 / 28 February 1933 were not named in the annulment, because the annulment was of van der Lubbe's judgment. Those four thousand were the first arrests under the decree. They were followed by hundreds of thousands more, and then by millions, across the twelve years the decree remained in operative legal force. The decree itself was not annulled because the decree was not a criminal judgment; it was an executive instrument issued under Article 48(2) of the Weimar Constitution. The Federal Republic could and did refuse to recognise its validity from its own founding, but the events the decree authorised had already happened. No annulment retrieves them. The legal architecture of the Reich was dismantled by defeat and occupation in 1945; the human cost it had purchased was not refundable.
>
> The execution stands as an event. The decree's twelve operative years stand as events. Only the criminal judgment of 23 December 1933 is unmade.
>
> This is genuine. It is also late, partial, and unrecoverable in everything it leaves untouched. Both have to be true at once, in the same sentence, or the meaning of the escape is lost.

**译文：**

在 2007 年 12 月 6 日的一项裁定中（于 2008 年 1 月 10 日的新闻稿中公开宣布，案件编号 2 AR 187/07），德国的联邦总检察长——*Generalbundesanwalt*（联邦总检察长），时任为莫妮卡·哈姆斯（Monika Harms）——认定，范·德·卢贝 1933 年的定罪依据 1998 年 8 月 25 日的《关于撤销国家社会主义刑事司法中不公正判决的法律》（NS-AufhG）而自动失效。

联邦检察院（Bundesanwaltschaft）自身的表述是直截了当的：*"Die Bundesanwaltschaft hat am 6. Dezember 2007 festgestellt, dass das Urteil gegen den im 'Reichstagsbrandprozess' verurteilten Marinus van der Lubbe aufgehoben ist"*——联邦检察院于 2007 年 12 月 6 日认定，针对在"国会纵火案审判"中被定罪的马里努斯·范·德·卢贝的判决已被撤销（作者自译）。

撤销基于这样一个事实：范·德·卢贝的死刑判决基于两项特有的国家社会主义不公正法律条款：1933 年 2 月 28 日的《保护人民和国家的帝国总统令》，以及 1933 年 3 月 29 日的《关于死刑宣告与执行的法律》，即所谓的 *Lex van der Lubbe*（范·德·卢贝法）[<sup>21</sup>](#c1-21)。定罪基于一部追溯性刑事法规。这正是撤销的法律依据。撤销是自动的；该法律自 1998 年以来一直在发挥这一作用。范·德·卢贝只是众多判决之一。

这就是洗白模式被部分拦截的最佳写照。

这个机制有一个名字。它叫跨政权更迭的法定记录的持久性。德意志联邦共和国，在第三帝国之后三代宪政之下，立法定义了一类可撤销的不公正判决，然后将这一类别适用于一个由它所继承的政权的法院签署的具体记录。2008 年的德国国家，伸手穿越德意志国家 1933 年时的法律产出，拆除了其中一项判决。这个机制经历了三个政权——帝国、建国时期的联邦共和国、统一后的联邦共和国——以及贯穿所有这些政权的持续档案保管。它需要一个愿意为此类不公正立法的议会。它需要愿意适用该法律的检察官。档案保存了下来。法律人士可资利用。立法意志被找到了。

这也是那种只能在同一段落中如实说出其代价的拦截。

晚了七十五年。范·德·卢贝于 1934 年 1 月 10 日被处决。撤销发生在 2008 年 1 月。他的家族有三代人在这两个日期之间生生死死。他本人早在文件触及他之前七十四年就不在了。

形式上的，而非证据性的。2008 年的撤销并未重开谁点了火的历史问题，也没有对那个用他的定罪来进行的替代做出裁决。它在一个狭窄的基础上撤销了定罪——该定罪基于一项与基本正义不相容的追溯性法规。更深的洗白——利用他个人的认罪来授权一场针对四千人的宪政紧急状态，然后针对整个大陆——并非 1998 年 NS-AufhG 所处理的内容。这部法律修正判决。它无法修正基于这些判决而建立的政权。

非修复性的。1933 年 2 月 27 日至 28 日晚在普鲁士被捕的四千名德共干部、议员、记者和党员，并未在撤销令中被提及，因为该撤销令针对的是范·德·卢贝的判决。那四千人是该法令下的第一批逮捕。随后而来的是数十万人，然后数百万人，在这部法令持续有效法律的十二年间。该法令本身没有被撤销，因为该法令不是一项刑事判决；它是依据魏玛宪法第 48 条第 2 款颁布的一项行政工具。联邦共和国可以从且确实从其自身建国之日起就拒绝承认其效力，但该法令所授权的事件已经发生。没有哪项撤销能收回它们。帝国的法律架构在 1945 年被战败和占领拆除了；它所购买的人力代价是不可退还的。

处决作为一个事件存在着。法令的十二个有效年份作为事件存在着。只有 1933 年 12 月 23 日的刑事判决被解除了。

这是真实的。它也是迟到的、部分的、且在它所触及的一切之外无法挽回的。两者必须在同一句话中同时为真，否则逃脱的意义就丢失了。

**翻译笔记：**
- "Generalbundesanwalt" → 保留德文并括号注「联邦总检察长」
- "Bundesanwaltschaft" → 「联邦检察院」
- [TERM-NOTE] "partial interception" → 「部分拦截」——这是一个重要的技术术语。不是"阻止"（prevention）也不是"干预"（intervention），而是"拦截"（interception）：在洗白过程进行到某个节点时将其截断。但拦截只是部分的，过程仍然在其他层面继续运行

## 结尾段落

**原文：**

> Step back from Berlin in 1933 and from Leipzig in 2008, and notice the position we now stand in.
>
> We recognised the pattern. Probably before we were halfway through. The fire, the foreign man at the scene, the speed of the framing, the law signed in the same week — we have seen this rhythm before, not in a textbook but in a news cycle within the past several years. We will see it again, in a different language and a different country, within several years more. The recognition is not something we installed here. It is something we named. Which example each of us reaches for is itself part of what comes next to discipline.
>
> What follows is about three further things, in order. The first is the recognition itself — how to tell when we are watching it happen rather than only feeling that we are. The second is the kinds of substitution that exist, because there are several and they are not interchangeable. The third is the questions to ask of any particular case to find where blame is actually doing its work. None of those are here yet. We have done one thing so far, which is to make the rhythm audible.
>
> Three signs we can already feel.
>
> The first is speed. When the official story arrives faster than any investigation could have produced it — hours rather than days, days rather than weeks — the story has not been derived from the event. It was waiting. The event activates it.
>
> The second is scale mismatch. When the named cause is small relative to the consequence the cause authorises, the gap between the cause and the consequence is doing work. Something else is filling the gap. The shapes the gap takes are not identical across cases — a constitutional emergency is not a corporate settlement, and what follows will keep the differences visible — but the structural sign is the same one.
>
> The third is absence. When the people whose names appear in the coverage of the event are not the people whose names appear on the documents signed in the days after, the visible names and the operational names have separated. The cameras are pointed at the substitute. The signatures are happening elsewhere. Between those two sets of names runs the responsibility chain.
>
> These are signs, not a method. The method comes next, and after that. Recognition without method is our deliberate stopping point. We can stop here, having learned something true; we will not yet have learned how to use it.
>
> One last thing.
>
> Read it carefully, because it is the only sentence here that asks something of us directly.
>
> If our face is the public face of a decision, and we cannot name in writing who authorised the decision above us, we are at the altar.
>
> This is not a metaphor for some other kind of person. The named employee in the press release. The named clinician on the chart. The named local commander in the after-action report. The named junior signatory on the contract. The ancient *pharmakos* was sometimes paid for the role and was sometimes coerced into it; the modern position is the same role, with neither the ritual's openness nor the *pharmakos*'s stipend. Knowing this does not yet free us from it. But not knowing it makes the substitution easier on the people performing the rite.
>
> The altar moved.
>
> We already know where it is.

**译文：**

从 1933 年的柏林和 2008 年的莱比锡退后一步，注意我们现在所处的位置。

我们认出了那个模式。很可能在读到一半之前就认出了。那场火，现场的那个外国人，框架构建的速度，同一周签署的法律——我们以前见过这个节奏，不是在教科书里，而是在过去几年的新闻周期里。我们还会再次见到它，在不同的语言和不同的国家，也许再过几年就会。认出它并不是我们在这里安装的东西。我们是给它命了名。我们每个人各自联想到的例子，本身就是接下来需要通过训练来把握的内容的一部分。

接下来，按顺序涉及三件事。第一是辨认本身——如何判断我们正在亲眼目睹它发生，而不仅仅是感觉它发生。第二是存在的替代类别，因为有好几种，且它们不可互换。第三是提出针对任何具体案例的问题，以找到指责实际上在哪里做功。所有这些都还没有在这里。到目前为止我们做了一件事，就是让这个节奏可以被听见。

三个我们已经能感觉到的迹象。

第一个是速度。当官方故事以快于任何调查所能产生的速度到来——数小时而非数天，数天而非数周——这个故事并非源自事件。它一直在等待。事件激活了它。

第二个是规模不匹配。当被命名的原因相对于该原因所授权的后果而言很小时，原因与后果之间的差距就在做功。别的东西在填补这个差距。这个差距在不同案例中所呈现的形状并不相同——一场宪政紧急状态不是一笔公司和解，接下来的内容将会让这些差异保持可见——但结构性信号是同一个。

第三个是缺席。当出现在事件报道中的人名与出现在此后几天签署的文件上的人名不是同一批人时，可见的名字与操作性的名字已经分离了。镜头对准的是替代品。签名发生在别处。在这两组名字之间，流淌着责任链。

这些都是迹象，而非一种方法。方法在后面，在后面更远处。没有方法的辨认是我们有意停靠的点。我们可以停在这里，已经学到了一些真实的东西；但我们还没有学到如何使用它。

最后一件事。

仔细读它，因为这是这里唯一一句直接对我们有所要求的话。

如果我们的脸是一项决策对外的那张面孔，而我们无法以书面方式指出授权这一决策的上级是谁，我们就在祭坛上。

这不是一个适用于某种别的人的比喻。新闻稿中那个被点名的主管。病历上那个被点名的医生。行动报告中那个被点名的地方指挥官。合同上那个被点名的初级签字人。古代的 *pharmakos* 有时为这个角色得到报酬，有时被迫担任；现代的处境是同一个角色，却没有仪式的公开性，也没有 *pharmakos* 的那份报酬。

知道这一点并不能使我们从中解脱。但不知道这一点，会让替代仪式对执行者而言更加容易。

祭坛移动了。

我们已经知道它在哪里。

- "scale mismatch" → 「规模不匹配」——第二个迹象。注意"scale"在此指因果关系的规模不对等（小火→大后果）
- [AMBIGUOUS: "the gap between the cause and the consequence is doing work" — 「doing work」在此是一个力学/物理学隐喻（做功能/出力），指这个差距本身在施加影响/发挥作用。中文「做功」保留了此隐喻，但在日常中文中可能不易理解。替代可能：「这个差距在发挥作用」——但丢失了原文的物理学隐喻。暂用「做功」]
- "cameras are pointed at the substitute" → 「镜头对准的是替代品」——"camera"在此为比喻性用法（媒体/公众注意力的焦点）
- "Recognition without method is our deliberate stopping point" → 「没有方法的辨认是我们有意停靠的点」——这句话定义本章的边界。全书的诊断工作在此章节之后才会展开
- [TERM-NOTE] "If our face is the public face of a decision" — 全文的道德挑战核心。"face"在此既是字面的"面孔"也是隐喻的"对外代表"。译为「脸」+「面孔」以保留双重含义
