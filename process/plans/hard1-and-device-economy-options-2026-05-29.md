## HARD-1 — what each "five" does

**"Five roles, one institution"** (ch-7 §"Five roles, one institution," l.35–65). This is the **diagnostic result** — what the Post Office *was*: deployer, audit-trail holder, complainant, prosecutor, disclosure controller. It is the load-bearing export. Ch-7 itself exports it (l.143: "This is what we export to Part III. The five-role conflation…"), and ch-9 references it six times verbatim ("the five-role conflation," l.67/79/133/149/171/195). This "five" is named, portable, and carries forward.

**"Five layers, each lawful alone"** (ch-7 §"Five layers, each lawful alone," l.81–97). This is the **defense mechanism** — *how* the record was made unfalsifiable: liability contract, computer-reliability presumption, private prosecution, victim dispersion, plea-rate architecture. It is *internal to ch-7*. It is never exported; ch-9 never references "five layers." Its analytical payload is the genuinely distinct lesson "record-hardening is layered, each layer lawful, all five must be attacked separately" (l.95–97).

**The collision.** Two numbered "fives" about the same institution, in adjacent sections, with no first-appearance signpost telling the reader they are different *kinds* of five (one is roles-held, one is legal-defenses-converged). They do not overlap in content — no item appears in both lists — but they collide in the reader's working memory because both are "five [X] of the Post Office." Critically, only the *roles* five is exported; the reader's downstream confusion ("which five do later chapters mean?") is real but the answer is unambiguous in the text — it is always roles. Both fives are necessary: the roles-five is the book's instrument; the layers-five is ch-7's distinct teaching ("attack each layer separately"). Neither can be dropped.

## HARD-1 — three options

**Option A — de-number the layers five (RECOMMENDED).** Keep "five roles" as the only numbered "five" in the chapter. Recast "Five layers, each lawful alone" as a non-numeric label — "The layered lawful-step defense" (heading) — and drop the explicit "Five layers" / "the five record-hardening layers" counts to prose ("each layer, lawful in itself, converged…"; "every layer has to be attacked separately"). The five defenses stay, fully described; they simply stop being a *counted* five competing with the roles-five.
- *Costs:* low. One heading + ~3 in-body count phrases reworded (l.81/83/123). Footnote-safe — the layer descriptions carry the `[^N]`; the *count* carries none. No content lost.
- *Changes:* removes the numeric collision at the source; "five" now means roles, uniquely, in ch-7 and everywhere downstream.
- *Ch-9 edit:* none required. Ch-9 already says only "five-role conflation."

**Option B — keep both fives + signpost.** Add a one-sentence first-appearance distinction at the top of "Five layers" ("These are not the five roles above; those named what the institution *was*, these name *how* the record was made unfalsifiable") and harden the ch-9 export to "the five-role conflation (Chapter 7's five roles, not its five layers)" on first ch-9 mention.
- *Costs:* low-medium. Keeps two counted fives alive — the signpost manages the collision rather than removing it; a fast reader can still trip. Adds a defensive sentence (mild D7 self-announcing-signpost surface).
- *Changes:* preserves the layers-five as a memorable count; relies on the reader holding the distinction.
- *Ch-9 edit:* yes — one clause on first mention.

**Option C — merge / restructure so one five carries the chapter.** Fold the five defenses into the five-role walk (e.g., attach each defense to the role it protects) so only one numbered structure exists.
- *Costs:* high. The mapping is not 1:1 — five roles do not align cleanly to five defenses (the plea-rate and victim-dispersion layers are cross-role). Forcing the merge blurs both the diagnostic and the distinct "layered defense" lesson. Risks V5 (capacity-change) regression on the layered-defense teaching.
- *Changes:* most invasive; highest regression risk; not warranted by the defect.
- *Ch-9 edit:* possibly, depending on merge shape.

**Recommendation: Option A.** It removes the collision at its root (only one counted "five" survives), costs the least, is footnote-safe, loses no content, and needs no ch-9 edit. Option B manages rather than removes the friction; Option C over-corrects.
