# Wit-Pass — Find-Candidates Inventory

**Role boundary (Wayne / Narrative Lead).** This is a find-only pass. No chapter edited. Every candidate below is drawn from the documented record already in the chapter; none requires invention. Target is always the laundering mechanism, the launderer, or the procedural absurdity — never a cost-bearer. Where wit was tempting but touched (or sat within reach of) a cost-bearer or needed invention, it is logged LEAVE GRAVE.

**Voice constraint.** Severe, cold, analytical irony only. No comic register, no flippancy, no "light" joke. The book is gravest in ch-5; wit there is held to near-zero.

**Quarantine zones honored.** ch-4: Nisour Square dead incl. nine-year-old Ali Kinani, the MH17 dead. ch-5 (HEAVY): Bhopal dead, deceased Lion Air/Ethiopian crews. ch-6: the children on the ProPublica recording, separated families.
