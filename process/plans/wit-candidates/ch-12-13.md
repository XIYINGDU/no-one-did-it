# Dry-Wit Candidates — Ch 12 & Ch 13

**Phase:** FIND-CANDIDATES ONLY. No rewriting. This is an inventory for a later pass.

**Mandate:** the book is about grave subjects; humor here is high-risk. Only the SAFE form is logged below.

**SAFE form (the only form proposed):** dry, deadpan, analytically COLD irony aimed at the LAUNDERER or at the ABSURDITY of the procedural shell — the euphemism that collapses under its own weight, the proportion mismatch, bureaucratic self-seriousness. Drawn from the DOCUMENTED record, never invented. Three sub-forms used below:
- **witty-comment** — a dry observation on the mechanism.
- **punchline-on-recognition** — a line that lands on the reversal (the click), not on a laugh.
- **dry-dark-line** — a cold line aimed at power, never at the harmed.

Voice stays severe and cold throughout. Wit is a register of contempt for the launderer, not comedy.

**FORBIDDEN (not proposed below):** any wit (light or dark) on or within reach of a COST-BEARER; any invented gag; any trivializing joke. Tempting-but-unsafe spots are logged as "LEAVE GRAVE — no wit here."

**Device-economy note up front:** Ch-12 is the real opportunity chapter — record-destruction-that-failed is structurally ironic *in the record* (the shredded paper vs. the surviving backup tapes; the euphemism "encrypted-messaging traffic does not constitute federal records"). Ch-13 is synthesis/crosswalk with the gravest passage in the book inside it; its wit room is narrow and confined to the diagnostic/objection prose, never near the cost-reckoning. Be sparse in both. A wit-pass that over-fires turns severity into snark and forfeits the book's authority.
