# Developmental Fix Spec — ch08, ch09, ch10

## CROSS-CHAPTER REQUIREMENT (binds all three — read first)

The eight-question diagnostic is ONE fixed instrument. Canonical form lives in ch03 (lines 49-58), this exact order/wording:

1. Who or what was publicly blamed?
2. Who had control?
3. Who benefited?
4. Who knew or should have known?
5. Who could have prevented recurrence?
6. Who controlled the record?
7. Who bore the cost?
8. What would responsibility look like if it followed control instead of visibility?

Every downstream chapter that runs the diagnostic gets ONE **re-grip clause** on first invocation — never a re-install, never a renumbered restatement, never a count that drifts.

**Re-grip pattern (vary surface wording per chapter; must NOT be identical across chapters):** a short appositive that (a) names the instrument as the ch03 one, (b) recites the spine as a dash-run (NOT a numbered list) in canonical order, (c) signals it is a re-grip, not a new thing. Example shape: "The eight questions from chapter 3 — blame, control, benefit, knowledge, preventability, record-control, cost, and what responsibility looks like if it followed control instead of visibility — apply here…"

**Hard prohibitions (these broke trust in rounds 1-2; must not return):**
- Never "five of the eight shift" + five differently-worded bullets with no anchor (the 8/5/4 wobble).
- Never re-paraphrase the spine into a different count.
- Where a chapter runs an ABBREVIATED walk, say so explicitly and name WHICH of the eight it compresses and why — so the omission reads as deliberate, not a different tool.
