**Technique forms (per the preface "mental image" technique):**
- **render-the-record** — replace abstraction with a concrete detail ALREADY in the source record. Vividness pulled from the source, never invented.
- **reader-hypothetical** — a clearly-hypothetical "this could be us" image, confined to reader-applicability beats; claims nothing about a real party. Preface model: "the form we initialed, drafted by a hand we never met, on grounds we were never shown."
