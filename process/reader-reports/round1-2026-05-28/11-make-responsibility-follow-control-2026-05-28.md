## Findings (HARD)

### H1 — Two production markers visible in finished prose (Mechanics)
**Location:** §"Same country, opposite specification," paragraph beginning "At the regulated-financial-firm layer" (line 134); and §"Four designs in operation," panel 3 "UK SMCR in operation" (line 166).

Both passages, in the middle of otherwise-finished prose, hand me a bracketed editorial instruction:

> "...is the operating record [EVIDENCE NEEDED: Stephen selects one A-grade FCA Final Notice meeting our defamation-surface criteria; Nancy clears the selection]."

and

> "...records enforcement actions taken against named senior managers for failures within their allocated function [EVIDENCE NEEDED: Stephen selects one A-grade Final Notice for chapter citation, in consultation with Nancy on defamation surface]."

As a reader I am suddenly looking at the kitchen, not the meal. I learn there are people named "Stephen" and "Nancy," that there is a "defamation surface," that the chapter is unfinished at exactly the point it is making its claim. Worse, the substance the marker stands in for is load-bearing: the SMCR row is one of the four pillars of the whole argument, and at the two places the chapter wants to show me SMCR *actually biting a named person*, it shows me an empty slot instead. So the design that is supposed to be the most extended, most modern form of "name the person" arrives with no named person — the very thing the marker was supposed to supply. This is a HARD stop on the mechanics axis (visible production marker) and it also wounds Trust at the worst possible moment.

### H2 — The central recognition is delivered, not earned, and pre-empts the work the chapter set me up to do (Recognition)
**Location:** §"Same country, opposite specification," the italic line standing alone (line 140):

> "*The diagnostic was always a design rule wearing forensic clothes; chapter 3 named the questions, and now we name the answers the questions force into design.*"

The chapter's own opening promised: "What follows is how to read for that variable, and what to do when reading is not enough" — i.e., I was invited to do the recognizing. But the recognition is then handed to me as a free-standing aphorism in italics, announced rather than achieved. By the rhythm of chapters 1-10, the click is supposed to land in *me* when the forward-run of the eight questions clicks against the backward-run. Here the prose tells me the click happened ("was always a design rule wearing forensic clothes") before I have felt it. It reads as the author doing my recognizing for me. Compounding it, this same realization is then re-announced at least twice more — "The diagnostic was for after the harm. The design rule is for before the design ships" (line 188) and the protocol-section framing (line 144) — so the one insight the chapter exists to deliver is stated *to* me three times and earned by me zero times. That is a delivered-not-earned recognition, which is a HARD finding.

As a Part IV "now we design" chapter the inversion is legitimate, and I understood I was being asked to switch from diagnosing to building. The switch landed; it just arrived a beat earlier than my footing did. SOFT — I adjusted within a paragraph.
