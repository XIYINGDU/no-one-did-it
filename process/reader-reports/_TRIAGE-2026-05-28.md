## Class B — Recognition delivered, not earned — **NEEDS AUTHORIAL CALL**

The book's A2B mechanism. Cold readers consistently felt *told*, not *led*, at the recognition beats. Recurs in 6 chapters.

| Chapter | Where the recognition was delivered |
|---|---|
| ch-2 | Boeing DPA→NPA "asymmetry is the lesson" stated, not discovered |
| ch-3 | eight-question diagnostic handed pre-numbered before the reader needed it |
| ch-4 | multi-channel-interception recognition told 3× (incl. before the matrix) |
| ch-5 | "same case, two layers, two categories" turn told |
| ch-8 | boxed reversal ("The reversal sits here") after the point stated 3× above |
| ch-11 | central recognition delivered as a free-standing italic aphorism, re-announced 2× |

This is a **voice decision**: the book states its recognitions in italic aphorisms / boxed callouts. "Un-telling" them (cut the aphorism; let prose lead the reader to arrive) changes the book's voice substantially. xiaolai's call whether this is the book's deliberate didactic register or a defect to revise. If revise: Bonnie (where the beat sits) + Wayne (how it lands).
