### 5. Trust — CLEAN
Trust was high and it was *earned* by visible restraint. Three specific moves built it:

That is the single most trust-building paragraph in the chapter.

The repeated "(who died in 2024)" / "(who died in 2021)" parentheticals on Cheney and Powell read as careful currency-keeping rather than intrusion.

## The two flagged concerns

**(a) The eight-question walk — sustainable pace, not a proper-noun drowning.**
This was my chief worry and it did not become a HARD. The walk (lines 41–69) is dense — WHIG, OVP, Cheney, Libby, 10 Downing Street, Campbell, Scarlett, Tenet, McLaughlin, NIC, Hutchings, Powell, Wilkerson all appear inside the "Who had control" question alone — but two design choices kept me afloat:
1. The chapter pre-declared (line 45) that only three questions do new work and the rest "run as they did in chapter 3… only as far as the war case bends them." So I knew not every question would carry full weight, and I paced accordingly.
2. Each question paragraph ends on a *shape* sentence, not a name. "Who was publicly blamed" closes on "blame distributed across an institutional category, a senior official's reported informal remark, a defector, and a set of line analysts" — that's a pattern I can hold even after the names evaporate. The names are evidence; the closing sentence is the takeaway. That rhythm is what makes the density survivable.

*SOFT (comprehension):* "Who had control" (line 49) is the single densest paragraph and the one place the inventory briefly outran me — three rooms, ~10 names, two parenthetical death-dates, one nested clause about pushed material. I re-read it once. It held on the second pass, but it is the high-water mark of proper-noun load and the closest the walk comes to the failure mode.

**(b) The reversal lands as my own recognition, not announced.**
Confirmed CLEAN under axis 3 above. The "We are meant to recognise the move before it is named" line (line 37) is the load-bearing trust move: it presumes I've done the work, and I had. The explicit block-quote naming (line 73) comes *after*, as confirmation. Right sequence.
