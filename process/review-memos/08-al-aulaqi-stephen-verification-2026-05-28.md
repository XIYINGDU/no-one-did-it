## What locked at A, what is split, what cannot be used

- **Locked at A (the entire structural claim):** the memo exists; it is dated July 16, 2010; it was
  authored by Barron as Acting head of OLC; it asserted AUMF authority, the § 1119 public-authority
  justification (verbatim-verified), and Mathews due-process balancing; it was withheld and then
  court-released in June 2014; the pre-strike suit was dismissed without reaching the merits; the
  post-strike suit was dismissed with no individual accountability; and al-Aulaqi, a U.S. citizen,
  was killed by a U.S. drone strike on Sept. 30, 2011. **This is a genuine A-grade case and the
  cross-party structural twin of the Bybee/Yoo OLC torture memos already in the manuscript** — the
  single strongest answer to the cold reader's partisan-capture finding, and it requires no
  manufactured symmetry. Loki's overall A grade is confirmed.
- **Split grade (use within the envelope):** the Abdulrahman al-Aulaqi cost-bearer sub-claim. A for
  the fact that a 16-year-old U.S. citizen was killed by a U.S. strike and was not (per the court) a
  target; C for any account of WHY. Per rule 02, no chapter claim may rest only on C-grade evidence —
  so the cost-bearer beat must be confined to the A-grade facts plus an explicit hedge.
- **Cannot be used:** the reported former-official line attributing Abdulrahman's death to being "in
  the wrong place at the wrong time." It is NOT an admission and NOT adjudicated; do not quote it as
  either. (Loki already flagged this; I confirm the prohibition.)

## Gaps Wayne must hedge (rule 05 / rule 07)

1. **"Operational AQAP leader" — attribute to the government, never assert.** The factual case against
   al-Aulaqi was never tested adversarially. Use "the government described al-Aulaqi as..." Do not let
   the descriptor function as a court finding. (This is itself a chapter-on-point demonstration of the
   book's own "the citation is the first step, not the last" diagnostic.)
2. **No bad-faith / motive attribution to Barron or any named official.** The honest framing is the
   opinion-as-authorization STRUCTURE (a discretionary decision converted into a closed legal
   conclusion, controlled by the branch that benefits, resistant to external review) — which holds
   regardless of whether the strike was lawful. The framing is NOT "Obama murdered a citizen" (rule 05).
3. **Carry the steelman at full strength.** Per over-rule 4 and the Loki packet section 6: the strongest
   counter is that this is categorically different from Bybee/Yoo — targeted killing in an AUMF-authorized
   armed conflict is a lawful instrument of war and the § 1119 public-authority justification is a
   recognized doctrine, not an invention; al-Aulaqi was (on the government's evidence) a legitimate
   military target whose citizenship confers no immunity in armed conflict. This steelman is MORE
   plausible than the Bybee/Yoo counter and must be carried more strongly. The answer is that the
   diagnostic does not turn on legality but on control/benefit/knowledge/preventability and on the
   instrument detaching a decision from review.
4. **Abdulrahman cost-bearer beat must not outrun the C-grade record.** One hedged sentence: a
   16-year-old U.S. citizen was killed in a separate strike two weeks later, was not (per the court) a
   target, and no adversarially-tested public accounting of his death exists. Do not build a sympathy
   beat the evidence can't carry (rule 12 V1/V3).
5. **Dual citizenship.** al-Aulaqi was a dual U.S.-Yemeni citizen. The diagnostic requires only
   "U.S. citizen," which is accurate; if a draft foregrounds citizenship, note the dual status for
   precision.
