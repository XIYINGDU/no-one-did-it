## ch-1 — Manusmṛti 8.317 (Sanskrit) — **VERIFIED**

Devanagari:
> अन्नादे भ्रूणहा मार्ष्टि पत्यौ भार्याऽपचारिणी ।
> गुरौ शिष्यश्च याज्यश्च स्तेनो राजनि किल्बिषम् ॥ ३१७ ॥

IAST:
> annāde bhrūṇahā mārṣṭi patyau bhāryā'pacāriṇī | gurau śiṣyaśca yājyaśca steno rājani kilbiṣam || 317 ||

Repository: wisdomlib, *Manusmṛti with Medhātithi's commentary*, verse 8.317 — https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/manusmriti-with-the-commentary-of-medhatithi/d/doc201251.html
Match: `bhrūṇahā`→killer; `bhāryā apacāriṇī`→adulterous wife; `gurau śiṣyaḥ … yājyaḥ`→pupil/sacrificer on teacher; `steno rājani kilbiṣam`→a thief [throws guilt] on the king. Four-way guilt-transfer = Bühler verbatim. Verse number 8.317 confirmed (sits between 8.316/8.318). Devanagari script.

## ch-5 — Saʿdi, *Būstān*, Davie tr. (Persian) — **VERIFIED**

(a) Davie English byte-exact, located in **Chapter I "On Justice, Wisdom, and Government,"** p.35, in the suppliant-complaint run (archive.org, Davie 1882, *Garden of Fragrance / Bostan*: https://archive.org/stream/GardenOfFragranceSadiEnglishBostanSaadi/garden-of-fragrance-sadi-english%20Bostan%20Saadi_djvu.txt). Surrounding lines ("give ear to a suppliant's cry / Saturn roof"; "dog did not tear / peasant who reared him") pin the locus.
(b) Persian bayt (Ganjoor, *Būstān*, باب اول در عدل §6 «گفتار اندر نظر در حق رعیت مظلوم» — https://ganjoor.net/saadi/boostan/bab1/sh6):
> که نالد ز ظالم که در دور توست
> که هر جور کاو می‌کند جور توست
Match confirmed by bracketing bayts: تو کی بشنوی نالهٔ دادخواه / به کیوان برت کلهٔ خوابگاه (کیوان=Saturn) before; نه سگ دامن کاروانی درید / که دهقان نادان که سگ پرورید (dog/peasant) after — both align with Davie's adjacent couplets. RTL Persian (Perso-Arabic script).
