## C. ch-6 — Greek epimythium of "The Wolf and the Lamb" — Greek VERIFIED / English FLAG

Greek: el.wikisource.org, *Λύκος καὶ ἀρήν*. **Byte-exact confirmed**:
> Ὁ λόγος δηλοῖ ὅτι οἷα ἡ πρόθεσίς ἐστιν ἀδικεῖν, παρ' αὐτοῖς οὐδὲ δικαία ἀπολογία ἰσχύει.

(Perry 155 / Chambry tradition.)

**English — FLAG.** No PD English edition translates *this Greek epimythium* verbatim. Townsend (1867, PD) supplies his own moral — "The tyrant will always find a pretext for his tyranny" — which is a different sentence, not a rendering of the Greek. The literal rendering of the Greek line ("not even a just defense prevails…") is Laura Gibbs's Oxford translation (2002, **in copyright**). So a named-PD-source English for this exact Greek line does not exist.
Resolution: pair the byte-confirmed Greek with an in-book literal translation marked **(translation as shown / Stephen's literal rendering)** — do NOT attribute it to a PD edition that does not carry it.
