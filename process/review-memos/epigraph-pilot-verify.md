## Candidate 2 — ch-12 (the record)

**VERIFIED.**

### Exact text as it should appear in the book

> It would be better to be without the Book of History than to give entire credit to it.

### Locked citation

Mencius, 7B.3, trans. James Legge.

### Verification basis
- Byte-exact against Legge, confirmed via two independent renderings of his translation. Legge's wording is **"the Book of History"** — NOT "the Shu" or "the Shû King" in this sentence. (Legge uses "the Shoo King" elsewhere as the work's title; in *this* sentence of 7B.3 he writes "the Book of History." Match the epigraph to this sentence's wording exactly.)
- Locus confirmed: 盡心下 / Jin Xin II, section 3 = canonical Zhu Xi chapter number **7B.3**. (ctext labels it running-paragraph "49"; that is ctext's index, not the citation number. Cite 7B.3.)
- Public domain: Legge (d. 1897); *The Chinese Classics* vol. II (Mencius) published 1861/1895. No permissions required.

### Attribution nuance
The full Legge sentence continues: "...than to give entire credit to it. In the 'Completion of the War,' I select two or three passages only, which I believe." The epigraph stops at the first sentence's period — clean cut, no ellipsis needed. The "Completion of the War" (武成 / Wu Cheng) referent is dropped with the second sentence; the standalone first sentence carries its meaning without it.
