## Reading conventions used

- A figure cell that names a **living person/company** and attributes conduct/knowledge/motive must match the procedural stage of the underlying claim (defamation-wording skill, procedural-stage table). The cell may not reach above that ceiling.
- A figure is a **juxtaposition device by construction** — two columns placed side by side imply a relation. Rule 07 therefore bites figures harder than prose: the layout itself is the implication. I check that every "X said / the record showed Y" pairing is a fair-report juxtaposition, not an asserted-causation or asserted-bad-faith one.
- Convictions later **pardoned/quashed/vacated** must carry the reversal in the same cell (defamation-wording recipe 4 / memory [[sitting-judge-defamation-pattern]]).
- Anchor-case names that are dead, institutional, or already publicly adjudicated carry the lowest surface; I flag only the live/contested individual surfaces.
