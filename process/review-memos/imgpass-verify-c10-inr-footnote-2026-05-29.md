# C10-1 — "the dissent lived in a footnote" — record check

Evidence grade: A (declassified NIE Key Judgments + SSCI Phase I S. Rep. 108-301; both cards on file, undisputed)

## Candidate
Make the metaphor ("hedges became the model's footnotes; the footnotes were not in
the speech") literal by rendering INR's dissent as a **footnote / boxed alternative-view**
inside NIE 2002-16HC.

## What the record documents (Grade A)
- **Nuclear/uranium dissent (incl. aluminum tubes):** placed as an indented block
  **"State/INR Alternative View"** within the declassified Key Judgments, opening
  "INR's view is that Saddam… does not yet have nuclear weapons… [INR] considers the
  available evidence inadequate to support such a judgment." Source: declassified NIE
  Key Judgments (irp.fas.org; FAS congressional-record transcript h072103).
- **Aluminum-tubes dissents (INR and DOE):** documented as **text boxes** in the NIE body,
  plus **Annex A** ("Iraq's Attempts to Acquire Aluminum Tubes"). Per SSCI Phase I
  (S. Rep. 108-301), the INR uranium dissent was accidentally placed in the aluminum-tubes
  section due to the three-week timeline.
- **Niger/uranium dissent:** INR called Iraqi pursuit of African uranium "highly dubious"
  — characterised across sources as a footnote-style/marginal dissent.

## Findings
1. **Placement = Alternative View block + text boxes + Annex A — NOT a "footnote" for the
   nuclear/tubes dissent.** The literal "footnote" rendering is **inaccurate** for the
   aluminum-tubes claim. "Footnote" fits only the Niger/uranium dissent in loose usage.
2. **Which claim:** tubes/nuclear → Alternative View + text box + Annex A; uranium/Niger →
   "highly dubious" marginal dissent.
3. **Not already rendered literally in ch-10.** Lines 73/75 use "footnotes" purely
   metaphorically; line 233 says "INR's aluminum-tubes footnote dissent" — itself imprecise
   (it was a text box / Alternative View, not a footnote).
4. **Defamation risk: none.** Institutional product, 2002, no living individual.

## Verdict
**NO-GO** on the literal "footnote" image as worded — the record says Alternative View
block, text boxes, and Annex A, not a footnote, for the tubes/nuclear dissent. Rendering
"footnote" literally would manufacture a placement the citable record contradicts (rule 02
/ V1). **CONDITIONAL-GO** for an accurate render ("the dissent lived in a boxed Alternative
View while certainty lived in the Key Judgments") — Grade A, both cards on file.
