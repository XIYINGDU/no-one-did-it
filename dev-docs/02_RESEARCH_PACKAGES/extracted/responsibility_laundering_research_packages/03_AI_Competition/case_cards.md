# AI Competition Package

## Industry dominance of frontier models
- **Area:** AI industry structure
- **Timeframe:** 2024-2026 reports
- **Type:** Structural control-benefit separation
- **Evidence grade:** A
- **Blame container:** The model, the benchmark, the paper, the open-source community
- **Laundering mechanism:** Corporate deployment control hides behind scientific language and benchmark culture.
- **Control/benefit vector:** Firms controlling compute, data, deployment, logs, product channels, and safety policy.
- **Record control:** AI Index reports, model releases, deployment records.
- **Outcome/status:** Industry produces most notable frontier models; academia remains important to research legitimacy.
- **Narrative line:** Academia supplies legitimacy; industry controls deployment.
- **Book use:** Opening frame for AI package.
- **Caution:** Use current AI Index numbers with date/version check before publication.
- **Source leads:**
  - https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report
