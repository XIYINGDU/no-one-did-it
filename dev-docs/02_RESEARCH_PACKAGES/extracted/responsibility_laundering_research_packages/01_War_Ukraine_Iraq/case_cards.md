# War Package: Ukraine and Iraq

## Russia invasion justification: denazification, NATO, external-threat alibi
- **Area:** Ukraine
- **Timeframe:** 2022-present
- **Type:** Strategic scapegoating / ideological laundering
- **Evidence grade:** A
- **Blame container:** Nazis, NATO, Ukrainian leadership, Western encirclement
- **Laundering mechanism:** An aggressive or discretionary war is framed as defensive necessity.
- **Control/benefit vector:** Russian state decision-making, invasion planning, public justification, military command.
- **Record control:** Official speeches, UN votes, state media, international legal records.
- **Outcome/status:** Russia’s public rationale rejected by UN General Assembly; war ongoing.
- **Narrative line:** War’s first laundering act is naming the necessary enemy.
- **Book use:** Use to open a war chapter on justification architecture.
- **Caution:** Do not flatten the analysis into slogan versus slogan; separate grievances from legal responsibility for invasion.
- **Source leads:**
  - https://en.kremlin.ru/events/president/news/67843
  - https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3965290
