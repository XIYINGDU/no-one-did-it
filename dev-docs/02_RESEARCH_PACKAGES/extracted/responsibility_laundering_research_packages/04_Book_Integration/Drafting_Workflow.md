# Drafting Workflow from the Packages

1. **Select the primary mechanism** for a chapter: proxy, bad apple, pretext, record control, machine alibi, metric laundering, cost-bearing goat, or reverse scapegoating.
2. **Choose one primary case** with A/B evidence and vivid scenes.
3. **Choose two secondary cases** from different domains to prove recurrence.
4. **Write the official story first.** Let the reader inhabit the public alibi before dismantling it.
5. **Reconstruct the responsibility vector:** control, benefit, knowledge, preventability.
6. **Add the best counterargument.** Explain why the official story seemed plausible.
7. **End with a rule.** Example: “The person closest to the disaster is not always closest to responsibility.”
8. **Verify claims before final prose.** Use the source ledger, then update with latest primary documents.
