# Self-Audit and Cautions

## Terminology fixes
- Use **Ukraine**, not “Ukrine.”
- Iraq is not one single current conventional interstate war. The Iraq package covers the 2003 war/occupation, anti-ISIS campaign, and continuing postwar/proxy-security environment.
- “Scapegoat” must not mean “innocent” in every case. The package distinguishes pure scapegoat, partial scapegoat, system/object alibi, and cost-bearing goat.

## Major risk areas
1. **Active litigation:** Trump II, AI copyright, Perplexity, xAI, and several immigration cases require docket refresh before publication.
2. **War attribution:** use UN, ICC, court, NGO forensic, or official inquiry sources. Avoid relying on battlefield claims alone.
3. **AI competition:** model releases, benchmarks, and lawsuits change quickly. Treat 2025-2026 details as timestamped.
4. **False equivalence:** Ukraine-side violations or U.S./Iraq-side violations can be discussed without equating them with invasion-level responsibility or ISIS atrocities.
5. **Defamation risk:** where a case involves allegations, use “alleged,” “reported,” “sued,” “claimed,” or “court found” precisely.

## Manuscript rule
Before final manuscript use, each case should be re-verified with at least one primary or highly reliable current source.
