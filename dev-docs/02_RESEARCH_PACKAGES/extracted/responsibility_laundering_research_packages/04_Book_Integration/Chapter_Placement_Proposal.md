# Chapter Placement Proposal

## Late-book stress-test chapters

### Chapter: War Is the Perfect Laundry
Use Ukraine/Iraq cases after the reader understands the basic mechanism. War combines secrecy, chain of command, proxies, contractors, national honor, and civilian invisibility.

Primary cases: Abu Ghraib, Blackwater, MH17, WMD intelligence, Bucha, Ukrainian child transfers.

### Chapter: When Power Calls Itself the Goat
Use Trump cases not as partisan spine, but as a modern stress test of reverse scapegoating, legal pretext, record control, and bureaucracy-as-goat.

Primary cases: family separation, census citizenship question, Ukraine aid hold, January 6 pardons/record changes, DOGE/USAID, Abrego Garcia, Presidential Records Act fight.

### Chapter: The Model Did It
Use AI cases as the final altar: the newest instrument through which responsibility becomes technical, distributed, and opaque.

Primary cases: LMArena/Llama 4, copyright/training data, GPT-4o sycophancy, Grok outputs, AI Overviews/Perplexity, data-labeling labor, AI-modified peer review.

## Avoid overbreadth
Do not give every event equal space. Use 6-8 as primary narrative cases, 15-25 as secondary proof points, and the rest as source-led examples for future essays.
