# Book-Grade Case Shortlist

These are the cases most likely to carry scenes, chapters, or major sections.

## Crimea “little green men”
- **Package:** War Package: Ukraine and Iraq / Ukraine
- **Type:** Proxy / deniable-force laundering
- **Grade:** A/B
- **Narrative line:** The proxy is close enough to act and distant enough to deny.
- **Why it matters:** Strong case for plausible-deniability warfare.
- **Caution:** Use precise language: denial, ambiguity, and later acknowledgement; avoid overstating unknown internal orders.

## MH17 shootdown
- **Package:** War Package: Ukraine and Iraq / Ukraine
- **Type:** Proxy attribution / forensic responsibility climb
- **Grade:** A
- **Narrative line:** The proxy’s hand is not the end of responsibility.
- **Why it matters:** Excellent example of records and forensics defeating an alibi.
- **Caution:** Distinguish criminal convictions of individuals from broader state responsibility claims.
