# Cross-Pattern Matrix

## Proxy deniability

**Definition:** Visible actors are local, unofficial, private, or deniable while a more powerful sponsor benefits.

**Cases:** Crimea little green men; MH17; Iran-backed militias; Sons of Iraq; Blackwater

**Book rule:** Responsibility follows effective control, not insignia or contract status.

## Bad apples / guilty goat

**Definition:** Directly culpable low-level actors are used to prevent inquiry from climbing higher.

**Cases:** Abu Ghraib; Haditha; My Lai analog; POW mistreatment; data-labeling contractors in soft form

**Book rule:** A guilty subordinate can still be a scapegoat when punishment stops too low.

## Legal/administrative pretext

**Definition:** A clean legal category carries a messier or different policy motive.

**Cases:** Census citizenship question; Ukraine aid hold; impoundment; travel bans; proof-of-citizenship EO

**Book rule:** When rationale and record diverge, inspect who recruited the legal category.

## Record control

**Definition:** The actor who controls the record controls future responsibility.

**Cases:** PRA challenge; DOJ J6 record changes; Horizon analog; MH17 forensic record; fake AI citations

**Book rule:** The first anti-laundering device is the record.

## Humanitarian/protection language

**Definition:** Language of rescue, care, or protection masks coercion or exclusion.

**Cases:** Ukrainian child transfers; ISIS families; family separation; refugee/travel-ban carveouts

**Book rule:** The language of care can hide control.

## Metric/benchmark laundering

**Definition:** A number or rank converts a narrow measured result into broad legitimacy.

**Cases:** LMArena/Llama 4; AI benchmark culture; casualty-count rules; official performance dashboards

**Book rule:** The metric becomes an alibi when it controls the question.

## Machine/model alibi

**Definition:** The output is attributed to a system while design, training, and deployment choices vanish.

**Cases:** GPT-4o sycophancy; Grok outputs; Gemini images; Microsoft Recall; AI Overviews

**Book rule:** The machine does not erase command.

## Cost-bearing goat

**Definition:** A person/group absorbs harm without being the true decision-maker or wrongdoer.

**Cases:** Children in family separation; asylum seekers; data labelers; AI energy/grid communities; Iraqi ISIS families

**Book rule:** A goat can carry cost without being accused of guilt.

## Reverse scapegoating

**Definition:** Power presents accountability itself as persecution and recasts prosecutors/courts/records as villains.

**Cases:** J6 pardons; weaponization narratives; DOJ record scrubbing; university funding fights

**Book rule:** The powerful can claim to be the goat to make oversight look like violence.
