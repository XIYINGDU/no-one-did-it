## Family separation / Zero Tolerance
- **Area:** Trump I
- **Timeframe:** 2018; litigation/inquiries later
- **Type:** Cost-bearing goat + legal-necessity laundering
- **Evidence grade:** A
- **Blame container:** Democrats, the law, migrant parents, border chaos
- **Laundering mechanism:** A discretionary deterrence/prosecution policy was publicly framed as legal necessity.
