# Narrative Weaving Notes — Trump Administrations

## Best chapter-grade cases
1. Family separation — children as cost-bearing goats; discretionary policy framed as legal necessity.
2. Census citizenship question — pretext with a strong administrative-law record.
3. Ukraine aid hold — “review” as delay/leverage; GAO finding.
4. January 6 narrative reversal — accountability recoded as persecution.
5. DOGE/USAID — ad hoc delegated authority and blurred formal responsibility.
6. Mass probationary firings — workers individualized as poor performers through records.
7. Abrego Garcia / El Salvador — foreign custody as modern wilderness.
8. Presidential Records Act fight — meta-case: record preservation as anti-laundering.

## Special mechanism: reverse scapegoating
Trump-era responsibility laundering often works by making the accountability institution — court, prosecutor, civil servant, university, election office, or record itself — look like the true villain.

## Use warning
Do not make Trump the spine of the whole book. Use these cases late, after the ancient-to-modern mechanism is already established. Otherwise, the book will be dismissed by readers who perceive it as partisan.
