### Overall AI-flavor read: **LOW**

Across 139 candidates flagged by lexical and syntactic scans, only 9 (6.5%) survived adjudication as genuine generic-LLM slop a discerning human editor would cut. Six of thirteen chapters (01, 02, 05, 08, 10, 12) yielded zero confirmed tells. The mechanical scans for the classic lexical fingerprints — "delve / navigate / tapestry / realm / testament / cornerstone / nuanced / multifaceted / myriad / intricate," the transition crutches "Moreover / Furthermore / Notably / Indeed / Ultimately / In essence," the empty intensifiers "crucial / pivotal / profound / remarkable / stark," and the empty closers "speaks volumes / cannot be overstated" — returned effectively nothing book-wide. The prose is heavily voice-engineered; the residual AI flavor is faint and concentrated in nine isolated framing tics.

### Most common confirmed tell-types (ranked)

1. **Empty-evaluative / hollow self-rating framing (4 of 9).** The single most common confirmed tell: prose that *asserts* a passage's importance instead of *demonstrating* it. — ch-07 "the most operationally portable observation we have"; ch-09 "the most operationally instructive moment we encounter"; ch-09 "in a moment that should hold our attention"; ch-13 "our most ambitious artefact." This family also overlaps the sermon/over-signposting register rule 14 forbids.
2. **Hedge / softening filler (2 of 9).** Generic LLM hedge-paraphrase openers that the surrounding evidence already discharges. — ch-03 "It is worth noting where…"; ch-06 "found, in essence, that…".
3. **Copula / padding restatement (1 of 9).** — ch-04 "it is also real that someone built it" (padded second copula).
4. **Filler intensifier as evaluation-substitute (1 of 9).** — ch-09 "genuinely fast by historical comparison".
5. **Figurative cliché idiom (1 of 9).** — ch-11 "sit at the heart of the design-rule argument".

The dominant signal: the book's residual AI flavor is **self-importance framing**, not vocabulary. The lexical fingerprints are absent; what leaked through is the LLM habit of telling the reader a passage matters.

### Preserve-vs-confirm ratio — the discrimination worked

**130 preserved : 9 confirmed (≈ 14.4 : 1).** Roughly 94% of everything that pattern-matched a tell was adjudicated to intentional, load-bearing voice — short reversal hammer-lines at moral turning points (rule 04), recurring diagnostic motifs ("the chain still climbs," "load-bearing," "the signature is the seam," "we put names back"), tricolons and "X-not-Y" pairs carrying genuinely distinct named content, rule-14 co-investigator "we" and imperative instruction, and legally load-bearing procedural-stage vocabulary protected by rules 05/06. That 14:1 ratio is the headline signal that the sweep discriminated rather than mass-flagging: a naive de-slop pass would have "corrected" the engineered voice into blandness. The bias-to-preserve held.

## Highest-value edits

The single most worthwhile edits across the whole book, ranked by how much AI-flavor each removes (the four hollow self-rating tics top the list because that is the book's dominant residual flavor). All 9 confirmed edits are listed; every one is verified footnote/anchor/procedural-stage-safe.

1. **ch-09** — *empty-evaluative superlative*
   Before: `The second move of the evaluation-layer alibi is the most operationally instructive moment we encounter.`
   After: `The second move of the evaluation-layer alibi is the one to learn from.`

2. **ch-09** — *sermon / over-signposting injunction (rule-14 forbidden)*
   Before: `The benchmark becomes, in a moment that should hold our attention, the instrument of its own anti-laundering rule.`
   After: `The benchmark becomes the instrument of its own anti-laundering rule.`

3. **ch-07** — *hollow self-rating superlative*
   Before: `This is the most operationally portable observation we have. Record-hardening is not a single decision by a single actor.`
   After: `This is the observation that exports. Record-hardening is not a single decision by a single actor.`

4. **ch-13** — *flattering self-evaluation (rule-04 non-flattering voice)*
   Before: `The crosswalk is our most ambitious artefact.`
   After: `The crosswalk is our largest artefact.`

5. **ch-06** — *hedge-paraphrase filler*
   Before: `Chief Justice John Roberts found, in essence, that the evidence in the administrative record did not match the explanation the Secretary had offered for the decision.`
   After: `Chief Justice John Roberts found that the evidence in the administrative record did not match the explanation the Secretary had offered for the decision.`

6. **ch-03** — *throat-clearing transition crutch*
   Before: `It is worth noting where the record did not harden.`
   After: `Some records did not harden at all.`

7. **ch-09** — *filler intensifier (evaluation-substitute)*
   Before: `The 72-hour interception window is genuinely fast by historical comparison.`
   After: `The 72-hour interception window is fast by historical comparison.`

8. **ch-04** — *copula-stacking padding*
   Before: `The shell is real, and it is also real that someone built it.`
   After: `The shell is real, and someone built it.`

9. **ch-11** — *figurative cliché idiom*
   Before: `Among its many provisions, two sit at the heart of the design-rule argument.`
   After: `Among its many provisions, two carry the design-rule argument.`

(That is the complete confirmed set; the 10–15 range in the brief is not reached because the manuscript is clean — there were only 9 genuine tells book-wide.)

## Recommendation

**Apply-all-approved.** All 9 edits are low-risk, surgical, and self-justifying: each removes a self-rating or hedge tic without touching a footnote, anchor, italics, procedural-stage word, or any factual / motive / knowledge / causation claim (verified per-chapter at the VERIFY step; no edit was dropped for safety). The edits sharpen the book's own severe declarative register rather than altering meaning, so the regression risk is near zero. The 14:1 preserve ratio confirms the sweep did not over-reach.

**Closer human read warranted on ch-09 (the-model-did-it).** It is the only chapter carrying multiple confirmed tells (3 of the 9), and all three are the same self-importance-framing family ("genuinely," "the most … moment we encounter," "in a moment that should hold our attention"). That clustering suggests the chapter's drafting voice drifted toward telling the reader what to feel — exactly the over-signposting rule 14 forbids — and a human pass may surface softer instances the sweep's PRESERVE bias left in place.

**Voice is the author's to approve.** Every edit is verified footnote/procedural-stage-safe, but each is a register choice; the principal author has final say on whether the book's voice prefers the original or the de-slopped form, particularly on the borderline framing in ch-07 and ch-13.
